# MCKEE — RESEARCH FOR KALEMIE

> *To win the war on cliché, you must know your world in depth and detail.*

---

## Geography

### Lake Tanganyika
- **Depth:** Second deepest lake in the world
- **Length:** Over 600 km
- **Countries:** DRC, Tanzania, Burundi, Zambia
- **Significance:** The lake is a character in the story
- **Research Questions:**
  - What does it smell like?
  - What does it sound like at night?
  - What fish are in it? (Dagaa)
  - What are the myths about it?

### Kalemie
- **Location:** On the western shore of Lake Tanganyika
- **Population:** ~150,000
- **Economy:** Fishing, mining, trade
- **Research Questions:**
  - What are the neighborhoods?
  - What are the streets? (Ave de Tanganyique, Ave de Bobozo, Rue Saint Michel)
  - What is the architecture?
  - What is the climate?
  - What does it smell like?

## History

### Colonial History
- **Leopold II:** Belgian Congo
- **Exploitation:** Rubber, minerals, forced labor
- **Legacy:** Corruption, poverty, violence

### Post-Colonial
- **Mobutu Sese Seko:** Dictatorship, corruption
- **The Congo Wars:** 1996-2003, millions dead
- **Legacy:** Instability, displacement, trauma

### For Kalemie
- The war is in the background
- The violence is normalized
- The corruption is systemic

## Social Structure

### Class Divisions
- **Rue Saint Michel:** The ghost kids, the wealthy
- **Mihogoni:** The poor
- **Schools:** Monseigneur André Kaseba (elite) vs. Ecole Secondaire de Kitumaini (ordinary)

### Race/Ethnicity
- DRC is multi-ethnic
- Tensions exist but may not be the focus

### Gender
- Girls are vulnerable
- Djamela's desire to escape is about survival

## Culture

### Language
- **Swahili:** Everyday language (untranslated in story)
- **French:** Official language, Jean Luc's power language
- **Lingala:** Street language, music language

### Religion
- **St. Augustine Parish:** Catholic church
- Traditional African healing (D'juma's practice)
- The tension between the two

### Music
- **Fally Ipupa:** Congolese rhumba
- The music that plays in Jean Luc's BMW

### Food
- **Ugali:** Staple
- **Dagaa:** Small fish from the lake
- **Chips kuku:** Chips and chicken (luxury)
- **Watermelon:** Birthday "cake"
