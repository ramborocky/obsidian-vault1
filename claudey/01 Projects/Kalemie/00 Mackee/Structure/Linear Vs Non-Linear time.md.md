# MCKEE — LINEAR VS NONLINEAR TIME

## The Distinction

> **LINEAR TIME:** A story with or without flashbacks arranged into a temporal order of events that the audience can follow.

> **NONLINEAR TIME:** A story that either skips helter-skelter through time or blurs temporal continuity so the audience cannot sort out what happens before and after what.

---

## Comparison

| | Linear Time | Nonlinear Time |
|--|-------------|----------------|
| **Chronology** | Followable | Disrupted |
| **Flashbacks** | Clear markers | Confusing |
| **Flash-forwards** | Clear markers | Confusing |
| **Structure** | Archplot/Miniplot | Antiplot |
| **Examples** | Casablanca, The Godfather | Bad Timing, Last Year at Marienbad |

---

## Linear Time

**Characteristics:**
- Starts at a point in time
- Moves through more or less continuous time
- Ends at a later date
- Flashbacks are handled so the audience can place events

**Examples:**
- **The Godfather:** Clear chronology
- **Casablanca:** Flashbacks are clearly marked
- **The Verdict:** Straightforward timeline

---

## Nonlinear Time

**Characteristics:**
- Scrambles or fragments time
- Makes it difficult to sort what happened
- Suggests "character as destiny"
- Creates a sense of absurdity or fate

**Examples:**
- **Bad Timing:** Disconnected temporal fragments
- **Last Year at Marienbad:** Time is confused and confusing
- **Pulp Fiction:** Interlocking but nonlinear narratives

---

## For Kalemie

**Recommended:** Linear Time with Flashbacks

**Why:**
- Daudi's story is straightforward
- Flashbacks can reveal the past
- The present drives the story

**Possible Structure:**
1. Present: Daudi at the lake, finding the motherboard
2. Flashback: Daudi and Djamela's history
3. Present: Daudi hears about Djamela's death
4. Flashback: Jean Luc appears
5. Present: The police station
6. Flashback: The kiss
7. Present: Daudi's final realization

---

## Using Flashbacks Effectively

### Rules for Flashbacks
1. **Dramatize them:** Make them mini-stories, not info dumps
2. **Use them only when needed:** Create a need and desire to know
3. **Save the best for last:** Reveal critical information at the right moment

### Example: Casablanca
- The Paris Flashback comes only after we're curious about Rick and Ilsa's past
