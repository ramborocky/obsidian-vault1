# MCKEE — MIXING GENRES

## The Principle

> Genres are frequently combined to resonate with meaning, to enrich character, and to create varieties of mood and emotion.

---

## Why Mix Genres?

1. **Resonate with meaning** — Two genres together create new meanings
2. **Enrich character** — Different genre demands reveal different aspects
3. **Create variety** — Mood and emotion change with genre shifts

---

## Examples of Mixed Genres

### The Fisher King
- **Threads:** Redemption Plot, Psycho-Drama, Love Story, Social Drama, Comedy
- **Effect:** A rich, complex film that transcends any single genre

### The Musical Horror Film
- **Genres:** Musical + Horror
- **Effect:** Delicious invention, unexpected tone

### Love Story + Crime Story
- **Genres:** Love Story subplot inside Crime Story
- **Effect:** Complicates the protagonist's life, adds emotional stakes

---

## How to Mix Genres

### 1. Use Subplots
> A Love Story subplot finds its way inside almost any Crime Story.

### 2. Cross-Breed Conventions
> Given over two dozen principal genres, possibilities for inventive cross-breeding are endless.

### 3. Shift Tone
> A scene can shift genre tone to create surprise and depth.

---

## For Kalemie

### Genre Mix

**Primary:** Literary Fiction / Art Film

**Secondary Genres:**
1. **Education Plot** — Daudi changes (or doesn't)
2. **Social Drama** — Police corruption, class structure
3. **Disillusionment Plot** — Daudi learns the system is corrupt
4. **Love Story** — Daudi and Djamela's relationship
5. **Mystery** — What happened to Djamela?

**Result:** A rich, complex story that can't be reduced to one genre

---

## Genre Mixing in Practice

### Scene 1: The Lake (Education Plot)
- Daudi learns something about himself

### Scene 2: The Dala Dala (Social Drama)
- Class structure is revealed

### Scene 3: Jean Luc Appears (Crime Story)
- A predator enters the scene

### Scene 4: The Kiss (Love Story)
- Daudi and Djamela connect

### Scene 5: The Police Station (Social Drama + Crime Story)
- The system fails

### Scene 6: The Ending (Disillusionment Plot)
- Daudi realizes the truth
