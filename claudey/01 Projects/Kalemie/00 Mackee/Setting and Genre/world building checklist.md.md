# MCKEE — WORLD BUILDING CHECKLIST

> *Before you write a single scene, you must know your world in depth and detail.*

---

## The Four Dimensions

### 1. PERIOD
- [ ] What year/era is it?
- [ ] What historical events are happening?
- [ ] How does this period affect the characters?
- [ ] What is the social/political climate?

### 2. DURATION
- [ ] How long does the story span?
- [ ] Is it real-time? Days? Months? Years?
- [ ] What happens in the gaps between scenes?

### 3. LOCATION
- [ ] What is the specific geography?
- [ ] What are the key locations?
- [ ] What are the details of each location?
- [ ] What do they smell like? Sound like? Look like?

### 4. LEVEL OF CONFLICT
- [ ] What is the primary conflict level?
- [ ] What are the secondary conflict levels?
- [ ] How do they interact?

---

## The Knowable World Questions

### Work
- [ ] How do my characters make a living?
- [ ] What is their relationship to work?
- [ ] What do they hate/love about their jobs?

### Politics
- [ ] What is the structure of power?
- [ ] Who has power? Who doesn't?
- [ ] How does power operate?

### Rituals
- [ ] How do they take meals?
- [ ] What are their daily rituals?
- [ ] What are their special occasions?

### Values
- [ ] What do they consider good? Evil?
- [ ] What do they believe is worth living for?
- [ ] What would they give their lives for?

### Biographies
- [ ] What are the characters' backstories?
- [ ] What significant events shaped them?
- [ ] What secrets do they carry?

## For Kalemie

### Period
- [ ] Contemporary DRC
- [ ] Post-colonial, post-war
- [ ] The legacy of Leopold, Mobutu, and the wars

### Duration
- [ ] Approximately one month
- [ ] From Djamela's disappearance to Daudi's realization

### Location
- [ ] Kalemie, Lake Tanganyika
- [ ] Mihogoni (Daudi's neighborhood)
- [ ] Rue Saint Michel (the ghost kids' neighborhood)
- [ ] Ave de Bobozo (police station)
- [ ] Lakeside hotel
- [ ] The road between the lake and the mines
- [ ] Marché le Lubuye
- [ ] Ecole Secondaire de Kitumaini
- [ ] Monseigneur André Kaseba Catholic School
- [ ] St. Augustine Parish

### Level of Conflict
- [ ] Primary: Inner (Daudi's passivity)
- [ ] Secondary: Personal (Djamela leaving, D'juma)
- [ ] Tertiary: Extra-Personal (Police system, Jean Luc, class)

---

## The Rules of Kalemie

- [ ] Poverty is inescapable for the poor
- [ ] The system is corrupt (police, government)
- [ ] Those with money have power
- [ ] Class divisions are rigid
- [ ] The lake is always present
- [ ] Daudi watches, but doesn't act
