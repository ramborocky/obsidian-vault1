# MCKEE — FILM GENRES

> Through tens of thousands of years of tales, countless generations of storytellers have spun story into an astonishing diversity of patterns. The audience is already a genre expert.

---

## The 25 Genres

### Basic Genres

#### 1. LOVE STORY
- Boy meets girl, loses girl, gets girl
- **Subgenre:** Buddy Salvation (friendship replaces romance)
- **Examples:** Mean Streets, Passion Fish, Romy and Michele's High School Reunion

#### 2. HORROR FILM
- **Uncanny:** Rational explanation (aliens, maniacs)
- **Supernatural:** Spirit realm
- **Super-Uncanny:** Ambiguous between rational/spiritual
- **Examples:** The Tenant, Hour of the Wolf, The Shining

#### 3. MODERN EPIC
- Individual vs. the state
- **Examples:** Spartacus, Mr. Smith Goes to Washington, Viva Zapata!, 1984, The People vs. Larry Flynt

#### 4. WESTERN
- Good vs. evil in the Old West
- **Study:** Will Wright's Six Guns and Society

#### 5. WAR GENRE
- Specifically about combat
- **Subgenres:** Pro-war, Antiwar
- **Examples:** Apocalypse Now, Platoon, The Deer Hunter

#### 6. MATURATION PLOT
- Coming-of-age
- **Examples:** Stand By Me, Saturday Night Fever, Risky Business, Big, Bambi, Muriel's Wedding

#### 7. REDEMPTION PLOT
- Moral change from bad to good
- **Examples:** The Hustler, Lord Jim, Drugstore Cowboy, Schindler's List, La Promesse

#### 8. PUNITIVE PLOT
- Good guy turns bad and is punished
- **Examples:** Greed, The Treasure of the Sierra Madre, Mephisto, Wall Street, Falling Down

#### 9. TESTING PLOT
- Willpower vs. temptation to surrender
- **Examples:** The Old Man and the Sea, Cool Hand Luke, Fitzcarraldo, Forrest Gump

#### 10. EDUCATION PLOT
- Deep change in view of life (naive → wise)
- **Examples:** Harold and Maude, Tender Mercies, Winter Light, Il Postino, Grosse Pointe Blank, My Best Friend's Wedding, Shall We Dance

#### 11. DISILLUSIONMENT PLOT
- Deep change from positive to negative
- **Examples:** Mrs. Parker and the Vicious Circle, L'Eclisse, Le Feu Follet, The Great Gatsby, Macbeth

---

### Mega-Genres

#### 12. COMEDY
- **Subgenres:** Parody, Satire, Sitcom, Romantic, Screwball, Farce, Black Comedy
- **Convention:** Nobody gets hurt (permanently)

#### 13. CRIME
- **Subgenres:** Murder Mystery, Caper, Detective, Gangster, Thriller, Courtroom, Newspaper, Espionage, Prison, Film Noir
- **Convention:** Crime, detective, clues, suspects, apprehension

#### 14. SOCIAL DRAMA
- **Subgenres:** Domestic Drama, Woman's Film, Political Drama, Eco-Drama, Medical Drama, Psycho-Drama

#### 15. ACTION/ADVENTURE
- **Subgenres:** High Adventure, Disaster/Survival
- **Examples:** Raiders of the Lost Ark, Die Hard, The Poseidon Adventure, Alive

---

### Supra-Genres

#### 16. HISTORICAL DRAMA
> The best use of history is anachronism—to use the past as a clear glass through which you show us the present.

#### 17. BIOGRAPHY
> The biographer must interpret facts as if they were fiction, find the meaning of the subject's life, and cast him as the protagonist of his life's genre.

#### 18. DOCU-DRAMA
> Centers on recent events.

#### 19. MOCKUMENTARY
> Pretends to be rooted in actuality but is utter fiction. Subverts fact-based filmmaking to satirize.

#### 20. MUSICAL
> Characters sing and dance their stories.

#### 21. SCIENCE FICTION
> Hypothetical futures, technological dystopias.

#### 22. SPORTS GENRE
> Sport as crucible for character change.

#### 23. FANTASY
> Plays with time, space, and the physical.

#### 24. ANIMATION
> Anything can become something else.

#### 25. ART FILM
> **Subgenres:** Minimalism, Antistructure
> **Conventions:** Celebration of the cerebral, unconventionality
