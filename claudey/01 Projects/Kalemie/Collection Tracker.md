### Status definitions (staggered maturity — never two stories "Drafting" at once)
- **Idea** — a premise, an image, a character worth stealing from the trunk
- **Outline** — step-outline / scene cards only, no prose (see [[A Writer's Method]])
- **Drafting** — active full-prose work (only one story should hold this status at a time)
- **Resting** — untouched for 2+ weeks minimum, cooling off before revision
- **Revision** — active editing pass
- **Submission-ready** — polished, formatted, ready to send
- **Submitted** — out to a market/competition; track below
- **Trunk** — dead or paused; cannibalize freely for parts, images, characters
