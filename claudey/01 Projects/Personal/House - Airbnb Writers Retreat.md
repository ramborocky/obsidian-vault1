## The Idea
Turn the property into one (or a hybrid) of:
1. **Standard Airbnb** — short-term tourist/business rental.
2. **Writers' retreat** — quiet, themed space for writers (fits naturally with the Kalemie project — could even become a personal case study: "I finished a story here").
3. **Tourist spot** — leans on location/setting more than the writer angle.

These aren't mutually exclusive — a lot of successful small retreats market as "Airbnb" on booking platforms but brand themselves as a writers'/creative retreat to stand out from generic listings.
