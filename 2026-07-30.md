## The ADHD layer — five rules, not lectures

| Rule | What it means tomorrow |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone in another room, tasbih in the pocket** | Same swap as yesterday's plan. The reach reflex needs a different object to find, not willpower |
| **Scheduled phone, not banned phone** | Every break, 3 minutes, on a timer. A total ban usually breaks by 2pm. A boxed one holds |
| **Hard tasks first, novel task at the slump** | 1–4 go in the morning while executive function is freshest. Task 6 (the audit) sits after Dhuhr on purpose — it's the most game-like of the seven, which is exactly what a post-lunch dip needs |
| **External timer, not your own sense of time** | Set every sprint on a visible timer before you start. Time blindness isn't a character flaw, it's just a reason not to trust your own clock |
| **Paper first, laptop second** | Write the seven on paper before anything opens on a screen — same rule as yesterday, it's the one worth keeping permanently |
