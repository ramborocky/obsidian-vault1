# 🖋️ ESSAYS — the money stories

**One a month. 800–1,500 words. Narrative non-fiction: a true scene from Dar first, then the finance underneath it.**
They're the bridge between [[MAF]] and the fiction. You practise fiction technique on true material, and each one is posted publicly, so each one is an exposure in [[The Game]].

> [!quote] Same constitution as the fiction
> We write scenes, not explanations. Objects carry emotion. Dialogue reveals class.
> **The finance paragraph comes after the scene, and it's shorter than the scene.**

## The monthly cycle

| Sunday | Job |
|---|---|
| 2nd Sunday | **Draft.** Pick one entry from [[Money and Motive]], find the real scene, write it |
| 4th Sunday | **Revise and post.** LinkedIn or a blog. Then set `exposure: 1` in that day's note |

> [!warning] Kept apart from the fiction
> Nothing in this folder crosses into `Kalemie/`. True people stay true here: change names, and ask before writing about anyone who could recognise themselves.
