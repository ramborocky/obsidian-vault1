# 🧠 MONEY AND MOTIVE
### Every bias you study is a reason a character does something

Behavioural finance is a catalogue of why people act against their own interest, and that's what a character is. Each time a bias comes up in Behavioral Investments or in your reading, give it one row.
- **Who in Mihogoni would act it out?** That's fiction material, for the cycle.
- **Where have you seen it in Dar?** That's an essay seed, for [[Essays]].

The bias column is a definition. **The other two columns are yours to write.** Leave them empty until you have one.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Loss aversion** | A loss hurts about twice as much as the same gain pleases | | |
