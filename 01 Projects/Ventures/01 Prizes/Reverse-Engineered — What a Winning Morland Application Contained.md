# 🔬 REVERSE-ENGINEERED
### The Foundation never publishes a proposal. **Three of them can be reconstructed anyway** — and the reconstruction says the proposal is not what anyone thinks it is.

→ [[Morland 2022 — The Five, Read Closely]] · [[Morland — What They Actually Fund]] · [[Morland 2025 — Post-Mortem]] · [[The Village, The House, The Road — Proposal Sheet]]

> [!warning] What this page is, and what it deliberately is not
> **Every fact in the tables is sourced.** Every line marked **inferred** is my reasoning from those facts, and is labelled.
>
> **There are no reconstructed documents here.** No invented bios, no invented extracts, no invented proposals in a real person's voice. Those would be fabrications about identifiable living writers, they would be indistinguishable from the real thing once copied out of this vault, and they would be **worth less than what is here** — because a specification transfers to your book and a forgery does not. **The worked example at the foot of this page is on *The Village, The House, The Road*, which is the only application any of this has to serve.**

---

## THE METHOD — three fixed points, and what sits between them

You cannot see a Morland application. **You can see three things around it**, and an application is bounded by all three:

| Fixed point | What it constrains | Source quality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A · What they had published *before* applying** | **The extract.** Entry requires a previously published, for-sale piece of 2,000–5,000 words. If a writer had only three magazine stories in print, the extract was one of those three | Hard — publication records are public |
| **B · The Foundation's own announcement of the project** | **The description.** The blurb is what the Foundation wrote down *after* reading the 400–1,000 words. **It is their compression of your document** | Hard — published by MMF |
| **C · What the book actually became** | The gap between promise and delivery — **and this turned out to be the finding** | Hard — books exist |

# 🔴 THE FINDING

> # **The funded project is not the book that arrives.**
> **Three of three, wherever a book exists.** And the two where it does not exist yet are **two and four years past a twelve-month scholarship.**

| Writer | Promised | Delivered | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| Emezi | *The Death of Vivek Oji* | *Freshwater* first | **5 yrs** |
| Muchemwa | A Chimurenga novel | A contemporary story collection | **2 yrs** |
| Kimutai | Colonial-era twins | A contemporary queer novel | **3 yrs** |

## What that means about what is being bought

**If the Foundation were buying a manuscript, this record would be a scandal.** It is not, so they are not.

> ## **They are buying the writer. The proposal is evidence, not a contract.**

**This is why [[Morland — What They Actually Fund|the six patterns]] are what they are.** Every one — dated wound, one-clause hook, named form, geographic specificity, only-you — is a test of **whether this person can conceive and hold a book-sized idea.** Not one is about feasibility, budget, research access or a delivery schedule. **A rubric built to buy books would have those. This one doesn't.**

### Three consequences, in order of how much they change what you do

**1 · The 2025 post-mortem was right, and now it has a mechanism.**
> *"This is the format of a funding request, and they are not primarily funders — **they are readers.** Those read like books. Yours reads like a form."*

**Not a style note. A structural fact about what the document is for.**

**2 · The extract carries more weight than the proposal, and by some distance.** It is the only place in the application where the writer is *demonstrated* rather than described. **All three of these winners had no book and won on a magazine story.** → [[Morland 2026 — Due Diligence and Rerun|*the extract is the audition*]] was already the right instinct.

**3 · ⚠️ Down-weight the "deliverable in 12 months" axis as a *judging* criterion.** [[Morland 2026 — Due Diligence and Rerun|The rerun table]] scores it out of five and it cost Zanzibar two points. **Nobody delivers in twelve months — not one of five.** It still matters enormously for whether *you* finish the book. **It appears not to be what they are scoring.**

## 📌 THE BLURB FINDING — the most directly usable thing on this page

**The Foundation writes a one-sentence summary of every funded project.** That sentence is written **after** a judge has read the 400–1,000 word description. **It is their compression of your document, published.**

| Writer | The Foundation's own sentence | Words |
| --- | --- | :-: |
| **Kejera** | *A woman haunted by the living ghost of the Gambian dictator throughout her life* | **13** |
| **Kimutai** | *Twins separated in the 1800s in the Kenyan highlands, whose paths cross a British explorer* | **16** |
| **Komba** | *Mkomanile, a formidable Ngoni general in the Majimaji rebellion against the illegitimate colonial administration* | **15** |
| **Muchemwa** | *A teenage girl coming of age against the 2nd Chimurenga, among ordinary villagers* | **13** |
| **Oduah** | *A journalist traversing Nigeria to witness the uprising of Boko Haram* | **11** |

> ### **Eleven to sixteen words. Every time.**
>
> **The description's real job is to survive being compressed into that sentence by somebody else.** You do not get to write it — **they write it, and what they write is what they took away.** If your 1,000 words compress to something dull, that is the sentence that goes on the page, and it was true before it was printed.

# 🧩 THE BLUEPRINT

## Document 1 · THE EXTRACT — 2,000–5,000 words, published, for sale

| Spec | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **A magazine-grade short story is sufficient.** No book required | All three winners had no book |
| **It is the only proof you can write.** Weight it above the proposal | Inferred — nothing else in the application is prose |
| **Same country and register as the proposed book; different door** | Never spend the book's material in the audition |
| **The first 400 words decide it** | A reader working through ~800 submissions |

## Document 2 · THE DESCRIPTION — 400–1,000 words

| Spec | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **1 · It must compress to 11–16 words with its strangeness intact** | The blurb table above, five for five |
| **2 · Open on an image, not a thesis** | Every blurb is a person or a scene. **Not one is an argument** |
| **3 · Name a person and a dated event** | Mkomanile / Majimaji · Jammeh / the TRRC · 2nd Chimurenga · Boko Haram |
| **4 · Name the form** | Pattern 4 is the weakest of the six ([[Morland 2022 — The Five, Read Closely\|~2/5 in 2022]]) — do it, but don't chase it |
| **5 · Do not write a project plan** | Nothing in five funded blurbs is about method, access or schedule |
| **6 · Read like a book, not a request** | The post-mortem's diagnosis, now with a mechanism |

## Document 3 · THE BIO — 200–300 words

| Spec | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **Establish trajectory, not achievement** | Three winners, no books, all with magazine credits climbing |
| **A shortlist is a credential** | Muchemwa ×2 SSDA · Kejera Commonwealth 2020 · Kimutai Kraak — **and Kimutai lost Morland in 2017 before winning in 2023** |
| **Every sentence must carry a fact** | *"My connection to this material is both personal and intellectual"* — the post-mortem: **that sentence contains no information** |
| **Locate yourself physically** | Pattern 6 answers *why you* without being asked |

# ✅ WORKED — on *The Village, The House, The Road*

**The compression test, run now, because they will run it either way.**

> **A Prime Minister spent fourteen months making the rich account for every shilling, then died on a road nobody believes was an accident.** — 23 words

**Too long by the table's standard, and the second half is the strong half.** Two candidates at blurb length:

> **A Tanzanian Prime Minister who made the rich explain their money, and died on a road.** — 16
>
> **The fourteen months Tanzania searched its own houses, and the death that ended them.** — 14

**Test them out loud on somebody who does not read.** The one that makes them look up is the sentence the Foundation will write about you.

### What each document has to do

| | The job |
| --- | --- |
| **Extract** | Prove you can hold a Tanzanian interior in a specific year and let objects carry the politics. **Not Sokoine. Not villagisation.** → *Voices of Azania* already clears the gate |
| **Description** | **Open on the televisions in the gutters of Oysterbay.** Sokoine as the engine, Ujamaa as what the investigation keeps running into. **No method section, no schedule, no verdict on Nyerere** |
| **Bio** | An accountant in a lake town, forty minutes from living witnesses, who has published X and been shortlisted for Y. **Three facts, no adjectives** |

> [!important] And the reframe this whole page earns you
> **You are not promising to deliver this book in twelve months, because nobody has ever done that and the Foundation knows it.** You are proving you are a writer worth twelve months. **That should make the proposal easier to write, not harder** — it is a piece of writing, not a tender.
