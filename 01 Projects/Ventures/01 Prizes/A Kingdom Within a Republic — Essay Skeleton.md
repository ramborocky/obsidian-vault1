# A Kingdom Within a Republic

> [!abstract] What this is
> Skeleton for an essay citing **Bwana Heri bin Juma** of Saadani and **Nassib Bunda** of the Gosha. **Structure, evidence and traps only — every sentence is the author's.**
> Supersedes the framing in [[A Republic in the Forest — Essay Skeleton]], which stays useful for the Gosha material.

## The thesis

Modern Tanzania is a republic laid over ground where other sovereignties actually stood. Two of them are within one lifetime and both touch the Wazigua:

- **Saadani**, on the coast, ruled by **Bwana Heri bin Juma** — a real authority whose power rested on the caravan trade in people
- **Goshaland**, on the Juba, built by **Nassib Bunda** — a confederation of the people that trade produced, governing itself a thousand miles away

**The essay's spine: one Zigua-connected polity was built on selling people; the other was built by the people who were sold. Both were sovereign in fact. Both were ended by Europeans within twenty years of each other. Neither is in the national story.**

> [!success] Why this beats the heroic version
> A piece about noble ancestors resisting colonialism is a piece a hundred people could write and an editor has read. **A piece that puts a slave-trading Zigua ruler and a slave-descended Zigua polity in the same frame, and does not flinch, is one only you can write.** The discomfort is the argument's proof of honesty.

## The two men

### Bwana Heri bin Juma — Saadani

| Fact | Confidence |
| --- | --- |
| Ruled Saadani from the early 1870s | Reported consistently |
| Power built through **the slave trade**; influence over the Zigua hinterland and the Nyamwezi | Reported — **central to the essay, so source it properly** |
| Described as of Swahili origin *and* as chief of the Zigua | **Handle with care.** State the position, not a tidy ethnic label |
| Led coastal resistance from Saadani in the **Abushiri revolt, 1888–1890**, alongside Abushiri ibn Salim al-Harthi at Pangani; the Germans were expelled from much of the coast | Well attested |
| Saadani bombarded and taken **June 1889**; he fled inland and built a series of forts, destroyed one by one by **Wissmann** | Well attested |
| **Surrendered at Saadani, April 1890** | Well attested |
| **That he was then hanged** | ⚠️ **DO NOT PRINT WITHOUT CHECKING.** One source says hanged; this may be confusion with **Abushiri, who was hanged in December 1889**. Other accounts have Bwana Heri pardoned and dying later in the 1890s. **Settle this in a book, not online** |

### Nassib Bunda — Goshaland

| Fact | Confidence |
| --- | --- |
| Of **Yao** origin, himself formerly enslaved | Reported |
| Built a confederation from the runaway settlements of the lower Juba | Reported |
| By **1892**, sole authority over **46 villages** on both banks, Yoontoy to Mfudu | Reported — verify in the Virginia Tech thesis |
| In the 1880s secured **the Sultan of Zanzibar's recognition** and arms, in exchange for the Sultan's rights on the river | Reported — the essay's centre, so verify hardest |
| **Hanged by the Italians, 1907** | Reported |

> [!danger] Two errors that would end the piece
> **1 · Bunda was Yao, not Zigua.** Your claim is to the population of Goshaland, not to the man.
> **2 · The Juba is not the Shabelle.** The Juba holds those brought as slaves; the Shabelle holds older Bantu-ancestry communities with a different history. Your proposal currently merges them.

## The beats

### 1 · Two men, one sentence each — 1 paragraph
Open with both, flat, no framing. A ruler at Saadani whose wealth came from selling people inland. A man who had been sold, governing forty-six villages on a river a thousand miles north. Let the reader feel the symmetry before you name it.

### 2 · Name what they were — 2 sentences
Not chiefs, not rebels. **Sovereigns in fact**: they taxed, armed, negotiated and were negotiated with. Zanzibar recognised one of them by treaty. Germany had to send Wissmann against the other.

### 3 · Saadani — 2 paragraphs
Bwana Heri from the early 1870s. The source of the money, stated without euphemism. Then 1888: the coast rises, the Germans are pushed off it, and for a moment an African authority holds the littoral. Then the bombardment, the forts inland, Wissmann taking them one at a time, the surrender in April 1890.

### 4 · The Juba — 2 paragraphs
Bunda, formerly property, assembling a polity out of Yao, Zigua, Nyasa, Makua, Kikuyu and Nyamwezi — most of whom had already lost their languages on the way. Forty-six villages. **The treaty with Zanzibar: a man who had been sold, negotiating with the throne at the centre of the trade that sold him, and getting guns out of it.** This is the paragraph the essay exists for.

### 5 · The symmetry, stated once — 1 short paragraph
Both Zigua-connected. One made by the trade, one made of its survivors. Both destroyed by Europeans, twenty years apart, by two different empires. **Do not moralise here.** The facts are already unbearable; a judgment added on top makes them smaller.

### 6 · What the republic inherited — 2 paragraphs
Tanzania abolished chieftaincy in 1963 *(verify)*. But what was abolished was the colonial-appointed chief, not these. The republic took the ground and left the sovereignty out of the story. Uganda restored its kingdoms in 1993 *(verify)*; two neighbours, opposite answers, and neither settles where authority is supposed to come from.

**If Saadani is now a national park** *(verify — I believe it is, gazetted in the 2000s)*, that is your closing image and you should check it today: the seat of a sovereignty, now a place to look at animals.

### 7 · The close — 3 sentences
A new constitution is being drafted. It will describe where authority comes from. **Neither of these men will be mentioned in it, and the people descended from both are being resettled at Chogo without being asked anything.** Stop there. One sentence more and it becomes a political letter.

## Traps

- ❌ **Making them heroes.** Bwana Heri sold people. Write it plainly in his own paragraph or the whole essay is propaganda
- ❌ **Making them villains.** Bunda armed himself by dealing with the Sultan. Neither man is a lesson
- ❌ **The hanging.** Check Bwana Heri's end in a printed source before it goes anywhere near an editor
- ❌ **Claiming Bunda as Zigua** · ❌ **merging Juba and Shabelle** · ❌ citing forums and wikis
