## THE EVIDENCE, AXIS BY AXIS — *Fastest Car*

**1 · Opening authority — 2.** Band 1 names *throat-clearing, double start*, and both are present: the story opens on seasons, weeds, the African sun and the Ethiopian famine, then starts again with the radio and the Peugeot. The second opening is the real one.

**2 · Prose control — 2.** Band 1 is *register lurches*, and it does. *"The trees seemed to pass me by as a river passed its banks"* sits near sentences with no subject at all. Detached participles run throughout — *Coming out of the foyer, a man in a black Kaunda suit… Entered a black Mercedes.* One sentence breaks person entirely: *"Sandimba remembered being told, by Moses yesterday morning."* Tense drifts from past into present at Dar es Salaam.

**3 · Character interiority — 3.** Band 3 is *consistent voice*. Sapi has a genuine interior — the amnesia, *did I kill a man?*, the mother he cannot reach, the child whose sex he never asked. It is more often reported than enacted, and the reader is rarely ahead of him.

**4 · Scene turn — 4.** Most scenes turn and several turn where they are not expected to: the classified FRELIMO document arriving as maandazi wrapping; Nzoo's shoes reappearing on a man getting into a Mercedes; the release with no statement taken. The hospital-food passage and the Dar es Salaam description do not turn.

**5 · The gap — 4.** The expectation is arrest, guilt, prison. What arrives is a conviction of a different South African and a release nobody explains. That gap is wide. It stops short of band 5 because the close moves off it before the reader has finished reorganising.

**6 · Structural economy — 2.** Band 3 is *nothing wasted*. The seasons paragraph, the cosmopolitan-Dar paragraph, the ward-meals paragraph and the doubled police interviews are surplus against a story whose engine is one crash and one conviction.

**7 · Controlling idea — 4.** *The state needs a story more than it needs the truth, and a man who might complicate it is released rather than asked.* Never stated anywhere. It falls short of 5 because the last paragraph looks away from it.

**8 · Resonant close — 3.** Band 3 is *resolves*. The BBC bulletin drags Dube, Batuli, Lilongwe and the child behind it — then the white-baby item arrives with nothing earlier holding it up.

**9 · Something to say — 4.** A real position, and not a dismissible one: the official record of a national death is manufactured, and the manufacture is still standing. Short of 5 because the ending declines to press it.
