# UNKNOWN UNKNOWNS — THE WRITING SIDE
### What nobody had gone looking for

Companion to [[Provenance — The AI Question and the Public Repo]]. The vault's prize apparatus is unusually good, so this note only reports **what it did not already contain** — and says plainly where it was already ahead of me.

---

## 🔴 NEW · 1 · The Foundation now investigates provenance, and the method matters
Covered in full in [[Provenance — The AI Question and the Public Repo]]. Short version: in 2026 the Commonwealth Foundation examined **working drafts, time-stamped documents and notes** to answer AI allegations, and is reviewing its processes. Your git history is the best evidence class available for defending authorship, and your public repo currently sits beside an AI draft of the same story. **Both facts are true at once.**

## 🔴 NEW · 2 · "Published online" has a boundary and nobody has tested where the repo sits
The rules exempt **personal blogs, personal websites and personal Facebook pages** from counting as prior publication. Magazines, anthologies and public platforms are not exempt. A public GitHub repository reads naturally as the former. It has not been confirmed, and rule 3g means Chantal cannot be entered a second time if the reading is wrong. **One neutral email, asked early.**

## 🟠 NEW · 3 · The expected values are not close, and the ranking is backwards
The vault treats the Commonwealth entry as the campaign and Morland as the parallel one. The arithmetic says the reverse.

| | Prize pool | Field | EV per application | Pays like |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --- |
| **Commonwealth Short Story Prize** | £15,000 | 7,806 | **£1.92** | A lottery ticket |
| **Miles Morland Scholarship** | £18,000 × 4 | ~800 | **£90** | **A monthly wage** |

**Morland is roughly 47× the Commonwealth per application** — and that understates it, because the Commonwealth pays a lump sum to one person while Morland pays **£1,500 a month for twelve months.** At today's rate that is about **$1,900/month against a household-and-debt figure of $1,228.**

> **Morland is the only thing in this vault, on either side of it, that pays the monthly bill in 2027.** It is not a prize. It is a salaried commission with a delivery schedule.

*(The vault already knows the delivery schedule — 10,000 words a month, whether you feel like it or not — and asks the right question about it in [[Morland — What They Actually Fund]]. What was missing was the side-by-side.)*

## The one thing the money analysis was missing

All three routes are priced per *outcome*. None is priced per **hour**, and the hours are where the decision actually is.

The Atelier has been running at roughly 25 hours a week. Over the twelve weeks to the deadline that is **300 hours** aimed at a £1.92 expectation. The same 300 hours against the paid-markets ladder is perhaps a dozen finished submissions; against Morland it is a proposal, a biography and a polished extract for a £90 expectation and a monthly wage if it lands.

**The craft hours are not wasted in any of the three cases** — that is what makes this hard, and it is why the vault has never had to choose. But the hours are finite, and only one of the three currently has a deadline in September.

## What to actually do about it
1. **Morland first, until 22 September.** It is 47× the EV, it pays monthly, and the extract, proposal and biography are all part-built.
2. **Chantal still goes in on 25 October.** The work is bought and paid for and rule 3g means there is no second attempt. But it is now the cheap ticket, not the campaign.
3. **Start the paid-markets ladder in November**, from the bottom, with the cut material the cycle generates anyway.
4. **Settle the repo question before either deadline.**
