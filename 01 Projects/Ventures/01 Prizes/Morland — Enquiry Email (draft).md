# Morland — enquiry email

→ [[Morland 2026 — The Campaign]]

**To:** `scholarships@milesmorlandfoundation.com`
**Subject:** Eligibility question — published sample, Morland Writing Scholarship 2026

---

Dear Miles Morland Foundation,

I am preparing an application for the 2026 Morland Writing Scholarships and have read the entry requirements and the FAQs. Two points about the published sample are not clear to me, and I would rather ask than guess.

1. The requirements ask for an excerpt of 2,000–5,000 words from work that has been published and offered for sale. The FAQ states that publication on Kindle is acceptable. **Does that include a book published on Kindle by the author themselves — self-published, listed for sale at a normal price — or is it limited to Kindle editions issued by a publisher?**

2. "Accepted works" and "Proposed Work" both exclude short story collections. I take that to govern the book being proposed rather than the published sample, since the sample is described only as published prose. **Can the excerpt therefore be a short story, or come from a published collection of short stories, provided the book I propose to write is a full-length novel?**

If it is helpful, may I also ask what evidence you consider sufficient for a Kindle title — would the Amazon listing URL, the ASIN and the publication date be enough to verify it without effort on your part?

Thank you for your time, and for the Scholarships.

Kind regards,
[your full name]
[town, Tanzania]
[phone]

---

## ✅ THE REPLY — received 5 August 2026, from Mathilda, MMF

> Hello,
>
> Thank you for your email.
>
> A book published by the author on Kindle would be accepted as would a short story from a collection used as the writing extract. You are right that a book proposal for a new book would not be accepted. An Amazon listing URL would be acceptable proof of sale for the writing extract.
>
> Best wishes,
>
> Mathilda

**This is the Foundation's written ruling. Keep the original email — do not delete it, and put a copy in the submission pack.**

| Settled | |
| --- | --- |
| Self-published Kindle book by the author | ✅ accepted |
| A short story, from a collection, as the extract | ✅ accepted |
| Proof of sale | ✅ the Amazon listing URL |

⚠️ **Unsettled — one garbled sentence.** *"A book proposal for a new book would not be accepted"* contradicts the entry requirements, which ask for exactly that: *"The proposal should be for a new work, not a work in progress."* **Do not write the proposal until this is clarified.** See the follow-up below.
