# 🧱 BRICK 3 · THE DETACHED SENTENCE
### *Two evenings. This is the single biggest score on the board.*

**Sentence structure scores 2 of 6.** The fault is one repeating shape: a sentence opens with an *-ing* or *Having* clause and then attaches it to the wrong subject, or to none.

**Hunt method:** search the file for sentences beginning with **-ing**, **Having**, **Being**, **Coming**, **Putting**, **Calling**, **Thinking**, **Making**. **Every hit gets a subject.**

**Governed by:** [[Sentence-Level Clumsiness]] — Gardner on the accidental awkwardness that breaks the fictional dream · [[Write Sentences That Work]] — Cleland's sentence discipline · [[Sentence Rhythm as Emotional Instrument]] for what to do with the rhythm once the syntax is sound.

> **✅ Done when:** no sentence in the file leaves the reader guessing who performed the action. **Read each repaired sentence aloud — if you have to reread it, it is not fixed.**

# 🧱 BRICK 4 · THE OPENING

**Governed by:** [[The Beginning — Three Paragraphs at Most]] — Barrett's ceiling, stated in 1900 in nearly the modern panel's words · [[The First-Page Cut]] — the vault's own instrument · [[Voice Is What You Cannot Help Doing]] — Gaiman, on why the first page must already sound like you · [[Set the Stage]] — Cleland.

# 🧱 BRICK 5 · ECONOMY
### *One evening. Structural economy is a 2.*

Four passages currently justify themselves rather than the story: **the seasons paragraph · the cosmopolitan-Dar description · the ward meals · the doubled police interviews.**

**Governed by:** [[The Art of Omission — Unity, Padding, and Proportion]] — Barrett, the only base in the vault about the short story *as a form with edges* · [[The Fullness of Omission]] and [[White Space as Grammar]] — Faulkner, on what an absence does that a presence cannot · [[The Isolation Principle — One Emotion, One Incident]].

**The test to apply to each:** *if this passage were gone, would anything later break?* **Band 5 on this axis is "cutting any scene breaks two others."**

> ⚠️ **Do not cut the two constables at the war memorial.** It scores on axis 4 and on Chair-Test *World*, and it costs seventy words. **Economy is not shortness.**
>
> **✅ Done when:** every remaining passage is load-bearing, and the word count is still above 2,000.

# 🧱 BRICK 6 · REGISTER
### *One evening. Prose control is a 2 — and the cause is not vocabulary.*

The register lurches because the **distance** lurches: one sentence is inside Sapi's skull, the next is a tour guide describing Dar es Salaam, the next is a newsreader.

**Governed by:** [[Psychic Distance]] — Gardner's ladder from *It was winter* down to *Kaboom*, and the rule that you move along it deliberately, never by accident. **This is the exact diagnosis for your axis 2.** · [[The Vivid and Continuous Fictional Dream]] — what the lurch breaks · [[Three Faults of Soul — Sentimentality, Frigidity, Mannerism]] — for the seasons paragraph specifically · [[The Paragraph as Dwelling]] — Faulkner, on the paragraph as a unit with one altitude.

> **✅ Done when:** you can mark each paragraph 1–5 for distance and the moves between them are ones you chose.
