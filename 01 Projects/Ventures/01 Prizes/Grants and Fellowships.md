# 01b · GRANTS AND FELLOWSHIPS
### Grants pay far better than prizes. Most of them you cannot use.

→ back to [[00 The Board|The Board]] · sits beside [[Prize Portfolio]]

---

## THE FILTER THAT MATTERS

Most writing money for African writers comes as a **residency** — six weeks in Italy, four weeks in Nairobi, a season in Sharjah. Beautiful, and largely **useless to someone with a salaried job, school fees and dependents.**

So this list is sorted by **one question: can you do it without leaving home and without leaving your job?**

| | Can you actually take it? |
| --- | --- |
| **Grant paid to you, work from home** | ✅ The only category that fits your life |
| **Online fellowship** | ✅ Fits |
| **Residency abroad** | ⚠️ Needs leave, visa, and someone to run the household |
| **Nomination-only** | ❌ Cannot apply. Ignore entirely |

---

## ✅ WORTH YOUR TIME

### 1 · Miles Morland Writing Scholarship — **£18,000**

**The biggest realistic number available to you.** Paid **monthly across 12 months**, to write at home. Non-fiction may extend to 18 months.

- Born in Africa, or both parents born in Africa ✅
- Writing in English ✅
- Project must be a **full-length book, 80,000+ words**
- Requires a **2,000–5,000 word excerpt from work already published and offered for sale**
- **Deadline 22 September**

> ✅ **Blocker cleared, 5 Aug 2026.** The MMF FAQ: *"Being published on Kindle is acceptable; it is the only online medium that is."* Free publication does not count; the sample must be prose. **A £0.99 Kindle short story creates eligibility in 72 hours** — no novel required. → **[[Morland 2026 — The Campaign]]**
>
> ⚠️ **New for 2026: any AI use in the submission or book proposal means silent disqualification, with no notification.** Read the collisions section of the campaign note before touching this application.

### 2 · African Liberty Writing Fellowship — **paid, entirely online**

Runs a full year, **conducted online**, with stipends paid on performance. No travel, no leave, no visa.

**It is essay and opinion writing, not fiction** — which matches the "essays" part of your plan and builds a public byline history far faster than fiction does. It leans policy/liberty in editorial slant; go in knowing that.

**Cycle ran 13 July 2026 – 13 July 2027, applications closed 30 April.** Diarise the 2027 call for **March–April**.

### 3 · Sillerman First Book Prize — $1,000 + publication

**Poetry only.** Listed because it is the cleanest African-specific book prize with an open call — **submissions 15 September to 1 December.** Only relevant if you ever write a collection.

## ⚠️ REAL MONEY, BUT YOU'D HAVE TO TRAVEL

### Tejumola Olaniyan Creative Writers-in-Residence Fellowship — **50,000 AED (~$13,600)**

Inclusive of accommodation, return airfare, visa, and a **10,000 AED monthly stipend**. Open to novelists, short story writers, playwrights and poets working on Africa and its diaspora. At The Africa Institute.

**Deadline was 1 March 2026** — so the next window is early 2027. Genuinely open application, genuinely well funded. **The question is whether you could physically go.**

### Africa Women Writers Residency — fully funded, 4 weeks, Nairobi

Mentorship, training and a publishing platform for emerging African women storytellers. **Eligibility is restricted to women writers** — listed for completeness.

## ❌ DO NOT SPEND TIME ON THESE

| | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| **Windham-Campbell** — $175,000 | **Nomination only.** You cannot apply. The money is enormous and irrelevant |
| **Civitella Ranieri** — 6 weeks, all found | **Invitation only**, via international nomination and jury. No unsolicited applications |
| **Aké Arts & Book Festival** | A festival, not a grant. Useful for network, costs money to attend |

**A prize you cannot apply for is not an opportunity, it's a daydream.** They are listed here so you stop looking at them.

## HOW GRANTS COMPARE TO EVERYTHING ELSE ON THIS BOARD

```
Miles Morland                £18,000   ≈ $23,000  ≈ TSh 61M   (approximate)
Tejumola Olaniyan            ~$13,600              ≈ TSh 36M
Safal Kiswahili               $5,000               ≈ TSh 13.2M
Commonwealth, expected value  £1.92
```

> **One grant is worth more than every prize on the board combined, several times over.** And unlike a prize, a grant is awarded on a **plan plus a sample** rather than on one story beating 7,806 others.
>
> **This is the highest-value writing money available to you, and it is the part of the writing track most worth keeping alive.**

**But note what every serious grant requires: a published sample and a book-length project.** That is the actual argument for [[Paid Markets]] — magazine credits and a self-published book are what make you *eligible* for the money that matters. The stories are not the destination; they're the entry ticket.
