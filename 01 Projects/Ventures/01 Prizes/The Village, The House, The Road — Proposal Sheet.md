## THE BOOK

> ### **Seventeen years in which the Tanzanian state entered three spaces: the village, the house, and the road.**

**Part I · THE VILLAGE — 1967–1976**
The Arusha Declaration, February 1967. Then **Operation Vijiji**: millions moved into planned Ujamaa villages, houses left standing, graves left behind, whole settlements emptied by lorry. **The state enters where you live.**

**Part II · THE HOUSE — 1983**
**The Economic Sabotage Act No. 9 of 1983**, launched 5 April, run by Sokoine. The burden of proof reversed — explain where your wealth came from. Special courts, no lawyers, defend yourself. **Televisions and bundles of money thrown into the gutters of Oysterbay and Upanga**, collected by police, sent to the Bank of Tanzania. **The state enters what you own.**

> [!important] ➕ ADDED 2026-08-12 — the motive layer, and the hinge → [[The Scarcity — What 1983 Was Standing In]]
> **The Act is unintelligible without the shortage.** Empty shelves, *foleni* for sugar and soap, yellow American maize, no cloth and no *mitumba* to fall back on — and behind all of it the **$500m Kagera War**, the 1979 floods and drought, and a month in **November 1980 when the country's major industry was ordered to switch off.** Sokoine's logic only works standing in a queue: *these goods exist somewhere, and somebody is holding them.* **This is the cleanest route to rule 1 — you need no verdict on Nyerere if you show what he was standing in.**
>
> 🔴 **And the find: *Nguvu Kazi*, the Human Resources Deployment Act 1983 — same year, and driven by the Office of the Prime Minister.** It rounded up unemployed men in Dar and **sent them back to the rural areas.** Vijiji put people on lorries *to* the village in 1973–76; Nguvu Kazi put them on lorries *back* in 1983, out of Sokoine's own office. **The three parts become one motion instead of three episodes.**
>
> ⚠️ **Motive and weather, not a fourth part.** No THE QUEUE, no THE SHOP — a survey does not get funded. And **verify the commencement date**: the legal record says *retroactive to 25 March 1983*, not 5 April.

**Part III · THE ROAD — 12 April 1984**
Fourteen months in, on the Dodoma–Dar road at **Dakawa**, Sokoine's car is struck by a Land Cruiser driven by **Dumisani Dube**, a South African freedom fighter. Dube is convicted. **Forty years later the country still does not believe it.** **The state ends on a road.**

**Roughly 85,000 words. Investigative narrative non-fiction, first person where the reporting is yours.** ⚠️ **Non-fiction may buy up to 18 months** at the Foundation's discretion — and a book with this much archive work is exactly the case for it.

## THE SPINE — one sentence

> *A Prime Minister spent fourteen months making the rich account for every shilling, and then died on a road — and to understand why, you have to go back seventeen years to the morning the lorries came for the villages.*

**The investigation is the engine. The history is what the investigation keeps running into.** Not a survey of Ujamaa with a death at the end — **a death, opened up, that turns out to contain seventeen years.**

## 🔴 THE ONE SERIOUS RISK — and how it is handled

**The Foundation excludes *"works of special interest such as religious or political writings."*** This book is about a state, a president, a party and a set of laws. **A careless proposal reads as a political argument and is excluded before literary merit is considered.**

**Three rules that keep it safe, and they are also what make it a better book:**

**1 · No verdict on Nyerere.** Not a defence, not a prosecution. **He is a character with intentions, and the book follows what happened to people.** The moment a paragraph argues about socialism, you have written the excluded thing.

**2 · People, not policy.** The woman whose house was left standing. The man in Upanga who threw a television into a gutter. The officer who collected it. **A policy has no scenes; a person does** — and this is the Writing Constitution, not a compromise for the judges.

**3 · Do not solve the murder.** ⚠️ **You cannot prove an assassination and you must not imply you will.** The book is about a country that does not believe the official story and why it does not. **Certainty is the trap; the doubt is the subject.**

> **Baraka's Shakahola book is the exact precedent** — an atrocity with political roots, funded because it was reported through the lives lost rather than argued.

## 🚧 THE SCOPE BOUNDARY — decided 5 August 2026

> ### **The book stops in 1984. The proposal names nothing after it.**

**Raised:** whether to bring in **Magufuli**, who died in office in March 2021 having built a reputation on graft and on renegotiating mining contracts with Western companies — and the **October 2025 election crackdown**, in which the UN and Amnesty documented extrajudicial killings, enforced disappearances, mass detentions and a digital blackout.

**Both rhyme with the book. Neither goes in it.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **The rules** | A book that stops in 1984 is history. One that reaches 2025 is a live political intervention, and *works of special interest such as religious or political writings* are excluded. **That is the difference between funded and dropped** |
| **The craft, and this is the real reason** | The whole method is two facts side by side and no comment. **A Tanzanian reader in 2026 supplies the rest without help.** Name it and you have done their work for them, and an observation becomes a thesis. **The silence is the loudest thing in the book** |
| **The arithmetic** | 700 words. Every sentence on 2021 or 2025 is a sentence not spent on 1983, and 1983 is the part nobody else has |
| **The rhyme becomes an allegation** | Put three dead or displaced leaders in one paragraph and you have made a claim you cannot support in 700 words and should not try to |

**Where it can live:** the book's **final chapter**, decided in 2028, when the book exists, a publisher exists, and you have your own read on the country. **Not now, and not in an application form.**

## WHAT THE 400–1,000 WORDS MUST DO

**Seven things, in your words. One continuous piece of prose — no headings, no numbered sections, no bullets.** → [[Morland 2025 — Post-Mortem]] for why that matters.

1. **Open with an image, not a thesis.** The televisions in the gutters. It is true, it is strange, and no reader walks away from it
2. **The spine sentence** — the death, and the seventeen years inside it
3. **Name the form** — investigative narrative non-fiction, roughly 85,000 words, three parts
4. **The three movements** — village, house, road. Five seconds and they see the shape
5. **The dates**, plainly. 1967 · 1973–76 · April 1983 · 12 April 1984
6. **Why you** — *shown, never claimed.* A Tanzanian who works in Kiswahili, in the region, with the generation that remembers still alive to be asked
7. **One paragraph of real prose.** They judge literary merit and the proposal is the only new writing they have from you
