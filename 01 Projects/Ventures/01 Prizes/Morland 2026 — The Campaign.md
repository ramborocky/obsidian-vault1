## 🚧 THE THREE STANDING BOUNDARIES

**1 · The book stops in 1984.** Nothing after it is named — not Magufuli, not October 2025. **The reader supplies the present without help, and naming it turns an observation into a thesis and the application into a political document.** → the scope boundary in [[The Village, The House, The Road — Proposal Sheet]]

**2 · No verdict on Nyerere.** A character with intentions, never a defendant. **People, not policy** — a policy has no scenes. **And do not solve the murder:** you cannot prove it, and the country's doubt is the subject.

**3 · The public remote.** `github.com/ramborocky/obsidian-vault1` is readable by anyone. **Twenty seconds to make private, nothing deleted, history intact.** Standing recommendation, your call, not done yet.
