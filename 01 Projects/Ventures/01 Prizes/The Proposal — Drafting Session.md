# ✒️ THE PROPOSAL — DRAFTING SESSION
### Seven moves, in order. **No headings, no numbers, no bullets — one continuous piece of prose.**

→ [[The Village, The House, The Road — Proposal Sheet]] · [[Morland 2025 — Post-Mortem]]

> **The band is 400–1,000 words. Aim at ~700.** Under 500 looks thin against a £18,000 ask; over 900 starts explaining. **The word budgets below add to about 670, which leaves you room to run long somewhere that deserves it.**
>
> **You have every fact you need already.** Nothing below asks you to invent. It asks you to choose.

---

## MOVE 1 · THE IMAGE · ~80 words
### *Open on something true and strange. Never on a thesis.*

**What you have:** April 1983. The Economic Sabotage Act. In Oysterbay and Upanga, people threw **televisions and bundles of money into the streets and gutters** rather than be asked where they came from. **Police collected them and sent them to the Bank of Tanzania.**

**The decision:** whose eyes. A man carrying a television out of his own house at night, or the constable who picked it up in the morning. **Pick one and stay in them for the whole paragraph.**

⚠️ **The trap:** the 2025 proposal opened by comparing a chief to a Marvel character. **Open on the gutter, not on a comparison.** If your first sentence needs a second sentence to explain why it matters, it is the wrong first sentence.

---

## MOVE 2 · THE SPINE · ~60 words
### *One sentence the reader could repeat to somebody else.*

**What you have:**

> *A Prime Minister spent fourteen months making the rich account for every shilling. Then he died on the Dodoma road, and a South African went to prison for it.*

**The decision:** whether the seventeen years appear here or in move 4. **Here is braver.**

⚠️ **The trap:** *"This book will explore…"* **Never write that.** State what happened. The book is implied by the fact that you are writing to them.

---

## MOVE 3 · THE FORM · ~70 words
### *Give the judges a shelf to put it on.* → pattern 4

**What you have:** **investigative narrative non-fiction · roughly 85,000 words · three parts.**

**Say all three, plainly, in one sentence.** Every funded project named its form: *walking memoir · AfroSurrealist fable · climate fiction*.

⚠️ **The trap:** *"a compelling narrative that bridges scholarly history and popular biography."* **That is the 2025 proposal and it says nothing.** Name the form, name the length, move on. **No adjective belongs in this paragraph.**

---

## MOVE 4 · THE THREE MOVEMENTS · ~180 words
### *The shape, in five seconds. This is the paragraph that gets you shortlisted.*

**What you have — and every date is verified:**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **THE VILLAGE** · 1967–1976 | The Arusha Declaration, February 1967. Then Operation Vijiji — millions moved into planned villages, houses left standing, graves left behind. **The state enters where you live** |
| **THE HOUSE** · 1983 | The Economic Sabotage Act No. 9, launched 5 April, run by Sokoine. Burden of proof reversed. Special courts, no lawyers. **The state enters what you own** |
| **THE ROAD** · 12 April 1984 | Dakawa, on the Dodoma–Dar road. A Land Cruiser driven by Dumisani Dube, a South African freedom fighter. He is convicted. **Forty years on, the country still does not believe it** |

**The decision:** whether to name the three parts as titles. **Do it.** *The Village, The House, The Road* is memorable and it does the work of an outline without looking like one.

⚠️ **The trap:** this is where the 2025 proposal became a table of contents — *"Part I will portray… Part II will detail…"* **Write it as three things that happened, not three things you will write.**

---

## MOVE 5 · WHY YOU · ~120 words
### *Shown. Never claimed. This is the paragraph nobody else could write.* → pattern 6

**What you have, and it is extraordinary:**

- Born 1977, the year CCM was founded. **Called Chama at home for it**
- Your father served close to Nyerere; **ambassador to the United Kingdom from 1986**
- **1990, a hospital in London.** Nyerere asked your name, your father explained the nickname, **and Mwalimu laughed**
- Christmas cards at the house with his signature
- **CPA(T).** You can read a ledger, a tribunal record and a seizure inventory — **most of the 800 cannot**
- **Kiswahili.** Most of this archive is in Kiswahili and most of your competition cannot read it

**The decision:** how much of the Nyerere material to spend here, given it is also in the biography. **Spend the laugh. Leave the Christmas cards to the bio.**

⚠️ **The trap:** the heading *"Why Me?"*, and the sentence *"my connection to this material is both personal and intellectual."* **Neither goes anywhere near this.** State the facts and let the reader conclude.

---

## MOVE 6 · WHAT THE YEAR BUYS · ~120 words
### *Deliverability. Foundations fund books they believe will exist.*

**What you have:**

- **Iringa** — the Vijiji resettlements, on the ground
- **The Dodoma–Dar road at Dakawa** — where it happened
- **Tanzania National Archives** · party records · the 1984 inquiry papers · special-tribunal court records
- ***Daily News*** and ***Uhuru***, April 1983 – April 1984
- **Dube's trial record**
- **Living witnesses** — people moved in 1973–76 and people arrested in 1983 are alive **now**

**The decision — and it is your strongest argument:** say plainly that **this is the last decade in which those people can be asked.** That is a reason for *this year* rather than some year, and no other applicant's project has a clock on it.

⚠️ **The trap:** *"identifying key archives and sources"* and *"potentially to Germany."* **Name them or cut them.** Also worth saying: every site is inside Tanzania. **No visa, no flight, no fixer.** This book can actually be delivered.

---

## MOVE 7 · THE LAST LINE · ~40 words
### *End on the doubt. Not on a claim.*

⚠️ **Do not end as the 2025 proposal did** — *"I am confident this book will become a definitive work on an African icon."* **A promise of quality is the weakest sentence available to you.**

**End on the thing that is actually true: the country does not believe the official story, and the book is about why.** → [[End Two Paragraphs Earlier]]

**Test:** cover everything but your last sentence. **Does it make a stranger want the book?**

---

## 🚫 THE FIVE THINGS THAT MUST NOT APPEAR

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Headings, numbers, bullets** | It is prose. The 2025 proposal had *2. · 3. Why Me? · 4. Why the Morland Scholarship? · 5. Conclusion* |
| **Adjectives about your own book** | *compelling · vivid · engrossing · definitive · powerful* |
| **A verdict on Nyerere** | Not a defence, not a prosecution. **A character with intentions** |
| **Solving the murder** | You cannot prove it and must not imply you will. **The doubt is the subject** |
| **Asking for the money** | They know why you wrote |

## 🖊️ HOW TO ACTUALLY WRITE IT

**1 · Three sittings, not one.** Moves 1–3, then 4–5, then 6–7. **Never edit while drafting.**

**2 · Write move 1 last if it will not come.** The image paragraph is the hardest and the most important; the other six will tell you what it has to do.

**3 · Read the whole thing aloud before you count words.** → [[The Cold Read Protocol]]

**4 · Then count.** 400–1,000, and 700 is the target.

**5 · Then run [[The De-Slop Pass — English Prose]] and [[The NAPLAN Floor — Ten Mechanical Criteria]].** The proposal gets the same mechanical floor as the story. **A typo here costs more than a typo anywhere else in the application.**

> **Deadline: written by 31 August, submitted 1 September.**
