# 🔍 ODUAH READ CLOSELY
### *Mama Boko Haram* — the reporting behind ***Daughters of Bloody Soil***, the [[Morland 2022 — The Five, Read Closely|2022 Morland non-fiction project]], and the nearest thing in the whole roll to the book you are proposing.

→ [[Whom To Emulate — Four Writers From Outside the Vault]] · [[The Proposal — Draft to Rewrite by Hand]] · [[The Village, The House, The Road — Proposal Sheet]]

> [!important] Why this one and not Komba
> Komba wrote fiction about a dead general. **Oduah wrote non-fiction about a living state's violence, in the first person, from inside the country, with witnesses she had to earn.** Same form as yours, same problem as yours, and she was funded for it. **This piece is the method, in public, at full length.**

---

## THE EIGHT MOVES

### 1 · The opening is a room, not a war
> *"It was another scorching afternoon in Maiduguri… 51-year-old Aisha Wakil sat in her office talking to a jihadi fighter named Usman."*

A time, a room, two people, and a secret. **The 30,000 dead do not appear until the third paragraph** — after you are already in the room and want to know what happens.

✅ **Your Move 1 is correct as drafted.** The televisions going into the gutter is this exact move. **Do not trade it for a statistic.**

### 2 · One person is the lens, not the subject
The subject is a fifteen-year insurgency. **You see all of it through one woman**, and she is not a hero: an Igbo Catholic who converted, married north, and calls Boko Haram fighters her sons.

⚠️ **This is where your proposal is thinnest.** Sokoine is your engine — correct — but **he is dead, and there is no living named person anywhere in the document.** *"The people who were put on the lorries in 1974"* is a category. **Wakil is a name.**

### 3 · The central figure is never resolved
Wakil stops a university bombing — **by sending her own daughter onto the campus as a human shield.** She hides wanted men in her parlour. She is declared wanted by the army. She ends the piece in prison on fraud charges, blaming a subordinate. **Oduah does not adjudicate a single one of these.**

✅ **This is your rule 1 and rule 3, executed at professional length.** Sokoine ran a law with no bail and no lawyers and is remembered as the honest one. **Leave him exactly that unresolved.**

### 4 · 🥘 Objects carry the entire politics — and one object carries the thesis
*Ka'aji* incense in the corner. *Lalle* henna on her hands. Bomb parts buried in her garden, pointed out on a walk. A tablet showing Garga dancing.

**And then the masterstroke: *isi ewu*.** A southern Igbo dish — spiced goat's head in red palm oil — cooked by a convert, fed to Kanuri boys she raised, and later to **Boko Haram fighters and state security agents eating in the same room at the same time.**

> **It is never explained. It carries the whole argument of the piece and it is a plate of food.**

**Your equivalents are already in hand:** the televisions, and now [[The Scarcity — What 1983 Was Standing In|the yellow maize]], the *foleni*, the rags. **What you do not yet have is your *isi ewu*** — the one object that appears three times and means more each time.

### 5 · The reporter appears about six times in five thousand words
> *"The first time I met Wakil, in 2018, she grinned and embraced me tenderly when I told her that I was also Igbo. 'It's like being with my sister.'"*

**That is the only place her own identity enters, and it is doing work** — it explains why she got the access. Everywhere else: *I watched her shoulders shake up and down. I sat on the fluffy pink comforter beside Wakil.* **Presence, never autobiography.**

✅ **This is what *"first person where the reporting is mine"* actually means.** Not memoir. **You appear to explain how you know, and to witness. Nothing else.**

### 6 · History arrives in the middle, compressed, then leaves
The Kanem-Bornu empire, sharia adoption in twelve states 1999–2001, Yusuf's rise, the 2009 raid. **Perhaps a sixth of the piece, all of it delivered between scenes, none of it at the top.**

✅ **Directly transferable.** Arusha 1967 and Operation Vijiji go *between* scenes. **A reader will accept a great deal of history once they are already inside a room.**

### 7 · Structure is not chronological
Opens May 2019 → back to 1989 and the conversion → the boys → the history → 2009 → the negotiations → 2016 → 2018 and Garga → prison → the video.

✅ **Your book is already this shape** — *"organised not by chronology but by the three spaces."* **Keep it.**

### 8 · It ends on an image and refuses to conclude
> *"All she wanted, she said, was for her boys to come out, so she could cook isi ewu for them and they could eat together like old times."*

No verdict. No lesson. **The last image in the piece is Boko Haram dancing under a bright sky**, and Oduah simply stops.

✅ **[[End Two Paragraphs Earlier]], proven at long-form length.**

## 🚫 AND WHAT SHE REFUSES TO SOLVE

**The piece is built on things that cannot be established, and it says so on the page:**

| The question | What she writes |
| --- | --- |
| Who killed her informants? | ***"She never knew who did the killing."*** |
| Who fired first in June 2009? | *"It's not clear who shot first… The government never investigated what happened."* |
| Is Wakil guilty of the fraud? | Charges, her denial, the postponed trial. **No adjudication** |
| Where are the missing Chibok girls? | *"about 112 are still missing"* |

> **This is your Move 6½ — the refusal — vindicated.** Oduah's whole piece runs on unresolved things, and it did not weaken the work. **It is the work.** *You cannot prove Dakawa was an assassination* belongs on the page for the same reason.

---

## ✅ FOUR THINGS TO DO TO THE PROPOSAL

- [ ] **🔴 Put one living named person in it.** This is the biggest gap and the cheapest fix. One person, named, who was moved in 1974 or arrested in 1983, in one sentence. **Wakil is why Oduah's pitch worked.** *"The people who were put on the lorries"* is a demographic; **a name is a book.**
- [ ] **Find your *isi ewu*.** One object that recurs three times and gains meaning each time. The televisions are your opening image — **that is a different job.** Candidates from the research: the yellow maize · the *foleni* · a ration card · the bale.
- [ ] **Cut any first person that is not access or witness.** Oduah appears six times in five thousand words. **Audit yours against that rate.**
- [ ] **Keep the refusal exactly where it is.** Oduah proves it is a strength, not a hedge.

## 📌 One more thing worth knowing

**This ran in the Guardian Long Read at roughly five thousand words.** That is the register Morland funded, and it is also — near enough — **the length of a submittable extract.** Oduah did not win on a book. She won on journalism of exactly this shape.
