## THE DRAFT

I was born in Dar es Salaam in 1977, the year Chama cha Mapinduzi was founded, and at home they called me Chama for it.

My father had served close to Julius Nyerere. In 1986 he was posted to London as Tanzania's ambassador, and I finished my primary schooling at Malorees and my secondary at Whitefield before coming home to sit A-levels at Azania Secondary in Dar es Salaam. In 1990 my father took me to visit Mwalimu in a hospital in London. Nyerere asked my name. My father explained the nickname — born the year the party was born — and Mwalimu laughed. There were Christmas cards at our house with his signature on them.

I am an accountant. I hold an Advanced Diploma in Accountancy from the Tanzania Institute of Accountancy and an MBA in Information Technology Management from the Institute of Accountancy Arusha, and I am a CPA(T). For `[X]` years I have worked in public service, currently as a senior officer with the National Health Insurance Fund, under the Ministry of Health.

I write `[when — say the hour and the place]`. I have published two books in English, a collection of stories and a collection of poems, both on Amazon.

I live in Kigamboni with my wife of twenty years and our six children. In 2022 I cycled twenty-one kilometres at Pande. In 2024 I ran the Safee Marathon for autism awareness, and in 2025 the half at the Kilimanjaro International Marathon, for childhood cancer.

I was named after a ruling party and raised inside the state's own household. The book I want to write is about what that state did to its villages, and to the man who tried to make it honest.

## WHY IT IS BUILT THIS WAY

**The last two sentences are the whole document.** Everything above them is evidence; those two are the verdict, and they are what makes the proposal inevitable without ever saying *this is why I am the right person*. → pattern 6 in [[Morland — What They Actually Fund]].

> **A man nicknamed after the ruling party, raised in the ambassador's house, who made Nyerere laugh at nine years old — proposing to investigate what that state did to its own villages.**
>
> **Nobody else in eight hundred applications has that sentence.** It converts closeness to power into standing to ask, which is the single hardest thing a book like this has to establish, and you establish it in a line.

**The Nyerere hospital scene is left as action, not as feeling.** He asked, my father answered, he laughed. **No adjective anywhere near it** — the original WASIFU has *macho yakametameta*, and in English that register would read as reverence and cost you the reader. The facts are heavier. → the no-adjectives rule in [[Bios in the Wild — How Other Writers Do It]].

**The accountancy is stated the way Kalimamukwento states her law:** a qualification, an institution, a date, and nothing else. No struggle, no balance, no apology.

**The running is in because it is the only thing in your WASIFU that is purely yours** — not inherited, not credentialled. Two of those three races were run for other people's children. **It says something a paragraph of self-description could not.**

**What was cut, and why:** the closing paragraph of the WASIFU about *uongozi wa haki, utu kamili na haki ya kijamii*. **True, and written for a Nyerere prize.** To this panel it reads as a political position rather than a writer's, and the exclusion on political writing is live.

## 📌 TWO THINGS THE WASIFU CHANGED

**1 · You have two books on Amazon, not one.** A collection of stories and a collection of poems. **The MMF sample must be prose** — the poetry book cannot be the extract, but it is a second published title and it belongs in the bio.

⚠️ **And it closes a door: [[Sillerman First Book Prize for African Poetry|Sillerman]] excludes anyone who has published a poetry collection, including self-published if it was sold.** That prize is now formally out. → [[08 The Opportunities Board]]

**2 · Kigamboni, Kimbiji — and Dar es Salaam, not the lake.** Several notes in this campaign assumed a lake town. **Correct the geography before the proposal is written**, because "why you" for a book about Iringa and the Dodoma road has to run through family and archive, not through residence.

### 🔴 The structural risk nobody's bio takes

**In all three real bios, the writing arrives in sentence one or two.** Ndlovu is a *sarungano* before anything else. Kalimamukwento is a *writer* before she is a lawyer.

**In this draft the writing does not appear until paragraph four of five** — a reader spends 120 words on childhood, a father and a hospital before learning you write at all.

**It is defensible:** MMF asked for *yourself and your background*, not an author bio, and 300 words demands a life. **The Nyerere scene also earns its place by setting up the last line.** But it is a real departure, and it is the thing to watch when you rewrite.

### The two fixes, both cheap

**1 · Name the books.** *Voices of Azania* and the poetry title, as titles. **A named book is a fact; "a collection of stories" is a category.**

**2 · Get *Tanzanian* and the writing earlier.** One clause in the opening sentence does both, and it costs you nothing structurally — the Chama line still lands.
