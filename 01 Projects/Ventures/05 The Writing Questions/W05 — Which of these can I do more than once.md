# 5 · Which of these can I do more than once?

**Rule 3g:** a story entered cannot be entered again. Chantal gets one attempt at the Commonwealth, ever. That single fact should shape everything around it and mostly hasn't.

Sort the writing routes by repeatability and the picture reorders itself:

| | Attempts available | Feedback per attempt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chantal at the Commonwealth | **One. Forever.** | A yes or a silence, twelve months later |
| Morland | Annual, and losers reapply | A yes or a silence, annually |
| Paid markets | **Continuous** — every story, many venues, all year | Weeks, sometimes with a note |

One-shot events deserve care in proportion to their irreversibility, and **the correct amount of optimisation for a one-shot is high.** That is the honest defence of the Atelier: the apparatus exists because there is no second go.

But the same fact cuts the other way. **A one-shot cannot teach you anything in time to use it.** Whatever the Commonwealth's answer is, it arrives after the decisions it could have informed. Skill comes from the repeatable column, and the repeatable column is the one with no apparatus built around it.

The cycle makes this sharper than it looks. Chantal is story one of a planned cycle in Mihogoni, and the cut material is trunk material for the rest. **Every story after this one is a fresh attempt at everything** — every prize, every market, every reader. There is exactly one story in the plan that gets a single shot, and it is the one currently absorbing all the hours.

> **The question:** is this a one-shot or a repeatable — and am I spending like it's the other one?
