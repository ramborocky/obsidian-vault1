National Livestock & Commodity Exchange

Tanzania owns 39 million cattle. None of them are collateral.

MNADA turns a live animal into a graded, tagged, custodied, price-discovered lot — and then into a financial instrument a bank can lend against. Cattle and shoats first; sesame, cashew, pigeon peas and maize on the same rails.

01

The gap is not supply. It is price discovery and title.

Tanzania has the second-largest cattle herd in Africa and a functioning digital exchange for crops. It has neither for animals. A pastoralist in Simiyu sells to the one broker who showed up. A butcher in Dar pays a chain of four intermediaries. Neither knows the other exists, and no bank can lend against an animal it cannot identify, value, or repossess.

39.2m Cattle, 2024/25 — up 3.4% year on year. Plus 28.6m goats and 9.65m sheep. Ministry of Livestock & Fisheries
10–13% Offtake rate for indigenous cattle. Commercial dairy systems reach 22% — the gap is market access, not biology. Tanzania Livestock Master Plan
400+ Primary livestock markets nationwide, almost all settling in cash, on paper, with no price record. Ministry of Livestock & Fisheries
TZS 2.68tn Traded through TMX crop auctions in 2025/26, on 1.12bn kg. Proof the digital-auction model already clears at national scale here. Tanzania Mercantile Exchange

The seller's problem

No forward price signal. The herder walks animals to market on a fixed day and accepts whatever the assembled traders offer. Distress sales during drought clear at a third of value because the only buyers present are the ones who drove there.

The buyer's problem

Abattoirs run at roughly half capacity because supply arrives unscheduled and ungraded. Six abattoirs operate nationally with seven more under construction — all of them buying blind, on market day, against a spot cash requirement.

The bank's problem

TZS 33.22 trillion of livestock sits outside the credit system. There is no register, no valuation method, no custody, and no enforceable security interest. The asset exists; the instrument does not.

The state's problem

The Livestock Identification, Registration and Traceability Act has been law since 2010 and remains largely unimplemented outside pilots. Disease control, theft, and export certification all depend on a register nobody has funded into existence.

02

How an animal becomes an instrument

This is the whole business. Crops solved it with the Warehouse Receipt System: a graded deposit in a licensed warehouse produces a receipt that is title, and title is collateral. Livestock has never had an equivalent because you cannot pour a cow into a silo. The four steps below are our answer — and they are strictly sequential, because each one is only meaningful if the previous one happened.

STEP 01

Identify — the tag is the ISIN

Every animal entering the system gets a uniquely coded ear tag registered against the keeper's National ID and the origin ward. This is not our invention: it is the National Livestock Identification, Registration and Traceability Registry that the 2010 Act already requires. We implement existing law rather than lobby for new law.

Tag + application ≈ TZS 1,800/head.
Recovered via a verification fee at first sale.
Field-proven on the Northern Tanzania–Narok–Nairobi corridor pilot.
STEP 02

Grade — turn an animal into a specification

At the market pen: weigh-band, dentition age, sex, breed group, body condition score. Output is a standard lot code — TZ‑C‑M2‑300‑350‑BCS3 — that a buyer 900km away can bid on without seeing the animal. Fungibility is what makes an exchange possible; grading is what manufactures fungibility.

Grader kit: cattle crush, weigh bar, tablet.
≈ TZS 8.4m per node, amortised 4 years.
Grade disputes settle on the recorded weigh-bar reading.
STEP 03

Custody — the Livestock Deposit Receipt

The animal is held at a licensed holding ground or partner feedlot under a custody agreement, and the platform issues a Livestock Deposit Receipt: a transferable electronic document of title naming the graded lot, the custodian, and the feed/veterinary schedule. This is the legal object the bank actually takes security over. Same architecture as a warehouse receipt; different asset.

Custody standard modelled on the Warehouse Receipts framework.
Mortality risk insured at the holding ground, not carried by the lender.
STEP 04

Clear — bid, match, escrow, settle

Buyers nationwide bid against the lot code. On match, funds move into escrow from mobile money or bank account, the LDR transfers, movement permits generate automatically, and the seller is paid on release. 80.98m mobile money accounts and 84.2% internet penetration mean the rails already reach the pastoralist. Feature-phone USSD covers the rest.

Settlement T+0 on spot, T+30/60/90 on forwards.
Escrow held at the partner bank — which is where the float sits.

Payback per node

Setup capex of TZS 23m and roughly nine months of sub-maturity operation put node payback at month 14–16. Nodes are opened in clusters of four along a single corridor so one supervisor and one veterinary officer cover the cluster.

Why 900 head is conservative

Pugu alone moves 600–1,000 cattle and 400–500 goats per session. A weekly-session secondary market clearing 900 cattle a month is roughly a quarter of one large market's throughput — we are modelling partial capture, not displacement.

What kills a node

Broker capture. If the incumbent brokers at a market organise against listing, throughput never matures. Mitigation is to recruit the largest three brokers at each node as licensed MNADA agents on commission share — the model co-opts the middleman rather than removing him.

07

What the bank gets that is not equity upside

This is the part that distinguishes MNADA from a marketplace app. You are not primarily buying a share of our commission. You are buying an origination channel into a secured asset class that does not currently exist on anyone's balance sheet, plus the settlement float that runs through it.

— Annual loan originations by Year 5, secured on Livestock Deposit Receipts Feedlot finance, trader working capital, and buyer inventory lines — each tied to a graded lot in custody.
— Average outstanding book at 45-day tenor Short, self-liquidating, and repaid out of a settlement the exchange controls end-to-end.
— Indicative annual net interest income at a 10% net margin Illustrative only — the bank's own pricing, provisioning and cost of funds govern.
— Average escrow deposits held at the settlement bank Two days of GMV in transit at any time, in a designated trust account.

Repayment sits inside the rail

A conventional livestock loan is repaid when the borrower chooses to come back. An LDR-secured loan is repaid at settlement, automatically, before the seller's proceeds are released. The bank is never chasing a herd across a district.

Valuation stops being a guess

Every settled trade feeds a published price index by grade and node. After twelve months the bank has a defensible mark for any lot in custody — which is what a credit committee needs before it will write the first shilling.

Exclusivity and first refusal

The funding bank takes settlement-bank exclusivity for 60 months and right of first refusal on all LDR-secured lending originated on the platform. Other lenders join the panel only on terms the anchor declines.

A portfolio that is not correlated to your existing book

Livestock credit performance tracks rainfall and disease, not the urban salary and trade cycles that dominate a Tanzanian bank's current exposure. This is diversification, priced as growth.

08

What could break this, and what we have done about it

Presented as a credit committee would want it: the risk, our honest assessment of severity, and the specific structural mitigation rather than an assurance.

Regulatory — highLicensing an exchange for live animals

No Tanzanian licence category maps cleanly to a livestock exchange. Mitigation: we do not seek one at launch. Phase 1 operates as a licensed livestock market operator and agent under existing LGA arrangements, with commodity trading conducted through TMX under its existing CMSA licence rather than in competition with it. Exchange licensing is a Phase 3 objective with 24 months of operating record behind the application.

Behavioural — highBroker capture at market level

Incumbent brokers control access at most secondary markets and lose margin if price becomes public. Mitigation: licensed-agent scheme. The top brokers at each node are recruited onto commission share and given the tablet. Their income rises with volume; it no longer depends on information asymmetry.

Operational — highDisease outbreak closes a corridor

Foot-and-mouth or Rift Valley fever quarantine can shut movement across whole regions for months. Mitigation: geographic diversification across four independent corridors by Year 2, and traceability that lets authorities quarantine at ward rather than region level — which shortens closures for everyone and is a direct argument for Ministry support.

Credit — mediumMortality and shrinkage in custody

The collateral can die. Mitigation: custody only at licensed holding grounds carrying mandatory mortality cover; LDRs are issued at 80% of assessed value; the insurer, not the lender, carries first loss. Weekly custodian reconciliation against tag reads.

Commercial — mediumA state platform makes us redundant

Government has launched e-Kilimo and Kilimo BaNDO for crops and could extend to livestock. Mitigation: build for integration, not competition. Our identity layer is the Ministry's own statutory registry, and we will contract to operate it. The state platform that owns the register is our best distribution partner and our hardest competitor — the choice of which is made in the first twelve months.

Financial — mediumTake rate compresses

A competitor undercuts 1.5%. Mitigation: the commission is the smallest of four revenue lines by Year 5 in relative growth terms, and the credit, custody and data lines are defended by the registry relationship rather than by price. Drag the commission slider in §06 to 0.90% and the business still reaches EBITDA breakeven in Year 4 — but peak cash deficit rises to roughly TZS 7.0bn, above this facility. At sustained sub-1% pricing we would return for a second tranche; we would rather state that now than discover it in Year 3.

The ask

TZS 5.5 billion over 36 months. Structured as TZS 3.5bn development and working capital, TZS 1.0bn equipment capex, and TZS 1.0bn ring-fenced as a settlement guarantee fund that never leaves the bank.

Base case shows a peak cumulative cash deficit of roughly TZS 3.4bn in Year 2 and cumulative cash positive during Year 4. The facility is sized at 1.6× peak deficit so that a twelve-month delay in node maturity does not require a second raise.

Milestones to first drawdown and beyond

  • M0–M3Registry MoU. Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries agreement to operate the statutory identification and traceability registry. This is the gating condition — no MoU, no drawdown.
  • M3–M6Four nodes live on the Shinyanga–Dodoma–Dar corridor. Grading standard published. First 5,000 animals tagged.
  • M6–M9First LDR issued and financed. Single-lender pilot, 200 head, 45-day feedlot facility. This is the proof the whole memorandum turns on.
  • M9–M1214 nodes live. Price index published weekly by grade. TMX integration for the crop side operational for the sesame season.
  • M12–M18Export gate. Traceability certification accepted at one border post or port, unlocking the Comoros and Gulf live-animal channels.
  • M18–M2440 nodes, four corridors. Second-lender panel opens. Exchange licence application filed with 24 months of settled-price record behind it.
