# Pilot Facility — Indicative Term Sheet

**Livestock inventory finance secured on a Livestock Deposit Receipt**
Milestone M6–M9 · MNADA Exchange · Draft 1 · August 2026

> **NON-BINDING.** This term sheet is an indicative summary for discussion. It creates no obligation on any party, is subject to the Lender's credit approval, satisfactory due diligence and executed documentation, and must be reviewed by Tanzanian counsel before it is relied on. Figures are illustrative and based on the MNADA index as at the date shown. Nothing here is a credit offer, a commitment to lend, or investment advice.

---

## 1. Why this transaction exists

Everything else in the MNADA memorandum is a claim. This is the test.

If a Tanzanian bank advances real money against 200 head of cattle it has never seen, secured only on an electronic document of title, and is repaid on day 45 out of a settlement the exchange controlled end to end — then livestock is a bankable asset class in Tanzania and MNADA owns the rails. If it fails, no amount of platform, map or projection matters.

**One lender. One lot. Forty-five days.** Deliberately small enough that failure is survivable and large enough that success is evidence.

## 5. Security

1. **First-ranking pledge over the Livestock Deposit Receipt** covering the lot. The LDR is an electronic document of title held in the MNADA registry and flagged to the Lender's order. MNADA is contractually unable to transfer, split or release it without the Lender's release instruction.
2. **Tripartite Collateral Management Agreement** between Lender, Borrower and MNADA, under which MNADA acknowledges it holds the LDR to the Lender's order and owes the Lender direct reporting duties.
3. **Custody Agreement** with the Custodian, acknowledging the Lender's interest, granting inspection rights, and waiving any lien over the lot ranking ahead of the Lender other than accrued feed and care charges as scheduled in §6.
4. **Assignment of the mortality insurance policy**, Lender noted as loss payee.
5. **Irrevocable settlement instruction** directing 100% of gross sale proceeds into the escrow account held at the Lender, applied per the waterfall in §6. The Borrower cannot intercept proceeds.
6. **Personal guarantee** of the Borrower's principal, limited to TZS [—].

**The structural point:** repayment does not depend on the Borrower choosing to repay. It happens inside the settlement rail, before the Borrower sees any money.

## 6. Settlement waterfall

Gross sale proceeds are applied in this order:

| | Application | Illustrative |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Statutory levies and market cess (1.5%) | TZS 2,700,000 |
| 2 | Custodian feed and care, capped at TZS 2,200/head/day | TZS 19,800,000 |
| 3 | **Lender — principal, interest and fees** | **TZS 120,327,671** |
| 4 | MNADA exchange commission (1.5%) and collateral management fee | *waived for pilot* |
| 5 | Residual to Borrower | balance |

Custody ranks ahead of the Lender because unpaid custodians do not release animals; a lien fight destroys more value than the ranking costs. **MNADA ranks behind the Lender in all cases** — the platform is not paid until the bank is whole.

## 7. Conditions precedent

No drawdown until every one of these is satisfied:

- **CP1** — Registry MoU between MNADA and the Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries executed and in force.
- **CP2** — Custodian holding-ground licence current; site inspected by the Lender and by MNADA.
- **CP3** — All 200 head tagged, graded and photographed; tag register reconciled 100% against physical count by MNADA in the Lender's presence.
- **CP4** — Mortality insurance in force, Lender noted as loss payee, premium paid.
- **CP5** — Collateral Management Agreement, Custody Agreement and settlement instruction executed.
- **CP6** — Escrow account opened at the Lender in MNADA's name, designated as a trust account.
- **CP7** — Veterinary certification: lot free of notifiable disease; no movement restriction in force over the district.

## 9. Events of default

- Reconciliation shortfall exceeding 1% of head count, unexplained for 48 hours.
- Mortality exceeding the insured limit, or insurer repudiation.
- Lapse, suspension or revocation of the Custodian's licence.
- Movement of any animal off the holding ground without Lender consent.
- Failure to cure a margin call within 5 business days.
- Non-payment of any amount at maturity.
- Any attempt to transfer, pledge or encumber the LDR outside the registry.

## 10. Why the Lender is protected

The number that matters is not the advance rate. It is the price at which the Lender stops being whole.

Working back through the waterfall, gross realisation must cover cess, custody and the Lender's claim:

```
G × (1 − 0.015) − 19,800,000 ≥ 120,327,671
G ≥ TZS 142,261,595   →   TZS 711,000 per head
```

| | Per head | vs break-even |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lender break-even realisation | TZS 711,000 | — |
| Entry index at drawdown | TZS 742,000 | +4.4% |
| Expected exit after 45 days finishing | TZS 900,000 | +26.6% |

**The lot can realise 21% below its expected exit price, or 4.4% below the price it was valued at on day one, and the Lender is still repaid in full** — before touching the guarantee, the insurance or the margin-call mechanism. Mortality is insured, not borne. Price risk is capped by the coverage triggers. The residual exposure is operational: does the reconciliation hold, and does the settlement rail execute.

That is what the pilot is testing.

## 11. Success criteria

The pilot passes only if **all five** are met. Anything less is a data point, not a proof.

| | Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100% tag reconciliation at maturity | The register is real, not a spreadsheet |
| 2 | Mortality within insured tolerance (≤2%) | Custody standard is adequate |
| 3 | Full repayment at or before day 45 | The instrument performs |
| 4 | Settlement executed through escrow with no manual intervention | The rail works without heroics |
| 5 | **Realised price within ±5% of the MNADA index at drawdown** | **The index is a valid collateral mark** |

Criterion 5 is the one that changes the Lender's business. The other four prove one loan was repaid. Criterion 5 proves the bank can value any lot in custody without inspecting it — which is the difference between a pilot and an asset class.

### Notes on the numbers

- Index values are MNADA's own modelled figures, consistent with `MNADA — Bank Pitch Memo.md` and the demo app: TZ-C-M2-300-350-BCS3 at TZS 742,000.
- The 16% interest rate is an illustration only. The Lender sets pricing from its own cost of funds and provisioning.
- Custodian charge of TZS 2,200/head/day and the expected exit of TZS 900,000/head are assumptions, not quotations. Both must be replaced with real quotes from the named Custodian and a real forward indication from a named buyer before this goes to credit.
- **Before this document is sent to any bank it requires review by Tanzanian counsel**, particularly on whether an electronic LDR is enforceable as a document of title under current Tanzanian law without amendment to the Warehouse Receipts framework. That question is the single largest legal risk in the structure and it is not resolved by this term sheet.
