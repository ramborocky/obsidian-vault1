## The three findings behind the rewrite

**1 · The Foundation now investigates provenance by reading drafts.** Your git history is the strongest authorship evidence most writers could produce — and an AI draft of the same story sits beside the manuscript in a public repository. Both true at once. → [[Provenance — The AI Question and the Public Repo]]

**2 · The ranking is backwards.** Morland is ~47× the Commonwealth per application (£90 vs £1.92) **and pays £1,500 a month for twelve months** — more than the entire household-and-debt figure. It is not a prize, it is a salaried commission with a delivery schedule. → [[Unknown Unknowns — The Writing Side]]

**3 · Two things I got wrong before checking.** The vault already knew the KDP 30% US withholding in full detail, and already knew the 10,000-words-a-month undertaking and asks the right question about it. Both were ahead of me. → [[KDP Business Plan]] · [[Morland — What They Actually Fund]]
