### £2,000 for a single poem. **Closes 31 August — the soonest deadline you have.**

→ [[08 The Opportunities Board]]

| | |

| Runner-up | £1,000 |

| **Judge** | Rowan Ricardo Phillips |

## THE DETAILS

- **Window:** 1 May – **31 August 2026**, midnight UTC
- **Fee: £10 per poem.** No limit on how many you enter — each poem a separate file
- **Length:** maximum 60 lines, excluding titles, epigraphs and blank lines
- **Format:** PDF only, via Submittable
- **Blind review** — no name or identifying detail in the file
- **Open worldwide**, 18 and over
- Poems must be **original and unpublished** — print or online, including blogs and social media
- **Must not be generated with the aid of AI tools**

---

## THE VERDICT

**You are eligible. That is not the same as this being for you.**

Two things sit against it. It is **poetry**, and you are a prose writer with no body of poems waiting. And it carries a **£10 fee per poem** — the only paid entry on the whole board, against a standing rule that says never pay an entry fee expecting a return.

**£10 buys one lottery ticket in an open worldwide field judged by a major poet.** That is not a scandal; it is just not a plan. If you have a poem you already believe in, enter it and think no more about it. If you would be writing one *for* the deadline, in the same month the Morland application is being built, the answer is no.

> **Decide by 20 August, and then stop thinking about it either way.** An undecided call eats more time than a submitted one.
