## ⚠️ THE NEWS THAT CHANGES THE PLAN

**The Commonwealth Short Story Prize is postponed.** The official page (checked today) says the prize is closed and will return in **January 2027** after a review of its processes, entry rules and guidelines. The usual Sept–1 Nov window is not happening this year. Rules may change in the review: re-read them when it reopens. Still listed: 2,000–5,000 words, Commonwealth citizens 18+, **Swahili accepted**.

What this means for Kalemie: the deadline pressure is off for now. The re-opened rules must be checked before any Chantal draft is shaped to them.
