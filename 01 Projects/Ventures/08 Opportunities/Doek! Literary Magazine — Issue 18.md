# 🇳🇦 DOEK! LITERARY MAGAZINE — ISSUE 18
### **Read the eligibility rule before anything else. Fiction is closed to you.**

→ [[08 The Opportunities Board]]

---

## ⛔ THE ELIGIBILITY SPLIT — the whole point of this note

| Category | Who may submit |
| --- | --- |
| **Fiction** | **Namibian citizens only** — in Namibia or the diaspora — plus foreign nationals permanently resident in Namibia |
| **Poetry** | **Same restriction** |
| **Nonfiction** | ✅ **African writers and people of African descent**, continent or diaspora |
| **Visual art** | ✅ Same as nonfiction |

**You are Tanzanian. The fiction door is shut and the nonfiction door is open.**

This is the kind of call that costs a writer a month: it reads as a Pan-African magazine building continental work, and the fiction restriction sits three paragraphs down. **Submitting a story here would be dead on arrival.**

---

## WHAT THEY WANT

Founded in Windhoek, Namibia. Building a Pan-African publication that establishes Namibian writing while showcasing continental work. Fiction, poetry, nonfiction and visual art.

**Nonfiction — your category:**
- **1,500 – 4,500 words**
- Times New Roman, 12pt, **double-spaced**
- **No identifying details in the document**
- One piece per window
- **Work produced with AI software will not be considered**
- Simultaneous submissions allowed, but must be retracted if accepted elsewhere
- 18 or older
- Previously published in Doek!? Wait six months
- *"Work directed primarily at the white gaze is discouraged"*

**Window:** 1 June – **30 September 2026**

---

## THE VERDICT

**A real door, in a category you have not used yet.**

No money, but a Namibian literary magazine with a Pan-African reach is a genuine byline, and nonfiction is a form you have material for — the lake, the railway town, the accountancy, the trade. It also builds the kind of publication history that serious grants ask for.

**Two things to be clear-eyed about.** It is unpaid, and being free to read online it does **not** create Morland eligibility — that gate needs work offered for sale, and Kindle is the only online medium the MMF accepts. So this is a credential, not a shortcut. → [[Morland 2026 — The Campaign]]

And it wants 1,500–4,500 words of nonfiction written by hand, in the same eight weeks as the Morland application. **Decide honestly whether that fits, and drop it formally if it does not.** A call left hanging is worse than a call declined.
