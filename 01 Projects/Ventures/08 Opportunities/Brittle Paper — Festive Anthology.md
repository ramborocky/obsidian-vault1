# 🎄 BRITTLE PAPER — FESTIVE ANTHOLOGY
### Free, open, by email. **Closes 14 November.** The theme is doing the work.

→ [[08 The Opportunities Board]]

---

## THE CALL

Brittle Paper's annual festive anthology. This year's theme: **the stories of those who spend the holidays away from "home."**

New home, studying abroad, on vacation, or diaspora building a life elsewhere. They want the whole emotional range — *"sometimes it's longing and grief, sometimes it's joy and awe, and sometimes it's a combination of it all."* Explicitly **not limited to Christmas**: any festive celebration, religious holiday, cultural moment, wedding, birthday, milestone. The thing that makes it work is that the moment **feels different away from home, country, family and continent.**

## THE VERDICT

**The most straightforwardly usable call in the document.** Free, open to you, email-based, a form you actually write, and a theme with real pressure in it.

**And the theme is not a chore — it is your material at an angle you have not written from.** Departure, the railway, the bus stage, the person who left and the family that stayed: this vault is full of arrival and staying. *Away from home during the festival* turns that around. Note it wants the **feeling of difference**, not a travel story.

**3,500 words is the ceiling**, which is tighter than you usually run. That is a discipline, not a problem — the Barrett base is the right one to read beside it.

**What it costs you:** the piece must be original and unpublished, and Brittle Paper is free to read, so publishing there does not create Morland eligibility — that gate needs work **offered for sale**, and Kindle is the only online medium the MMF accepts. This is a byline, and a good one. → [[Morland 2026 — The Campaign]]

**Timing is kind.** Morland closes 22 September, the other November deadline is the 1st. This one lands after both.
