## THE THREE THINGS THIS DOCUMENT ACTUALLY TELLS YOU

**1 · Doek! is a trap, and it is worth reading twice.**
It looks like an open Pan-African fiction call. It is not. **Fiction and poetry are restricted to Namibian citizens and residents.** Only **nonfiction** and visual art are open to African writers generally. Submit fiction there and it is dead on arrival. → [[Doek! Literary Magazine — Issue 18]]

**2 · None of these solve the Morland gate.**
The Morland sample must be **published and offered for sale**, and the FAQ allows exactly one online medium: **Kindle**. Brittle Paper and Doek! are free to read, so publishing in either creates a byline but **not eligibility**. The Kindle route stays the only route. → [[Morland 2026 — The Campaign]]

**3 · Three of the five are poetry.**
Oxford and Sillerman are poetry outright; Brittle Paper takes poetry among other things. **You are a prose writer, and the only paid entry on this page is a poetry competition.** The Board's standing rule applies: never pay an entry fee expecting a return. → [[Prize Portfolio]]
