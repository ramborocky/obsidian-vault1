# 06 · THE HOUSE
### An unfinished building is not an asset. It is a hole you pour money into until it becomes one.

→ back to [[00 The Board|The Board]] · builds on [[House - Airbnb Writers Retreat]]

---

## THE VERDICT FIRST

> **Park this until the debt is cleared — unless finishing it costs very little.**

This is the **most capital-hungry track on the board** and you are servicing debt at up to **4% a month.** Every shilling spent on plaster is a shilling not killing a 48%-a-year loan. **Borrowing to finish it would be the single worst financial move available to you.**

That is not "no." It is **not yet, and not on credit.** → [[Debt First]]

**The exception that would change this:** if the house is nearly finished and the gap is small — a few hundred thousand shillings of paint, a door, a water connection — then it converts to income fast and the arithmetic flips. **You do not currently know which situation you are in**, and that is the first thing to fix.

---

## WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DON'T KNOW

Everything below is a model. It becomes a plan only once these are answered — and **none of them need money, only a notebook and an afternoon.**

- [ ] **Where is it?** Location drives every number in hospitality. A house near a tourist route and a house in a residential quarter are different businesses
- [ ] **What exactly is unfinished?** Room by room: walls, floors, roof, doors, windows, plumbing, electrical, water, sanitation
- [ ] **What does finishing cost?** One quote for the top-priority item, not a guess
- [ ] **Is the title clean and in your name?**
- [ ] **What do comparable places charge locally** — per night, and per month for long-term?

**The existing note's Day 1 task — walk the property and write down every unfinished item — is still the right first move and still undone.**

## THE THREE THINGS IT COULD BE

### A · Long-term rental — **the one that fits your situation**

**Least capital, least work, fastest cash, no licence.** A long-term tenant does not need a furnished room, matching towels, a listing, photographs, a cleaner, or a tourism licence. They need it to be safe, dry, lockable and plumbed.

- **Finishing standard: much lower.** Furnishing: often none
- **Income: lower per month, but it starts sooner and costs a fraction to reach**
- **No TALA licence, no levy, no platform fees, no reviews, no operations**
- **You have a full-time job.** This is the only option that does not require daily attention

> **If the goal is money rather than a project, this is almost certainly the answer**, and it is the option the original note doesn't consider.

### B · Airbnb / short-term rental

Higher revenue per night, **and a real business** with daily operations attached.

**The regulation is not optional.** Short-term letting in Tanzania is treated as **regulated tourist accommodation**, not casual private rental:

- **TALA licence** — issued by the Tanzania Tourism Licensing Authority under the Ministry of Natural Resources and Tourism. Home stays are a licensable category. **Operating without it is illegal**
- **Tourism Development Levy — 1% per bed-night** on the mainland
- Plus local council permits, and TRA registration

**Market reference points:** national average occupancy around **46%**; Zanzibar city runs about **48% occupancy at ~$76 a night** across ~1,794 listings. **Mainland residential areas will be well below the Zanzibar rate** — do not plan on $76 unless you are on the coast.

#### A worked model — **assumptions, not forecasts**

```
ADR (modest mainland)          $40/night
Occupancy                      45%
Gross monthly    30 × 0.45 × $40   =  $540    = TSh 1,429,000

Less running costs (~40%):
  cleaning, laundry, utilities, restocking,
  platform fee, TDL, maintenance, vacancies
Net monthly                        ≈ $324    = TSh   858,000
```

**Every number above is invented for illustration.** Replace all of them with real local comparables before treating this as anything. And note the second line especially: **occupancy is the number that kills small operators** — 45% means the place is empty most of the month while the costs continue.

### C · Writers' retreat

**Be clear-eyed: this is a marketing skin on option B, not a different business.** Same licence, same levy, same operations, same nightly rate.

**What it buys:** differentiation. A generic listing competes on price; a themed one competes on story, and you can genuinely write that story — *"I finished a prize-shortlisted story in this room"* is a real hook if it becomes true.

**What it costs:** almost nothing. Character furnishing is cheaper than hotel-grade matching.

**What it will not do:** produce demand on its own. The retreat market in Tanzania is thin, seasonal and dependent on a network you do not yet have. **Brand it as a retreat, but underwrite it on ordinary Airbnb demand.** If the numbers only work with retreat guests, the numbers do not work.

## THE HONEST COMPARISON

| | Capital to start | Monthly work | Licence | Income |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A · Long-term rent** | **Lowest** | Almost none | No | Lowest, but **soonest and safest** |
| **B · Airbnb** | High — finish + furnish | Daily | **TALA, mandatory** | Higher, volatile, ~46% occupancy |
| **C · Retreat** | High — same as B | Daily + marketing | Same as B | Same as B plus a thinner niche |

**Against everything else on the board:** the freelance track reaches **$800–1,500/month within 4–12 weeks for roughly zero capital.** This house needs an unknown but certainly large sum before it earns a shilling. **On capital efficiency it comes last**, and that is why it is parked rather than dropped.

---

## THE SEQUENCE, IF AND WHEN

1. **Walk it. Write down everything unfinished.** Free, this week
2. **One quote** for the highest-priority item — that single number decides everything
3. **Check the title** is clean and yours
4. **Price the local market** — 3–5 comparables, nightly and monthly
5. **Then choose A or B**, on the numbers, not on which is more interesting
6. **Finish with cash, never credit.** At 4% a month, borrowing to renovate is how a house becomes a liability
7. Only if going short-term: **TALA licence before the first guest**, not after
