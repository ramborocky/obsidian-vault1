# 🗺️ ATLAS — THE BEAT OF MY HURT, ANNOTATED

## 0.3% · "There are eleven beats for the dead and me, I know nine."

`#atlas/kiteley` `#atlas/barrett` `#atlas/mckee`

- [[Point of View — The Reluctant I]] — a first person who tells you at once what he does *not* have. The missing two beats are the story.
- [[The Plot-Germ and the One-Sentence Test]] — Barrett's germ, in the first sentence.
- [[The Inciting Incident]] — technically at 25.9%, but **the want is declared here** and everything obeys it.
- ⚠️ *and me* leaves the clause without a clean parse. Flagged, unresolved — see [[The NAPLAN Floor — Ten Mechanical Criteria]].

## 5.0% · The goat skin

> *cow gives you the note, goat gives you the note and the thing underneath it*

`#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/barrett`

- [[Verisimilitude, Not Verity — The Use of Facts]] — Barrett's rule exactly: the *feel* of expertise, built from three facts.
- **The competence hypothesis, answered.** [[02 Comparison Board]] scored three stories `N` on *does the narrator know how to do something*. **This is the first clear `Y` in the vault** — a narrator shown at work, in procedure.

## 9.8% · Beat four described — the plant

> *You put it late. So late that a trained man would call it a mistake… forty people break at once, because you have built them somewhere to break.*

`#atlas/cleland`

- [[Reveal Answers Slowly]] — **planted here, detonated at 50.0%.** A 40-point gap. See [[Atlas — Plant and Detonate]].
- The reader is taught the mechanism *before* learning it was used on the narrator's own father. That order is the entire design.

## 10.9% · "That moment is the best thing that happens to me in a week." ⭐

> *I have had that feeling over a child. I had it, and then I ate the food, and then I slept.*

`#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/kiteley`

- [[What You Are Censoring]] — **the inversion.** Where most writers censor, this one says it at 11% and does not soften it after.
- [[Three Faults of Soul — Sentimentality, Frigidity, Mannerism]] — the exact opposite of sentimentality: the feeling reported at cost to the person reporting it.
- **Gate 3 · ugly honesty 5/5** and dangerous ingredient 5/5, both from this passage. See [[08 Three Gates — Field Comparison]].

## 14.8% · Stima and Giza

> *at seven the délestage takes us and their generators start in the same second*

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/barrett`

- [[Setting and Genre]] — the class engine, same function as Chantal's ghost kids at 13.3%. **Both stories install class at almost exactly the same position.**
- [[The Unities in Miniature]] — one town, one trade, one week. Barrett obeyed, unlike Baldwin.

## 25.9% – 28.1% · The Ngoie boy, and "beat four"

> *He is twenty-four years old. / He was, said the man.*

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/cleland`

- [[The Inciting Incident]] — the request that cannot be refused.
- [[Scene Design]] — turns on two words: *He was.*
- [[Illuminate Fear and Dread]] — *Down the hill a woman was calling her children in, one at a time, by name. There were three. The third did not come.* Dread placed entirely outside the scene.

## 35.6% – 40.8% · Sunday, and playing four

> *I played four. I put the lift where I put it. The room went at about eight seconds… The mother did not move.*

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Scene Design]] — the turn is the negative: forty people break, **one does not.**
- [[Show, Don't Tell — Needless Explanation]] — the mother's knowledge conveyed by her stillness alone.

## 44.9% · "And my hands were still going." ⭐

`#atlas/gardner`

- [[Psychic Distance]] — **the slide begins.** The narration stops reporting and starts being carried.
- [[Emotion as Movement]] — Kiteley's principle literalised: the emotion arrives as a physical motion he did not authorise.
- **Gate 3 · the slide 5/5.** The reader is inside it before noticing.

## 50.0% · "I was six. Our own yard." ⭐⭐

> *There was a cloth over my father from the chest down, and nobody was looking at the cloth.*

`#atlas/cleland` `#atlas/learning-curve` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Reveal Answers Slowly]] — **the detonation.** Planted at 9.8%, fired at 50.0%.
- [[05 The Late Reframe]] — everything before this is re-read. The uncle is not a mentor; he is the man who sold the lie.
- [[Working Backward from the Climax — the Victim-Story Trap]] — Gardner's trap **avoided**: Ilunga is not a victim of this, he is its inheritor and its practitioner.
- *nobody was looking at the cloth* is a verbatim callback to 37.3%. The same sentence, two funerals, twenty years apart.

## 50.5% · "Somebody had been paid for mine."

`#atlas/mckee`

- [[Controlling Idea]] — **the whole story in six words.** *The craft that comforts is the craft that lies.*
- [[Whisper Dont Shout]] — no exclamation, no elaboration, its own paragraph.

## 55.6% · "And nothing happened to me." — the crisis

> *I waited for the thing I have been paid in since I was eleven… and I felt nothing at all… That the wages stopped.*

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Crisis, Climax, Resolution]] — **the true crisis, and it is an absence.** He loses the only thing the job paid him in.
- [[The Fichtean Curve]] — the lowest point, and it arrives without an event.

## 58.5% – 61.5% · Four days, then the water point

> *He was two metres from me and I had four days of it in my mouth and I did not*

`#atlas/gardner`

- [[Delay and the Anguish of Choice]] — **Gardner's principle, executed.** The question is askable, the man is present, and the narrator chooses not to.
- *my mother has one son and one table and a tin, and that the answer takes one of those away from her.* Arithmetic as the reason — the same instrument Chantal uses at 82.8%.

## 68.9% – 73.3% · The seventh night, started wrong

> *My hands knew the thing and I did not, and there is a distance between those two facts that you have to cross with nobody helping you.*

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Act Design]] — third act opens on public failure. *Somebody in the yard laughed and stopped.*
- [[Interest, Free Will, and the Active Protagonist]] — he tries, alone, and fails alone. Necessary before the help lands.

## 75.9% · "And then, underneath me, the small drum came in." ⭐⭐

> *He put it down in front of me the way you put a tool in a boy's hand, without ceremony, expecting it to be used.*

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Crisis, Climax, Resolution]] — **the climax**, and it is given to the *other* man.
- ⭐ **This is the Baldwin structure.** [[Baldwin — Sonny's Blues|Sonny's Blues]] gives the competence to the man being watched, not the man narrating. **The Beat already does it here** — the uncle plays; Ilunga follows. The sheet's worry that Ilunga holds both jobs is answered by this paragraph.
- [[Character Dimension and Cast Design]] — the uncle's entire character in one gesture, no dialogue.

## 85.5% – 92.4% · The seeing, and the mother standing

> *I do not know what he is. I know what he took.* / *She was looking at the door, and the road, and the place where three men had stood and had not eaten.*

`#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/kiteley`

- [[Psychic Distance]] — closest point in the story. *Not remembered. Saw.*
- [[Writing That Learns, Not Teaches]] — *I do not know what he is* — the narrator refuses the verdict. **Compare Giorgis, who hands it down.**
- [[Atlas — The Withheld Fact]] — what killed the Ngoie boy is never stated. The mother's eyeline is the whole answer.

## 94.5% – 99.7% · The close

> *Rule one. The drum is not sad. You are not sad. The people are sad and you are working.*
> *She did not ask me why I was warming a drum I was not going to play.*

`#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/kiteley` `#atlas/barrett`

- [[The Resonant Close]] — Rule one, quoted at 1.5% and again here, meaning the opposite.
- [[Internal Structure — Shapes for an Ending]] — **a circle.** Fire in the yard at 4%, same fire at 99%.
- [[Climax and Conclusion — The Story in Six Words]] — climax at 92.4%, ending seven points later. Barrett satisfied.
- The mother in the doorway at 6.5% and at 99.7%. **Never named, in the whole story.**

## What the map shows

**The plant-to-detonation gap is 40 points (9.8% → 50.0%)**, against Baldwin's 44. The earlier sheet said the proportions were wrong. **Measured against Draft 4, they are not** — that critique was written against an older draft and is now stale.

**The climax is handed to the uncle at 75.9%**, which the sheet recommended as a fix and which the draft already does.

**Two `⭐⭐` beats, at 50% and 76%** — dead centre and three-quarters. That is the Fichtean shape without anyone drawing it.
