# 💣 ATLAS — PLANT AND DETONATE

> A fact laid down flat and early, left to be **misfiled**, then fired late enough that the reader re-reads everything before it.
> The opposite technique to [[Atlas — The Withheld Fact]] — this is what *does* get released, and when.

`#atlas/cleland` `#atlas/learning-curve`

**The concept lives in:** [[Reveal Answers Slowly]] and [[Add Surprise Sparingly]] (Cleland) · [[05 The Late Reframe]] · [[Inevitable but Unforeseen — Suspense and Surprise]] (Barrett)

## The measurements

| Story | Plant | Detonate | Gap | What |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --- |
| [[Baldwin — Sonny's Blues\|**Baldwin**]] | **20%** | **64%** | **44** | Grace — one clause, no name, no weight |
| [[Uwazuruike — Looking for Papa\|**Uwazuruike**]] | 52.4% | 94.5% | 42 | *"He had been at the church choir meeting, **he said**."* Two words carry the story |
| [[The Beat of My Hurt (Draft 4 — Free)\|**Beat**]] | 9.8% | **50.0%** | **40** | Beat four's mechanism, taught before you learn it was used on his father |
| [[Giorgis — A Double-Edged Inheritance\|**Giorgis**]] | 46.4% | 83.4% | 37 | A death in a subordinate clause, converted to murder by a note |
| [[2026-07-28 — Chantal (Draft 3, Prize Pass)\|**Chantal**]] | 27.6% | **96.2%** | **69** | The motherboard → the fob under the third bucket |

**The band is 37–44 points, and four of five sit inside it.** Chantal's 69 is the outlier and works because the motherboard is an object the reader keeps seeing, not a fact stated once.

## The rule that separates a plant from a mention

> **A plant is not a mention. The reader has to *file it wrong* first.**

| | Does it work? | Why |
| --- | :--: | --- |
| **Baldwin** — Grace named in passing, letter mentions her, story moves on for 44% | ✅ | The reader files her as background. **She is given a name and no weight** |
| **Uwazuruike** — *he said* | ✅ | Six words that read as attribution and are actually a father's alibi |
| **Beat** — the mechanism of beat four | ✅ | Filed as trade knowledge. It is a confession |
| ⚠️ **Beat's father** | ❌ | Mentioned **once**, at 1.5%. Nothing to misfile. Widest raw gap on the board, weakest effect |

**The fix for the last row is one sentence:** mention the father's funeral flatly and early, with no weight, and let it sit. That is the whole of what Baldwin does with Grace.

## The late reframe — a narrower thing

[[05 The Late Reframe]] is the special case where **the reader assembles the meaning before the narrator states it**, at around 85%.

| Story | % | The move |
| --- | --: | --- |
| **Uwazuruike** | **86.0** | Madam Nkechi's tone — *no joy or relief… a hint of disapproval, even disdain* |
| **Chantal** | **85.1** | The washed clothes, done with something after the soap |

**Two writers, no connection, the same technique within one percentage point of each other.** That is the strongest independent validation of anything in this vault — and it is why [[05 The Late Reframe]] keeps its one-device constraint. Used twice in a story it becomes a tell.

---

## Building one

1. **Choose the fact whose meaning will change**, not the fact itself. Grace does not change; what her death *meant to the narrator* changes.
2. **Plant it in a subordinate position** — a clause, an aside, an attribution. Never its own sentence, never its own paragraph.
3. **Give it a name and no weight.** Named things get filed. Weighted things get watched.
4. **Wait 40 points.** Below 30 it reads as forgetfulness; the reader has not had time to build the wrong version.
5. **Detonate with detail, not announcement.** Baldwin gives the fever, the fall, the silence. He does not write *and then I understood*.
