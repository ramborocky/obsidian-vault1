# 🔗 ATLAS — CONCORDANCE

> **Five theorists, writing decades apart with no knowledge of each other, keep arriving at the same laws under different names.**
> The other Atlas maps pin concepts to *stories*. This one pins concepts to *each other* — where McKee, Gardner, Cleland, Barrett and Kiteley are saying the same thing, and where Learning Curve is doing something genuinely different from all five.

**Same rule as the rest of this folder: nothing below was built by editing a base note.** Every link here is outbound only. Open any linked note and its Backlinks pane now shows this page — that's the whole mechanism.

## 1 · Show, don't tell — the same law in five vocabularies

| Base | Note | What it calls the law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gardner | [[Show, Don't Tell — Needless Explanation]] | The Craine rewrite — drama vs. explanation |
| Barrett (1900) | [[Methods of Narration — The Author Must Vanish]] | Authorial invisibility |
| Learning Curve | [[The De-Slop Pass — English Prose]] | The banned-word list, `#labeling-words` |
| Gardner | [[Vocabulary and the Buried Symbol]] | Meaning displaced into the object |
| Learning Curve | [[All the Small Things — Grief Through the Minor Object]] | Grief through the minor object |

**The pattern:** Barrett states it first, in 1900, as a rule about the author disappearing. Gardner restates it 80 years later as a rule about drama displacing abstraction. Learning Curve turns both into a mechanical checklist. Same law, three different centuries of vocabulary — which is itself evidence it's a real law and not a style preference. Ties directly to the Writing Constitution: *"Objects carry emotion."*

## 9 · Motifs — the section that isn't built yet

`01 Projects/Kalemie/04 Themes & Symbols/` holds seven notes — [[Entropy]], [[Gravity]], [[Luxury]], [[Memory]], [[Mirrors]], [[Rust]], [[Water]] — and **all seven are still the blank template**: no meaning, no first/last appearance, no associated character filled in. This concordance can point at them, but can't fill them in without inventing what the story means, which isn't Claude's call to make.

The theory to fill them *with* is already sitting one section up: [[Vocabulary and the Buried Symbol]] and [[All the Small Things — Grief Through the Minor Object]]. Motifs are also meant to be cycle-reusable — see `Collection Tracker.md` — so whatever gets written into these seven notes should be written with [[The Beat of My Hurt]]'s Ilunga in mind too, not just Chantal.

## 10 · What doesn't converge — Learning Curve's different axis

Notes like [[The Funhouse Mirror — AI Output as Cliché Detector]], [[The ELIZA Effect and the Vestments of Plausibility]], and [[Creativity Amplification — Vinge's IA and What Survives of It]] don't have counterparts in the other four bases, and forcing one would be dishonest. McKee, Gardner, Cleland, Barrett and Kiteley are theories of *writing*. Learning Curve is two different things wearing one folder: a theory of *judging* (the rubric, the gates, the cold-read protocol) and a theory of *AI-authenticity detection*. That's not a gap in the concordance — it's the accurate shape of what this base actually is.
