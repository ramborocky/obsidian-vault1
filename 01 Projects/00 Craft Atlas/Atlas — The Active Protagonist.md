## Chantal's five acts — all against her own interest

| % | The act |
| --: | --- |
| 30.7 | **She lies.** *Rue Saint Michel* — instantly, without deciding to |
| 40.7 | **She takes the name.** Not given. Taken. *Chantal, I said.* |
| 52.1 | **She kisses him.** *So I kissed him.* Three words, its own paragraph |
| 69.9 | **She plays the pregnancy.** Shows the photograph, then says the line, having worked out on the way that a hit and run is worth nothing |
| 96.2 | **She hides the fob.** The last act, secret, costing her everything |

**Every one is a choice made against her own interest, and she narrates each without asking for sympathy.** That is why the draft scores 5/5 on ugly honesty at [[08 Three Gates — Field Comparison|Gate 3]] and 40/45 at Gate 2.

> [!important] Why the master draft scores 24
> [[Working Backward from the Climax — the Victim-Story Trap|Gardner's victim-story trap]] says a protagonist things merely *happen to* cannot carry a story.
>
> The master draft's Daudi observes the nails, the perfume, the fob, the ring, the vaccination scar — **and does nothing with any of it, ever.** The Dashboard's own high-priority item is *"Let Daudi make ONE active choice: He chooses NOT to act."*
>
> **The 24 and Gardner's warning are the same fact, once as a score and once as theory.**

## The Baldwin variation — the one worth stealing

**Baldwin gives the competence to the man being watched, not the man narrating.**

The narrator teaches algebra and is never once shown teaching it. But the final section contains real knowledge of how a band works — and it belongs to **Creole and Sonny**. The narrator's job is to fail to understand for thirty pages and then understand in one scene.

**The distance between the man who can play and the man who can only listen is the entire engine.**

> [!success] The Beat already does this, at 75.9%
> [[Baldwin — Sonny's Blues|The story sheet]] worried that *"Ilunga currently holds the drum and the grief — two jobs Baldwin deliberately split."*
>
> **[[Atlas — Beat of My Hurt (annotated)|The map shows that critique is stale.]]** At 75.9% the uncle comes in underneath and plays the tenth beat; Ilunga follows. The competence is handed to the watched man at the climax, exactly as Baldwin does it. The sheet was written against an earlier draft.

## The operational rule

**Acting is not the same as doing something.** Found Things' Daudi hands over the fob — a real act — and it changes nothing, arrives too late, and costs him nothing anybody can see. **It still counts**, because the story knows it counts for little and says so.

What does *not* count: noticing, remembering, feeling, understanding, deciding to decide later. The master draft is made of those.

> **Test for any draft: list every sentence where the protagonist does something that changes what happens next. If the list is empty, no amount of prose will save it.**
