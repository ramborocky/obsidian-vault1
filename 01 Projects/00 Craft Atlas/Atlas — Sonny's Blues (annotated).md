# 🗺️ ATLAS — SONNY'S BLUES, ANNOTATED

> James Baldwin, 1957 · the benchmark, **44 / 45**
> Positions from [[Baldwin — Sonny's Blues|the story sheet]], measured against the source pagination. Technique is described rather than quoted — the text is under copyright and the sheet holds the citations.

`#atlas/baldwin`

---

## ~3% · The arrest, read in a newspaper

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/barrett`

- [[The Inciting Incident]] — delivered secondhand, in print, on a subway. The narrator learns about his brother the way a stranger would.
- [[Methods of Narration — The Author Must Vanish]] — the distance is the characterisation. He is already not close enough to have been told.

## ~20% · Grace, planted ⭐

`#atlas/cleland` `#atlas/learning-curve`

The narrator mentions in passing that he finally wrote to Sonny **just after his little girl died.** One clause. No name given, no detail, no weight. The returning letter names her, and the reader files it as background.

- [[Reveal Answers Slowly]] — **the plant, and the model for the whole technique.** See [[Atlas — Plant and Detonate]].
- [[Add Surprise Sparingly]] — Cleland's economy: one clause spent here buys 44 points of story.

## ~37% · The father's brother

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/gardner`

The mother tells how the father's brother was run down for sport by a car of white men who never stopped. She ends on an instruction, not a moral: hold on to your brother, let him know you're there.

- [[Controlling Idea]] — **stated as an instruction by a character**, never as narration. Compare Giorgis, who states hers in the narrator's voice and loses two axes for it.
- [[Delay and the Anguish of Choice]] — he promises. Two days later he marries and leaves, and forgets until her funeral. **Baldwin spends the rest of the story on the failure to keep it.**

## ~64% · Grace, detonated ⭐⭐

`#atlas/cleland` `#atlas/learning-curve` `#atlas/gardner`

The full account arrives: the fever that dropped, the fall, the silence that frightened Isabel more than a scream, the child unable to draw breath — and Isabel still waking from it years later.

- [[05 The Late Reframe]] — **the ceiling example. 44 points between plant and detonation.**
- [[The Vivid and Continuous Fictional Dream]] — held unbroken across the whole account.
- [[All the Small Things — Grief Through the Minor Object]] — the silence, not the death, is what carries it.

## ~65% · The hinge — five words ⭐⭐

> *My trouble made his real.*

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/kiteley`

- [[Controlling Idea]] — the whole story, in five words, **and it is placed one point after the detonation.** The order is the craft: the reader feels the trouble, then is given the sentence.
- [[Writing That Learns, Not Teaches]] — the narrator learns it on the page. He does not arrive knowing.
- **This is what Chantal's 82.8% is doing** — thesis in six words, at conversational volume, immediately after the crisis.

## ~80% · What the suffering actually is

`#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/kiteley`

Not the heroin. A storm inside that cannot be talked or loved out — and when you finally try to play it, nobody is listening. Sonny's conclusion is an instruction too: you have to find a way to listen.

- [[Interest, Free Will, and the Active Protagonist]] — **the competence belongs to the brother being watched**, never to the narrator.
- [[Point of View — The Reluctant I]] — thirty pages of a first person failing to understand, so that one scene of understanding lands.

## 88–100% · The bandstand

`#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/barrett`

Real knowledge of how a band works: Creole holding the players on a short rein then letting it out, the bass answering the drums, Creole waiting for Sonny to reach the water, then stepping back so Sonny speaks alone.

- [[The Resonant Close]] — every debt paid at once.
- [[Verisimilitude, Not Verity — The Use of Facts]] — somebody who has watched musicians work.
- [[Climax and Conclusion — The Story in Six Words]] — Barrett's rule obeyed at the very end of a story that breaks his unities everywhere else.

## Never released

**Whether Sonny stays clean.** Sonny forecloses it himself — *it can come again* — twice. Baldwin refuses to answer. See [[Atlas — The Withheld Fact]].

## What this map teaches the other two

| | Baldwin | Chantal | Beat |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| Inciting incident | 3% | **0.4%** | 25.9% |
| Plant → detonate | **20% → 64%** | 27.6% → 96.2% | 9.8% → 50.0% |
| Controlling idea stated | 65% | 82.8% | 50.5% |
| Climax | ~88% | 78.4% | 75.9% |

**All three state their controlling idea in one short sentence, immediately after the crisis, between 50% and 83%.** Nobody planned that. It is the most repeatable structural fact on the board.

**Baldwin breaks [[The Isolation Principle — One Emotion, One Incident|Barrett's unities]] across decades and two cities and still scores 44.** Losing his single point on structural economy. **Sprawl is survivable. Flat is not.**
