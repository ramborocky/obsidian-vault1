## 2.4% · The legend frame — "They say the grove still hums at noon."

`#atlas/barrett` `#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/mckee`

- [[The Beginning — Three Paragraphs at Most]] — Barrett satisfied. Voice, place and threat inside one paragraph.
- [[Methods of Narration — The Author Must Vanish]] — the story is handed to the reader as village rumour before any narrator claims it.
- [[Setting and Genre]] — the grove is installed as a *character* here and will be the last thing on the page. **A frame, closed at 99%.**

## 6.9% · Vishnu — "twenty-five wearing the face of fifty"

`#atlas/barrett` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Character by Salient Detail]] — age and its cost in seven words.
- [[Verisimilitude, Not Verity — The Use of Facts]] — ⭐ **the competence beat.** He knows the price of rice in the shop, what buyers give for wet cocoa, and how the distance between the two leaves a man short. **Poverty stated as arithmetic, not atmosphere.** See [[Nazir — The Serpent in the Grove|the sheet]] on the competence hypothesis.

## 13.0% · Sita — "as if sound were taxed"

`#atlas/gardner` `#atlas/mckee`

- [[Character Dimension and Cast Design]] — an orphan who *learned to take the shape of whatever container held her.* The whole future of the story is in that clause.
- [[Show, Don't Tell — Needless Explanation]] — *They did not cancel.* Three words for a marriage.

## 16.5% – 20.0% · The rum shop, and Zoongie

`#atlas/cleland` `#atlas/mckee`

- [[Set the Stage]] — the shop *leaned into the road like a rotten tooth.*
- [[The Devil Made Me Do It]] — Cleland's motive-machinery: the rum invents the encouragement. **Zoongie never looks back, and the story says so.** The temptation is entirely internal, which is the honest version.
- [[Illuminate Fear and Dread]] — dread built from a woman who does nothing.

## 23.5% · "Sita became obstacle by existing."

`#atlas/mckee`

- [[The Inciting Incident]] — the intent forms.
- ⚠️ **Stated, not shown.** *Vishnu began to plan with the patience of a reptile.* Compare [[Atlas — The Withheld Fact]]: the strongest stories leave the reader to convict. This one convicts him in the narration at 23%.

## 26.5% · The well — "It was not empty down there. It was waiting."

`#atlas/cleland` `#atlas/barrett`

- [[Illuminate Fear and Dread]] — the best two sentences in the story's first half.
- [[The Unities in Miniature]] — one place. The well is present at 26%, 35%, 50%, 70%, 92% and 99%. **The unity Barrett wanted, obeyed exactly.**

## 30.1% · "Play by Auntie Marsha." ⭐⭐

`#atlas/mckee` `#atlas/kiteley` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Active Vs Passive Protagonists.md|Active vs passive protagonists]] — ⭐ **Sita's one decisive act, and it is the story's hinge.** She reads the cutlass and the cleared path, and **sends the child out of the house.**
- [[What You Are Censoring]] — **she never says what she knows. Not here, not ever.** See [[Atlas — The Withheld Fact]].
- [[Interest, Free Will, and the Active Protagonist]] — without this beat she is purely a victim and the story collapses into [[Working Backward from the Climax — the Victim-Story Trap|Gardner's trap]]. **One paragraph saves it.**

## 35.3% · The fall

`#atlas/mckee`

- ⚠️ **She falls by accident.** The plank gives way on its own; he is not present.
- [[Casuality Vs Coincidence.md|Causality vs coincidence]] — McKee's warning, live. The trap is his; **the springing is not.** It costs the story two points on the gap — see the sheet.

## 37.4% · "Halfway to dying… gave way to small things" ⭐

`#atlas/learning-curve` `#atlas/gardner`

- [[Psychic Distance]] — the closest the narration ever gets. *He must remember me alive.*

## 41.3% · Marsha hears the silence

`#atlas/cleland` `#atlas/barrett`

- [[Character by Salient Detail]] — she is shelling peas and thinking about a letter for a mother whose son was held for cussing a policeman. **One clause, and a whole village exists.** Gate 3 leakage 5/5 comes largely from here.

## What the map shows

**A story with a 5-scoring close at 95.5% that keeps going.** Four separate endings — the rescue, *plant it by the door*, the granddaughter, the sermon.

**Sita acts exactly once, at 30.1%, and it is enough.** One paragraph is the whole difference between this story and a victim narrative. Compare [[Atlas — The Active Protagonist]]: Chantal acts five times, the master draft never.

**The frame is perfect and the body overruns it.** The grove hums at 2.4% and at 99.9%. Everything Barrett wanted about unity, obeyed — and everything he wanted about proportion, ignored.
