It may seem that I am particularly optimistic about this day, but as strange as it may seem, I feel a bit lost in this world. To begrudge the world as something that is distasteful would be cynicism if not a cliché. Yes a cliché, to seem a person that is strange to the bombardment of assorted distractions. Chiq, you might say, or nuanced in a way. But none the less, all these are all dire and might be atypical attempts to deter from the obvious.

I keep looking at the outside through the window of a dala-dala bus, anticipating my drop off point. A drop off point two hours away from now. Not wanting to seem out of place, and slightly avoiding eye contact from the rest of the passengers, wondering if they see through my treachery — if treachery would be the correct spelling.

But half an hour till drop off, a young woman of about twenty early years boards on. Not something pretty looking, chic or nuanced, yet a sort of just sophisticated drop of fashionware that seems to be on her — which might have been not her choice about it. She wears it elegantly.

Then it might be my own mind that sees such beauty. But is it real beauty, or just temporal arousal. You know the one that got you interested a year pre-puberty, is probably the only girl-woman that seems to be stuck on my mind.

But back to the bus analogy. It seems that the woman — I beg your pardon, the young early twenties, nuanced sophisticated simply put in place in a dala-dala bus rule — yes, she doesn't give a shit, fuck about me eyeing her for the fucking duration of half an hour. But the rest of the 20–30 odd passengers don't know what the fuck is taking place. And as I board off the bus, I'm them thinking they are looking at me, with my back turned to them.

The fact of the matter, what really puzzles me, is how people might know you are on Adderall, or even if you don't suffer from a disorder like ADHD.

But you think that in this slight moment, humanity will end up in an apocalypse where by my heart would fail. But then again you have this deep feeling that you are the smartest person on the bus. But then again, nobody gives a shit.

I wasn't on Adderall — maybe the coffee I sipped from a bottle in my backpack might be given the onset of jitters. But am I imagining things, whereby I act like I don't know anything, when I feel — ooh sorry, I think, I know everything.

Then I remember Sinéad O'Connor saying, "What's the point of living in a world where you anticipate death every fuckin' moment."
