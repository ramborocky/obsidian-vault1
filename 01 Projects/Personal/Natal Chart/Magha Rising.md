## Planetary hours

Chaldean order — **Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Sun → Venus → Mercury → Moon**. Split daylight into 12 and darkness into 12; the first hour after sunrise belongs to the weekday's ruler, then step the loop. Step 24 and you land on the next day's ruler, which is why the week is ordered as it is.

Favourable: **Sun** (lagna lord), **Mars** (yogakāraka, rules 4+9), **Jupiter** (current daśā lord, rules 5).

Saturn hours are not to be avoided. Saturn is on the ascendant, rules the 6th and 7th, and takes the mahādaśā in 2029 — there is no routing around it. The tradition's answer to a Saturn like this is service, not avoidance.

Run `ephemeris/planetary-hours.js` for any week.
