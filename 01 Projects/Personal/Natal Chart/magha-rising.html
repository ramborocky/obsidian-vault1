Chinese lodges are defined by right ascension rather than ecliptic longitude, so those two identifications are approximate. The nakshatra and manzil columns are not.

Planetary hours

The one piece of this that never expires, because it’s a method rather than a forecast. Derive it for any day, anywhere, forever.

Three traditions run the identical system. Sanskrit calls it horā; Arabic astronomers call it sāʿāt al-kawākib and al-Bīrūnī sets it out in full; the Latin West calls them planetary hours. Same order, same rules, one Hellenistic root.

  1. Split daylight into 12 equal parts and darkness into 12. These are your hours — unequal, and rarely 60 minutes. At 6°49′S they stay within a few minutes of 60 all year, which makes Dar es Salaam an unusually forgiving place to work this.
  2. The first hour after sunrise belongs to the ruler of that weekday. Sunday Sun, Monday Moon, Tuesday Mars, Wednesday Mercury, Thursday Jupiter, Friday Venus, Saturday Saturn.
  3. Every hour after that steps through the Chaldean order, slowest planet to fastest, looping forever:

Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Sun → Venus → Mercury → Moon → (repeat)

Why the week is ordered the way it is. Step 24 hours through that loop from any day’s first hour and you land exactly on the next day’s ruler. Sunday to Monday to Tuesday looks arbitrary until you know the Chaldean sequence — then it’s forced. The names of the days of the week are an artefact of this table.

The counterintuitive part

Saturn is not a planet you get to avoid. It is sitting on your ascendant, it rules the house of your health and the house of your marriage, it has just spent seven and a half years on your Moon, and in 2029 it takes over your life for nineteen years. There is no version of this chart where you route around it.

The tradition’s answer to a Saturn like this was never avoidance. It is service: consistency, discipline, unglamorous work done on schedule, and charity toward the old, the poor and those who labour. So use the Saturn hours rather than dodging them — a recitation kept up in a Saturn hour on a Saturday is the tradition’s own prescription for exactly your configuration. Saturn is said to answer to being shown up for. Nothing else moves it.

On the legal status. Using the hours to choose your timing is ordinary almanac practice. Using them to compel an outcome is where classical scholars draw the line, and it is the objection behind most rulings against this material. The safe position, and the one most ṭuruq operate under: pick your hour, do your dhikr, and leave the result to God.

The rule

Four complaints — the office you can’t leave, the people you can’t face, the body that aches, the mirror that lies about your age — map onto four placements. The rule answers all four. It is deliberately small, because a rule you keep beats a rule you admire.

Not a crowd. Mars is fallen in the 12th, so crowds cost you something real and pretending otherwise doesn’t help. One person. One hour. That is the whole obligation.

The knot

The three things wanted — the woman, the story prize, the rank at work — are all on the far side of the door being held shut. Every one of them requires being seen. That is the illness, and the rule above is aimed at it rather than at any of the three separately.

The story prize is the nearest fruit. Jupiter rules the 5th — what you make — and sits in the 11th, the house of gain and recognition, running its own mahādaśā until 2029. It is the single most favourable thing in the chart and it is live right now. Magha rising and Ketu in the 8th in Revatī say what the material is: the ancestors, the line, what was handed down unasked.

And the withdrawal is not the sickness — it is the studio. The pull inward runs through Jupiter/Mars to February 2027, from the 12th, the house of the closed room. That is where writing gets done. Use the season for the work rather than for hiding, then come out of it.

At work, the long hours are not the obstacle. The lord of the 10th is fallen and tangled with Rahu in the house of speech: the work gets done and is not seen being done. Visibility, not productivity. Saturn rises by outlasting in plain sight, and in 2029 that is the currency that pays.

And the body is a doctor’s business. Bloods — thyroid, vitamin D, B12, iron — and every cardiology appointment kept, every tablet taken as prescribed. Aching, tiredness, looking older and withdrawing have ordinary treatable causes far more often than cosmic ones, and only one kind of cause comes with a prescription. The tradition never permitted otherwise: seeking the cure is obedience, not doubt. Taking the medicine on schedule every morning is itself an act of Saturn.

Three tiers of confidence

Computed and verified.
Every position here. The lunar theory was validated against Drik Panchang’s independent Lahiri figures at four separate instants and agreed to better than one arcminute. Planetary positions use JPL approximate elements, good to arcminutes. The arithmetic is not in doubt.
Traditional, not demonstrated.
Every interpretation. Debilitation, tārābala, sade sati, the mansion attributions, the daśā readings. These come from specific text lineages that disagree with one another, and no controlled evidence supports them. They are reported accurately as tradition, and claimed as nothing more.
Not blind.
The rectification rests on confirmations given after the expected answers had been stated. Two independent tests pointed the same way, which is suggestive — it is not proof. A documented birth time would still beat all of it.
