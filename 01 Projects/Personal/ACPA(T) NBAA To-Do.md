# 🎓 BECOMING ACPA(T) — NBAA TANZANIA

## What ACPA Is
**Associate Certified Public Accountant (ACPA)** — the professional membership grade issued by NBAA (National Board of Accountants and Auditors, Tanzania) that a **Graduate Accountant (GA)** upgrades into after gaining practical experience. There's also **ACPA-PP** (Public Practice variant, for auditors).

*(If you're not already a registered Graduate Accountant with NBAA, that's the actual first step — everything below assumes GA status as the starting point. Flag it if that's not where you are yet and I'll dig into the GA registration route instead.)*

## Official Requirements (confirmed from nbaa.go.tz)
1. **3 years of practical work experience** as a Graduate Accountant, logged.
2. **Three completed logbooks** — one per year of practical experience — submitted as proof.
3. **Application form** (downloadable from nbaa.go.tz) filled and submitted.
4. **Supporting documents required:**
   - Copy of relevant Board-issued certificates
   - Name and address of employer
   - Two referees — professional accountants already registered with NBAA (ACPA or FCPA)
   - (For ACPA-PP specifically: referees must be ACPA-PP or FCPA-PP)
   - Two recent passport-size photographs
   - Proof of payment of the non-refundable application fee
5. Submission now goes through **MEMS** (NBAA's Members and Examination Management System) — online.
6. Application reviewed and approved by the Board.
