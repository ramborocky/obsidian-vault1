# THE MILLIONAIRE QUESTION
### "How do I become a millionaire in three years using Claude Code?"

Part of [[Operating Manual]]. The honest answer, with the division done.

---

## First: which million

**TSh 1,000,000 = $378.** You spent 2,184,000 in six days in the July log ([[Expenses]]). Local-currency millionaire is not the question being asked, so the question is USD.

**$1,000,000 = TSh 2,647,000,000.**

---

## The division

Three years is 36 months. Starting from roughly zero:

```
$1,000,000 ÷ 36 months  =  $27,778 per month, saved, every month
                        =  TSh 73,527,778 per month
```

Set against your own numbers, that figure is **36× the entire monthly running cost of your household** (~$775, from [[Three Tracks — An Honest Comparison]]), and **11.6× the top of the freelance range** in that same note ($2,400/mo at 20 hrs/week).

### The line that closes the door

At a specialist rate of $30/hour, $27,778/month requires **926 billable hours a month — 214 hours a week.** A week contains 168.

Even at $100/hour it is 64 billable hours every week for three years, saving one hundred percent of it and eating nothing.

> **Selling hours cannot reach $1M in 36 months at any rate, ever.** This is arithmetic, not pessimism, and it is worth knowing early because it eliminates an entire category of plan.

## So the only routes are

1. **Equity** — build something and sell it, or own a share of something that gets sold. This is how nearly every three-year millionaire happened.
2. **Capital you already have** — you don't.
3. **Luck** — see base rates in [[Ten Things I Was Blind To]], point 9.

Route 1 is real and not closed to you. It is also a different project from anything currently in this vault, it has a low base rate, and it does not answer a lender who bills next month.

## What is actually available in 36 months

Not a fantasy, and not nothing:

| Month | Target | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | Three paid invoices in hard currency | Proves the offer exists. See [[The Ten-Years-Ahead Operator]] Phase 1 |
| 12 | Debt cleared; income ≥ $1,500/mo | The 1.2M/month to AMON and DMK stops. This is the real prize |
| 24 | First inbound enquiry you did not chase | Distribution has started working |
| 36 | Three uncorrelated income lines; ~TSh 100M net worth (~$37,800) | Ambitious. Not fantasy. |

**TSh 100M in three years is a hard, respectable, achievable target.** $1M is not. Aiming at the second is the fastest way to miss the first, because it makes the boring reliable path look too small to bother with — and the boring reliable path is the one that ends the debt.

## What Claude Code actually does

Honest accounting, since it was in the question.

**It does:**
- Collapse the production cost of deliverables — models, templates, reports, scripts, a site, a publishing pipeline. Work that used to cost a week costs an afternoon.
- Multiply output per hour **on work you can already judge**. Your accountancy training is what makes its output safe to sell; without that you are shipping things you cannot check.
- Lower the cost of a *standing hour* (see the Two-Handed Test in [[Five Models I Can't Unsee]]) — building the asset that keeps earning is now cheap, which is exactly where compounding lives.
- Make the rare pair — numbers plus clean English — operable at a scale one person could not previously reach.

**It does not:**
- Create demand. Nobody has ever bought anything because of the tool it was made with.
- Send the ask. Ten asks a week is a human act and always will be.
- Supply judgement, taste, or a client relationship.

**And the warning:** it is the finest productive-procrastination machine ever built. It will help you construct apparatus, indefinitely, cheerfully, forever. See [[The Mentor Session — Seven Ideas]] §3 — the elaborate system is often armour, and this tool makes armour almost free.
