## IDEA A · Training mama ntilie and SMEs on bookkeeping and loans

**Verdict: correct insight, fatal business model, wrong decade. Park it — do not kill it.**

### What kills it

**1 · It already ran at scale and went dark.** Kippa (Nigeria) built exactly this — bookkeeping for informal traders. Raised **over $14M** from Target Global and others. Reached roughly **500,000 small businesses.** The payments arm shut in October 2023 with 40 layoffs, a co-founder left, and by 2025 the app was inaccessible with users unable to retrieve their own sales records. Half a million users, no revenue model that held. That is not an execution story; it is a willingness-to-pay story.

**2 · The training is already free, and better funded than you can be.** Vodacom ran financial literacy for 2,000+ women in Namtumbo with the district office. WFP and the Mastercard Foundation run Vijana Kilimo Biashara with the **BOT Academy itself.** CRDB's Malkia bundles mentorship and training into the loan. You would be selling, to people with no spare cash, what three better-capitalised organisations give away as customer acquisition.

**3 · Your own city has the funded version.** **Ramani.io** — Dar es Salaam, Y Combinator W20, Tanzanian founders, **$32M Series A, $34.3M total, and a Bank of Tanzania lending licence.** Model: digitise the small operator's records, lend against what the records reveal. That is the idea, licensed and capitalised, three miles away.

**4 · The customer cannot pay.** A mama ntilie clears a few thousand shillings a day. Regional research on informal-sector digital adoption is consistent — the barriers are digital literacy, fear that money will vanish, connectivity, and a real preference for the notebook and the WhatsApp voice note, which feel more trustworthy than any app.

**5 · So the only payer is a bank, telco or donor.** FSDT's **Biashara Financing Innovation Fund** is real money aimed at exactly this. But enterprise and grant cycles run 12–18 months, and [[00 Profile — The Memory|the lenders bill monthly]]. **You cannot fund a grant-cycle business out of a cash-flow emergency.** That is what kills it as a *first* business, not the idea itself.

### What survives, and it is not small

The underlying insight is provably right. **NMB's own MSE loan criteria require the applicant to have "a proper recording system."** CRDB's Malkia requires TZS 5M turnover and a personal bank statement. Records are the gate to credit, and **only 15–20% of Tanzania's ~3.5 million SMEs get bank loans** — at 17–20% interest, against collateral, through complex applications. MSEs are over 35% of GDP and employ 5M+ people.

**Keep this as the year-three product**, sold to a bank or funded by FSDT, with a Tier 2 microfinance licence if it ever lends (TZS 20M minimum capital, TZS 500,000 application fee, BOT). It may be the most valuable thing built here. → [[Legacy — What Scales]]

## IDEA B · The AI-native accounting practice

**Verdict: this is the business. It is a funded, proven 2026 category and the price ladder works from Tanzania.**

### The category is real and capitalised

| Company | Raised | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basis** | $100M Series B at **$1.15B** | Led by Accel, Feb 2026 |
| **Modus** | $85M seed + Series A | Lightspeed. AI-native audit — *and it buys firms* |
| **Accrual** | $75M | Out of stealth, General Catalyst |
| **General Catalyst Creation Fund** | **$800M → $1.5B** | Explicitly to buy and AI-modernise services firms |

And the shape of the thing, from the category itself: *"Last Accounting Company"* sells what a traditional firm sells — bookkeeping, filings — but **agents do the work and a certified accountant signs off.**

> **That sentence is the business. You are the accountant who signs. Claude is the agent.**

### One person can run it

**Elev8 CFO**, a solo advisory practice in Texas, went AI-native: month-end close fell from **18 days to 5**, about **90 hours a month** reclaimed, and the owner now services **37 client engagements with no staff accountants at all.**

### The price ladder, and where you sit on it

| Who | Rate | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US-based bookkeeper | $40–75/hr | Not your competition |
| **Offshore (India, Philippines)** | **$10–25/hr**, $1,200–2,000/mo per FTE | **This is your competition** |
| AI-assisted platforms | **$150–400 per client per month** | This is your price |
| What a US small business pays | $400–800/mo median | This is the budget |

You are not competing with American firms. You are competing with Bangalore and Manila, and you beat them on clean English prose, on European-hours overlap, and on being AI-leveraged rather than headcount-leveraged. **Payoneer, Wise and Deel all operate from Tanzania**, so the rail is not a blocker.

### The number that ends the emergency

Household + debt service = **$1,228/month**. At $350 per client per month, that is **four clients.**

### The valuation arithmetic, done properly

Firms sell at **0.8–1.5× revenue** or 3–7× EBITDA; under $500k revenue it's **0.9–1.2× revenue**; bookkeeping specifically at **2.5–3.3× SDE**. So $1M of enterprise value needs roughly **$850k–1.1M of revenue** — about **180–230 clients** at $400/month. That is a firm with a team, not a solo practice.

Realistic band, executed well:

| | Month 12 | Month 24 | Month 36 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clients | 10 | 25 | 45–60 |
| Revenue | ~$42k/yr | ~$105k/yr | **$215–290k/yr** |
| Sale value @ ~1.1× | — | — | **$235–320k** |
| Debt | cleared | — | — |

**Net worth landing around $300–450k (TSh 0.8–1.2bn) in three years.** Roughly a third of a million dollars. Not the million — and four to five times the TSh 100M figure in the first draft of [[The Millionaire Question — Honest Arithmetic]], which under-called what AI leverage does to a one-person practice.

## IDEA C · YouTube and content

**Verdict: a distribution channel, never an income line.**

CPM by audience: South Africa ~$10, **Kenya $3.20, Nigeria $2.50** · **US $32–36 general, finance $20–38, B2B/SaaS occasionally $50+.** A Swahili channel for a local audience is a 10–20× worse business than the same effort aimed west — and neither is income for you.

But: **one acquired client at $400/month is worth more than 100,000 local views.** Write in English, about one narrow niche, for the people who might hire you. For this buyer LinkedIn beats YouTube. → [[The Second Curriculum]] IV.4
