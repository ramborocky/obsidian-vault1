# BOOKLETS
### The same work, made readable

Part of [[Operating Manual]]. Four volumes, each saved three ways: as an Obsidian note in this folder (the one to read), as a standalone `.html` file for the browser, and as a private artifact. The fourteen plates live in `plates/` as SVG and are embedded in the notes.

> [!tip] How to open them
> **Read them in Obsidian** — each volume is now a note in this folder, with its plates as SVG files under `plates/`. The `.html` files are the browser edition; Obsidian does not display `.html`, so open those by double-clicking them in your file manager, or use the artifact link, which renders on a phone and follows light or dark mode.

## Which to read when

- **Nothing has changed and you want the whole picture** → The Lake and the Ledger. It is the shortest and it has the pictures.
- **You want the workings** → The Standing Hour for the arithmetic, the two question volumes for the reasoning.
- **You have ten minutes on a Monday** → the five questions at the end of [[Seventeen Questions]], or Part six of the Lake and the Ledger.

## Source notes
Everything in these volumes is derived from notes in the vault, not the other way round. The accounting side: [[00 Profile — The Memory]], [[Due Diligence — What the Market Says]], [[Unknown Unknowns — What Could Kill This]], [[Better Than Free — The Eight Generatives]], [[Seventeen Questions]]. The writing side: [[Provenance — The AI Question and the Public Repo]], [[Unknown Unknowns — The Writing Side]], [[05 The Writing Questions]].

**When a fact changes, fix [[00 Profile — The Memory]] first, re-run the affected prompts with `/second-brain`, then rebuild the volume.** In that order — the booklets are downstream and go stale silently.
