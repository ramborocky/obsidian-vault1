# THE RHYTHM
### Three a day · three books · three a month · three a quarter · three courses

> [!important] Read this first
> The vault already knows the failure mode: **sustained effort, no exposure** ([[The Mentor Session — Seven Ideas]] §3). Every item below is either a small daily act or something that puts work in front of another person. If this list turns into another apparatus, close it. One book at a time. Courses are not progress until a drill comes out of them.

---

## Daily — three things

| # | What | How long | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dawn: Fajr, then outside.** Twenty minutes of morning light, walking or sitting, phone in pocket. This is the meditation. Dhikr on the walk if you want words. | 20–30 min | From [[Magha Rising]]: the remedy for a fallen Sun is the rising one. Morning light also sets sleep and mood, and that is measurable. |
| 2 | **One writing block before anything else claims the day.** Kalemie / Mihogoni, or the two paragraphs [[Home]] says are owed. Timer on, door shut. **During the MAF: Tue and Thu 05:50, plus Sunday 07:00.** Mon/Wed/Fri mornings are study ([[MAF — Roadmap, Protocol and Timetable]] §4) | 45 min | Jupiter/Mars runs to 10 Feb 2027 from the 12th: the closed-room season. Use it. |
| 3 | **Five-line evening log.** (1) what I did, (2) one exposure: sent, asked, showed, (3) tablets taken ✓, (4) spend logged in [[Expenses]] ✓, (5) leave-work time kept ✓. | 5 min | This is the journal, but built to catch the pattern. A day with line 2 empty is the one to notice. |

> [!tip] Plus the fixed rules from [[Magha Rising]]
> Leave work at a **clock time**, not "when I'm done". **One person, once a week**, not a crowd. **Saturday, a Saturn hour**: Ṣalāt al-Tunjīnā.

## Books — three, in this order

1. **_A Swim in a Pond in the Rain_ — George Saunders.** Seven Russian short stories taken apart page by page. The closest thing in print to a masterclass on exactly what *Chantal* is. Read it a story at a time alongside your own drafts.
2. **_The Psychology of Money_ — Morgan Housel.** Short chapters on behaviour, debt and "enough". The right frame while 55% of a logged week went to debt service. This is a book about money habits, not investing advice.
3. **_Million Dollar Consulting_ — Alan Weiss.** How to price value instead of hours. The practice sells *interpretation with a signature on it* ([[Better Than Free — The Eight Generatives]]), and this is the book on charging for that.

*Already on the list:* _Superforecasting_ in [[The Second Curriculum]]. Don't start it until one of the three above is finished.

## Courses and videos — three

1. **_AI for Everyone_ — Andrew Ng, DeepLearning.AI on Coursera.** Free to audit, about six hours. Plain-language grounding for an AI-native accounting practice.
2. **_The Science of Well-Being_ — Laurie Santos, Yale on Coursera.** Free to audit. Evidence-based habits (sleep, gratitude, social connection), and it pairs with the daily three.
3. **_Story Club_ — George Saunders, on Substack.** The live companion to book 1: he takes stories apart with readers week by week. Some posts are free; the full archive is paid.

> [!warning] Drill, not consumption
> For each course, write one line in the evening log saying what you changed because of it. A course with no line after two weeks gets dropped.
