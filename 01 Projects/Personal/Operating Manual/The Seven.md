# THE SEVEN
### The prompt set, saved — and how to run it so it works

Part of [[Operating Manual]]. **Run `/second-brain` in Claude Code to execute all seven against the current profile.**

---

> [!important] The one rule that makes these work
> Run them **after** the memory is loaded, never before. Cold, they produce competent generic advice you have read a hundred times. Loaded with [[00 Profile — The Memory]], prompt 1 names the three unread fields on your MEMS profile and prompt 7 prices a business at $350 a client. **Same seven prompts. The difference is entirely the context.**

## The set

| # | Prompt | Output note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Teach me what I've ignored.** Ten critical skills, ideas or mental models I've likely been blind to, based on what high performers wish they'd learned earlier. Why each matters more than I think. | [[Ten Things I Was Blind To]] |
| 2 | **Collapse a year of wisdom into an hour.** Be my mentor. Compress the most important lessons from psychology, philosophy and real experience into one session. No fluff — ideas that shift how I see myself and the world. | [[The Mentor Session — Seven Ideas]] |
| 3 | **Show me what school never did.** Ignore traditional education. A personal curriculum to think clearly, act strategically, communicate persuasively and adapt faster than my environment. Include the why behind each lesson. | [[The Second Curriculum]] |
| 4 | **Decode how people become unstoppable.** The psychology, beliefs and behaviours of someone who keeps levelling up when things get hard, boring or overwhelming. Practical, and uncomfortable if needed. | [[Unstoppable — The Architecture]] |
| 5 | **Teach me like I'm building a legacy.** Assume I'm building something that matters, not just growing. The thinking that scales impact, influence and income — beyond tactics, into the mindset shifts to lead at the highest level. | [[Legacy — What Scales]] |
| 6 | **Create mental models I can't unsee.** Five original models that simplify how I make decisions, evaluate people, learn from failure and spot leverage. Simple to remember, powerful enough to change how I operate. | [[Five Models I Can't Unsee]] |
| 7 | **Train me to make more money.** Me operating ten years ahead. What would that person know, think and unlearn? A training blueprint that makes me impossible to replace. | [[The Ten-Years-Ahead Operator]] |

## When to re-run

Not on a schedule. Re-run the affected prompts when a **fact in the profile changes**, because the answers are downstream of the facts:

| If this changes | Re-run |
| --- | --- |
| The three MEMS fields get answered | 7, then 3 |
| The debt turns out to be one-off, not monthly | 7, then 1 |
| First paid client lands | 4, 5, 7 |
| An idea gets killed or validated by research | 1, 6 |
| Nothing has changed | **nothing.** Re-running for the feeling of progress is the failure mode named in prompt 3 |

## Two failure modes, named in advance
1. **Running them cold**, getting generic answers, and concluding the prompts are shallow. They are a lens; the profile is the light.
2. **Reading seven fresh answers as a substitute for doing what the last seven said.** This set is the most sophisticated productive-procrastination device in the vault. → [[The Mentor Session — Seven Ideas]] §2
