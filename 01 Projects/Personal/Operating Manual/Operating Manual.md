## The thesis, in four lines

**1 · You are not selling bookkeeping, because AI makes that the free copy.** You are selling **interpretation with a signature on it, delivered fast**: Kelly's generatives 3, 4 and 1. The credential is the moat, not the admin. The MAF strengthens the interpretation side, but **it doesn't give you the signature. Only the ACPA does.** → [[Better Than Free — The Eight Generatives]]

**2 · The number that ends the emergency is still four.** Four clients at $350/month covers the household plus the debt. *Caveat:* that figure comes from July–August spending, before the MAF fee. Re-check it at the October month-end.

**3 · The obstacle was never ability or information.** It is that the exposing action always comes second. A degree is the most respectable apparatus there is, and it can hide you for twelve months while looking like progress. Count exposures, not hours. → [[The Mentor Session — Seven Ideas]] §3

**4 · One finding could still reorder all of it.** NBAA may require a supervising mentor and an employer's signature on the logbook. The timetable now includes a **workday**. *Open question:* is that job accounting work, and could that employer sign? If so, UNKNOWN #4 may be smaller than it looked. Unverified. → [[Unknown Unknowns — What Could Kill This]]
