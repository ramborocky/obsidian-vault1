## The through-line

Every note above converges on one claim, so it is worth stating plainly at the top rather than making you assemble it:

> **The problem was never ability, and it is not information. It is that the exposing action goes second.**

The apparatus is finished; the phone call is not. The rubric has nine axes; the two paragraphs are unwritten. This is the shape of every stalled thing in this vault, and it is not laziness — it is [[The Mentor Session — Seven Ideas|avoidance]], which is the most heavily-reinforced behaviour a person can learn, and which never announces itself as fear.
