# SEVENTEEN QUESTIONS
### Time-stamped, with the story attached

Part of [[Operating Manual]]. One file per chapter in `Seventeen Questions/`.

> [!important] Why questions replaced models
> The old note held five named mental models. Your two documents argued against that form and they were right. Ferriss's seventeen are not concepts — each is **a question, stamped to the moment it entered his life, told through what actually happened**, with real numbers attached. A model is something you nod at. A question is something you can ask on a Tuesday.
>
> So the models are retired into this. [[Five Models I Can't Unsee]] stays as a record of what was cut and why.

## Where they came from
Nine are drawn from the two documents you supplied — Kevin Kelly's *Better Than Free*, Ferriss's own seventeen, and the podcast passages with Rothblatt, Young, Gottlieb and Nosrat. Four came out of research done for this folder. Four came out of the vault's own record of the last six weeks.

**Question 17 is the only one that has to be answered before tomorrow.** The other sixteen are worthless without it, and this booklet becomes the most sophisticated avoidance device in the vault if it is read and nothing follows.
