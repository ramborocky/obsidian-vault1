# 3 · What can't be copied here?

**Kevin Kelly wrote this in 2008 and barely had to revise it in 2026.** The essay is in your documents and it reframes the whole plan.

His argument: copies used to be hard, so we built a legal system around who may make them, and creators lived by selling them. The internet made copies free. **AI made competent variations free** — words, images, code, advice, in seconds.

> When copies are super abundant, they become worthless, and stuff which can't be copied becomes scarce and valuable.

So: what can't be copied? Kelly names eight **generatives** — qualities that have to be generated in place, in time, in relationship, and cannot be cloned, faked, stored or banked. Immediacy, personalization, interpretation, authenticity, accessibility, embodiment, patronage, findability. Trust is the archetype. You cannot download it.

Now apply it, and the practice stops being about bookkeeping.

**The bookkeeping output is a copy.** Reconciliations, schedules, a set of management accounts — AI can produce competent versions of all of it in seconds, and the price of that is heading to zero. If the plan is to sell the output, the plan has a clock on it.

What is being sold instead:

- **Interpretation** — Kelly's third generative, and his example is literally this business: *software, free; the software manual, $10,000.* Red Hat has run on it for twenty-five years. What the numbers mean and what to do about them is the invoice.
- **Authenticity** — a warranted, signed, professionally liable set of accounts, with a named human behind it.
- **Immediacy** — a close on day five instead of day eighteen.
- **Personalization** — fitted to their accounts, their board, their reporting, iteratively, which is exactly why it is sticky.

> **The reframe:** you are not selling bookkeeping. Bookkeeping is now the free copy. You are selling **interpretation with a signature on it, delivered faster than anyone else can manage.**

That sentence sets the price, writes the offer, and explains why $350 a month is simultaneously cheap for the buyer and excellent for you.
