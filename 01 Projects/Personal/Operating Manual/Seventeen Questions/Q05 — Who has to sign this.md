# 5 · Who has to sign this, and can it be me?

**Kelly calls it Authenticity** — the artist's stamp on a lithograph, the band selling the verified recording, the signature that makes one copy worth more than an identical one.

In 2026 that stopped being a metaphor about art and became the business model of an entire funded category. Basis raised $100M at a **$1.15 billion** valuation. Modus raised $85M and buys accounting firms outright. General Catalyst took a fund from $800M to **$1.5 billion** to acquire and AI-modernise services firms. And the shape of what they are all building is one sentence:

> **Agents do the work. A certified accountant signs off.**

Which means the scarce asset is not the ability to do the work. It is **being allowed to stand behind it.**

That reframes something that has been sitting in this vault as admin. ACPA — and specifically **ACPA-PP**, the public-practice variant — is not a qualification to get around to. It is the only part of this business that cannot be competed away by anyone with the same tools and a faster internet connection.

And then the research turned up the sting. **NBAA's three years of practical experience must be logged under a supervising mentor, with the logbook signed by mentor and employer.** Which means the two central moves — go independent for cash, get credentialed for the moat — may be in direct conflict, and nobody had checked. → [[Unknown Unknowns — What Could Kill This]]

That does not kill the plan. It changes the sequence: employment with a mentor while the practice runs alongside, or a mentor who supervises your own work if NBAA permits it, or the clock is already running and the problem dissolves. **All three turn on facts you can get in one call.**

> **The question, generalised:** in any market where machines do the production, find the act that legally or professionally requires a named human. Then ask whether that human can be you, and what it costs to become them.

It is the highest-return question in this entire folder, and it has been sitting under the word *admin* since July.
