# 10 · What's the least crowded channel?

**Ferriss's own question, and it is directly usable.** December 2006, *The 4-Hour Workweek* finished, launch in April, no idea what to do. He asked a dozen bestselling authors what they'd waste money on again and heard one word: blogs.

Then the second question, which is the one that matters: **how is everyone currently trying to reach bloggers?** Email first, phone second. Those were his strengths — and he deliberately did not use them. He went to CES, never walked in the front door, parked himself in the Seagate-sponsored blogger lounge, drank the free drinks and asked dumb questions.

His reasoning, in his words: better odds as one of five people in a lounge than one email out of 500 in an inbox.

Apply it. Small US and UK accounting firms that outsource are pitched constantly by Indian and Filipino providers, and pitched by **cold email**. Email is the crowded channel. It is also the easy one, which is why it is crowded.

So what is uncrowded? Some candidates, cheapest first:

- **The forums and Slack/Facebook groups where firm owners complain to each other** about their outsourcing provider. Answer questions for months. Never pitch.
- **Their podcasts.** Small-firm owners run a startling number of them and are permanently short of guests who have something specific to say. §7216 and named-tools AI clauses are something specific to say.
- **The providers themselves** — overflow work from a Manila firm that is full.
- **One genuinely useful public artefact** aimed at exactly that reader. Not "about bookkeeping". Something like *the month-end close checklist for e-commerce sellers, with the six places AI gets it wrong.*

The general form: **find where the buyers already gather, then go there as a participant rather than a seller.** It is slower per contact and enormously better per conversion, and the reason most people don't is that it cannot be automated and offers no dashboard.

> **The question:** how is everyone else reaching this buyer — and what is the room with five people in it instead of the inbox with five hundred?
