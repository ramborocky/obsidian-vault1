Email signature

Paste your logo link, press Copy, paste into Gmail. That is the whole job.

1  Logo link

Must be public and need no login. Google Drive share links do not work — they serve a preview page, not the image. Test the link in a private browser window first: if the logo appears on a blank page, it will work. Leave this empty to preview with a stand-in.

2  Check it

Under a real message, which is where spacing problems show:

Dear Mr. Kimaro,

Please find attached the reconciliation for the August benefit expenditure. The variance on the outpatient line is explained in note 4.

Kind regards,

3  Copy it

Over Gmail's 10,000-character limit. Shorten the logo URL or use Gmail's own image upload instead.

        Step 5 is the one people skip. Without it, replies put your signature below
        the entire quoted thread where nobody scrolls.

        can ignore step 1 entirely.

        Neither the Gmail nor the Outlook mobile app can store an HTML signature.
        They accept plain text only. Mail you send from the web keeps the full
        signature; mail sent from the phone uses whatever you set there. Use
        Copy plain-text version above and paste it into
        Gmail app → Settings → your account → Mobile signature.
      

    

  


  
Before you rely on it

  

    

      Send one to yourself and open it in a different client than you sent
      from. Mail clients block remote images on first receipt, so click
      “display images” to confirm the logo actually resolves. If you can,
      send one to a colleague on Outlook — that is where layout problems surface.
