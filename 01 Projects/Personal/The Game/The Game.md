# 🎮 THE GAME
### Points for showing up. The most points for being seen.

> [!important] Why it's built this way
> Your known pattern is **sustained effort, no exposure**: big systems, little shown to anyone ([[The Mentor Session — Seven Ideas]] §3). So the scoring is lopsided on purpose. **One exposure is worth more than two hours of study.** Building templates, dashboards or rubrics earns **0 XP**. That includes changing this game.

## How you score

You fill in a few properties at the top of each day's note, in `Personal/Daily/`. The timer fills in the minutes for you (see **Timers** below).

| Property | What counts | XP |
|---|---|---|
| `write_min` | Minutes of **fiction or essay drafting**. Not planning, not rubrics | 1 per 5 min, max **30** |
| `study_min` | MAF study outside class: pre-read, notes, flashcards, assignments | 1 per 5 min, max **30** |
| `class` | You attended the 17:00 class, in person or on Zoom | **10** |
| `exam_q` | Exam-style questions answered against the clock | **5** each, max 3 |
| `walk` | The dawn walk | **5** |
| `tablets` | Blood-pressure tablets taken | **5** |
| `log` | The five-line evening log is written | **5** |
| `exposure` | **Something of yours went in front of someone who can say no:** a submission, a post, a draft to a reader, a pitch, a question to a lecturer | **25** each, max 2 |
| *weekly quest* | Each of the five weekly quests completed (Mon–Sun) | **+50** |

**A strong weekday:** class 10 + 30 min study 6 + exam questions 15 + walk 5 + tablets 5 + log 5 = **46 XP**. Add an exposure and it's **71**.

## The chains

- 🔥 **Show-up streak:** consecutive days with the evening log written. The one to protect.
- ✍️ **Writing chain:** consecutive **writing days** (Tue, Thu, Sun) with any writing minutes. Missing a Monday doesn't break it, because Monday isn't a writing day.
- 📘 **Study chain:** consecutive weekdays with a class or study minutes.

> [!tip] Never miss twice
> One missed day is life. Two in a row is a new habit forming. **If the chain broke yesterday, today's only job is the smallest version:** 10 minutes and the log.

## Timers and reminders

- **Focus timer:** double-click **Write 25** or **Study 25** on your desktop. It counts down, beeps, and **adds the minutes to today's note by itself**. If you stop early with Ctrl+C, the minutes you did still count.
- **Reminders:** Windows pops a notification at each slot in the timetable ([[MAF — Roadmap, Protocol and Timetable]] §4), and it stays on screen until you dismiss it. They're listed, with how to turn them off, in `scripts/rhythm/README.md`.

## The house rules

1. **Honest numbers only.** The game only works if you fill it in honestly. Rounding up is lying to the one person it's built for.
2. **No XP for building systems:** templates, dashboards, rubrics, this page.
3. **One rule change a month,** on the last weekend, during the money review. Not mid-week, when a rule change is usually a way of avoiding the work.
4. **Exposure is defined by the other person.** If nobody else saw it, it wasn't an exposure.
