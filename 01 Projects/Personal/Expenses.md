## HOW TO LOG

Add a row to the table. **Only Date and Amount are required.** Everything else I can infer or fix later.

```
| 2026-07-27 | Bus fare to town | Transport | 3,000 | |
```

If you're in a hurry, this is enough — I'll fill in the rest:

```
| 2026-07-27 | dala dala | | 3000 | |
```

**Rules that keep it painless:**
- Amounts in **TZS**. No commas needed. No currency symbol needed.
- Leave Category blank if you don't know — I'll assign it.
- Put anything odd in Notes: *"for mum"*, *"split with X"*, *"not sure if I already paid this"*.
- Never delete a row. If it was wrong, add a note. The log is a record, not a tidy list.
- Cash and mobile money both go here. So does anything paid by someone else on your behalf.

## WHAT I'LL DO WITH IT

Say **"do the expenses"** and I will:

1. **Fill the gaps** — assign categories to blank rows, normalise the dates and amounts, flag anything ambiguous rather than guessing.
2. **Total it** — by month and by category, with the arithmetic shown so you can check it.
3. **Find the pattern you can't see from inside** — which category is quietly the biggest, what has crept up, what you spend on when a week has gone badly.
4. **Flag the specific things that matter to you**, not generic advice:
   - **School fees have a real deadline** and everything else is negotiable against them. If a fee period is coming and the run-rate says it won't be there, I say so early, not late.
   - **The House and ACPA both compete for the same money.** I'll show you what each is actually costing per month, because two long projects funded from one pocket is how both stall.
   - **Writing spend is small and worth protecting.** Submission fees and printing are trivial next to everything else here; I won't suggest cutting them.
5. **Never moralise.** I'll tell you what the numbers say. What you do about it is yours.

### What I won't do

- **No banking, no payments, no transfers.** I don't touch money, only the record of it.
- **Never enter card, bank or mobile-money credentials anywhere.** If something needs paying, you pay it.
- **No investment or financial advice.** I'm not licensed and won't pretend otherwise. Budgeting from your own numbers is not the same thing, and that I'll do.
