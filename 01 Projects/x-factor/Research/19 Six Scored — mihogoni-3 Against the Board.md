# 19 · SIX SCORED

**All five scoreable instruments, six stories.** [[mihogoni-3]] and [[mihogoni-3b]] against [[2026-07-28 — Chantal (Draft 3, Prize Pass)|Chantal D3]], ***Dite***, [[Nazir — The Serpent in the Grove|🏆 Serpent]] and [[Mothers Not Appearing in Search — Story Sheet|🏆 Mothers]].

**Why Lubwama is the fourth comparison.** He is the second prize-verified point on the board, he won in **the region a Congolese story competes in**, and *Mothers* is the closest published relative of the technique being tested — a narrator who reports accurately and understands nothing. Scoring the experiment against its own ancestor is worth more than scoring it against a fourth stranger.

> [!danger] Read all three of these before any number below
> **1 · Two of these six are my own drafts, scored by me.** The [[The ELIZA Effect and the Vestments of Plausibility|ELIZA problem]] in its purest form. Where a score flatters `mihogoni-3` or `3b`, **assume it is wrong first.** Two places below I have docked myself and said why; there are probably more I cannot see.
>
> **2 · Gate 4 is blank in all six columns.** Still no cold reader — unchanged since 2026-07-28. It is the largest single component of what a chair means, and it is 0% complete. **Every total on this page is partial.**
>
> **3 · Carried-over scores are the vault's, not re-derived** — Chantal, *Dite* and Serpent come from [[17 Chantal Complete — Scored Against the Three|note 17]] and [[18 Three Scored — chantal-3rd, Dite, mihogoni-2|note 18]] unchanged. New this page: **Lubwama on the Nine Axes** (never scored in the vault before), and **Gate 3 for Serpent, Dite and Lubwama** (also new). All four are marked ⊕ and are provisional.

> [!danger] Both experiments fail Gate 1b on rule 3e, permanently, and the instrument says what that means
> Gate 1b asks whether the *entrant* is eligible. **Every word of both files was written by Claude.** Commonwealth rule 3e requires the story to be the entrant's own work, and in June 2026 the Foundation investigated by examining working drafts and timestamped documents.
>
> [[The Three Gates — Unified Instrument]] is explicit: *"A story that fails a gate does not proceed, and its scores at later gates do not count."*
>
> **So every number below this line is diagnostic only.** It measures the *device*, not the prospects of anything. That is the correct and only use of a file in `Experiments/`, and the instrument reaching this verdict on its own — before I did — is the best evidence the Gates are built right. → [[12 The Rules — Source of Truth]]

**1a passed on a close call, twice.** The unquoted dialogue in `3b` is the same consistent system Chantal uses, and 1a explicitly passes a style decision. And withholding the narrator's identity is *not* a cohesion failure, because the narrator's identity is not a plot-critical fact — but it is the nearest thing to one this instrument has been asked to judge, and a stricter reader could rule the other way.

**⚠️ Lubwama fails 1b on our measurement and won anyway.** 5,112 words against a 5,000 ceiling. Either the submitted version differed from the Granta text, or **our counting method disagrees with the prize's by ~2%.** That is a live risk for a submission sitting at 3,896 and it is cheap to resolve: count Chantal in Word, the way [[README — Submission Format and Log]] already requires. **Do not carry a 2% unknown into a hard gate.**

> [!important] G3 is the finding. It is the only number on this page that moves four points on a single decision.
> [[The Three Gates — Unified Instrument]] records Chantal's **G3 · 3** as *"the lowest single score of the three finished drafts, and it comes directly out of Chantal's greatest strength… A voice that announces its own retrospection cannot slide."* The note calls the tension between Gate 2 rewarding that control and Gate 3 charging for it **the most useful thing the instrument has produced.**
>
> **`mihogoni-3` scores 2 — worse than Chantal.** It announces its narrator in sentence one and never pretends to discover anything.
> **`mihogoni-3b` scores 5.** The reader does not notice what is narrating until after it has happened, which is the band verbatim.
>
> **Same events, same material, same author, four points apart, and the only variable is whether the narrator is declared.** That is as clean a result as this vault has produced, and it answers Question 3 in the Three Gates note — *find the exact place a slide could be built without breaking the declarative voice.* **There isn't one inside Djamela's first person. There is one outside it.**

**G5 · defensibility 4 and 3, and this is where I score my own work lowest.** The argument against both in a judging room is one sentence: *it is a retelling.* `3b` gets 4 because the epigraph structure is defensible on its own terms; `3` gets 3 because a declared elemental narrator is a familiar move and a judge has met it before.

> [!important] The result that matters most, and it now replicates
> Note 18: ***"The Person (3) and The World (2) do not move for interiority work of any kind** — not insertions, not free indirect discourse, not sympathy engines."* Three attempts, all aimed at interiority, all moved the Chair Test by **zero**.
>
> **Both passes here move Person to 4 and World to 4.** Chantal's 16–17 becomes 21 — into the published band (21–24) — and `mihogoni-2` did the same thing to reach 22.
>
> **Two independent experiments now agree: the narrator moves the two hard axes and interiority never does.** The Person came from the mother, who acts offstage and is never seen doing it; the World came from surplus a narrator with no stake has no reason to prune — the man paid to wash the car, the eleven kilometres on a bicycle, the two seasons of rain on the heap, the queue at the standpipe, the car that will be finished in eleven years.

**Two self-docks, both against my own work:**

- **The Voice · 4, not 5.** Both meet the written test — a signature sentence inside the first 10% (*the back of her neck was giving her up faster than the hour called for*). But note 18 already flagged that it over-scored `mihogoni-2` at 5 here, and the same correction applies harder: **most of what sounds like voice in `3b` is Chantal's voice, borrowed.** A test that a borrowed sentence can pass is measuring the wrong thing.
- **The Position · 4, ceiling, not 5.** Band 4 names *epigraph, object-narrator, title* as proxy devices. `3b` uses all three at once, which does not promote it — it just uses band 4 three ways. Same ceiling as *Dite*. **The instrument caught me again.**

> [!warning] The Chair Test cannot tell the two passes apart. Gate 3 can.
> **21 and 21** on this instrument; **18 and 22** on Gate 3. The five chair-derived axes have no criterion that registers *when* a reader understands what is narrating — only whether the position is delivered by a proxy. **That is a real hole in a v1 instrument**, and the argument for always running both rather than picking the newer one.

## What this actually says

**1 · The experiment did the one thing three previous attempts could not, and it is not a prose gain.** The Person and The World have moved for the first time — 3→4 and 2→4 — and they moved because the *narrator* changed, not because the writing improved. Note 18 predicted this in one sentence and this page confirms it with a second data point. **The next work on Chantal is a narrator-and-other-people problem, and it has now failed three times as a sentence problem.**

**2 · Concealment is worth four points and costs one.** `3b` beats `3` by four at Gate 3 and loses one at NAPLAN cohesion. **Take that trade every time** — but log the cohesion loss, because it is the technique's genuine weak point and a first reader will feel it before they feel the slide.

**3 · Chantal's G3 · 3 is not a fault and is now proven fixable only from outside her.** The declarative first person that earns 40 at Gate 2 is the same thing that caps Gate 3 at 3. This page shows the cap lifts to 5 the moment the narrator stops being someone who already knows how the story ends — **and that is not a change Chantal can absorb.** It is a finding about the cycle, not about the draft.

**4 · Nothing here changes the submission.** Both experiments fail the floor on rule 3e and their scores do not count. **What is portable is three shapes** — the unattributed close third, instruction-as-epigraph in climbing registers, and an absence as motif — and the folder rule stands: a shape has to be rebuilt in the author's own hand or not used at all.

**5 · Lubwama is the best story on this board by both surviving instruments** — 66/70 and 24/25 — and the vault had never scored him on the Nine Axes until today. **The 2025 Africa winner is the most useful benchmark in the campaign and it is the least studied.** He is also the only story here that is funny, which the story sheet already flagged: *the two African winners on this board are both funny. Neither Chantal nor Beat of My Hurt is.*

**6 · The word-count risk is now concrete.** Lubwama measures 5,112 on our instrument and won. Count Chantal in Word before anything else is decided.
