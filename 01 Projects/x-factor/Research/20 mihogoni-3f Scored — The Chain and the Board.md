### The split pass — what was actually done

**The fault was one tic: polysyndeton.** Nearly every genuinely long sentence was a chain of clauses joined by *and… and… and*, and **all of them were in the historical apparatus I added** — the ship's four flags, the 1858 sounding, the Ujiji monument, the Lingala paragraph, Papa Kabeya, the libanga explanation, the deck sebene, the announcements.

**Eight sentences, split at the joint. The word *and* deleted, a full stop closed in its place. No words added.** This is [[2026-07-28 — Chantal (Draft 3, Prize Pass)|Draft 3]]'s own rhythm pass run backwards — that draft made 20 fusions by *deleting full stops, not adding words*, and this makes 19 splits the same way.

> **What was not touched, deliberately: every long sentence inherited from Draft 3.** *The hurt came off him like heat off a wall…* · *It was the cheapest thing she has ever given anybody…* · *She has had the measure of men like him since she was eleven…* · *Twenty minutes means he was already in town…* Those are the author's, several are named as protected in the Draft 3 header, and **they are the reason the over-30 figure floors at ~9% rather than going lower.** The remaining long sentences in this file are almost entirely Chantal's own.

**Result: 12.5% → 9.1% over thirty words, now marginally *below* Chantal D3 and *Dite*, at 13.7 average.** And it reads better — *The announcements are made twice, in Swahili and then in English. The Swahili is the same Swahili as the road outside her house. The English is for nobody on that deck.* Three beats instead of one clause-chain, and the third one lands.

**The X-Factor's finding 6 survives but needs a qualification.** *A digression is a long sentence* — true of the drafting, not true of the finished text. **A digression is a long sentence until you put full stops in it.** The surplus was never the problem; the punctuation was.

## What this says

**1 · The best result is the smallest one, and it is the only portable one.** *Shemeji* moved to the end, with a crowd staged in front of it, closes NAPLAN Finding B without a gloss and without a word of Claude's prose. **Everything else on this page is an experiment. That is a fix.**

**2 · It ties Chantal at Gate 3 and cannot take Chantal's points.** 23 each, by opposite routes — Chantal capped at 3 on the slide by its declarative voice, this capped on danger and defensibility by being derivative. **The trade is now measured rather than argued.**

**3 · The Chair Test has stopped working on this material.** 21, four times, through changes that transformed the file. Use it to compare *narrator strategies*; do not use it to compare *drafts*. That is a finding about the instrument and it should go into note 15's limitations.

**4 · The sentence profile was never the fault, and the finding that said it was is retracted.** The 17.8% was a broken splitter; the true figure was 12.5%, and **19 splits took it to 9.1% — below Chantal D3 and below *Dite*.** The apparatus did arrive in clause-chains, but that is a punctuation habit, not a structural cost. **A digression is a long sentence until you put full stops in it.**

> **The method is the transferable part: calibrate the counter against a column whose answer you already know before believing anything it says.** This page reported a headline finding off an instrument that could not reproduce Chantal D3. That is the second measurement error in this chain — the first was crediting a technique with a saving that was actually a deletion.

**5 · Nothing here changes the manuscript.** 1b fails on rule 3e and the scores do not count. **The shapes are: one object changing hands as the epigraph sequence · a clause that survives four regimes and becomes a price · the withheld word spent once at the end.** Rebuilt by hand or not used.
