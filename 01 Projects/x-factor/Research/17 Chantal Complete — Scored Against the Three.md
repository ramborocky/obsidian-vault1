## 🔴 THE FINDING

> ### The three insertions moved the Nine Axes by **+1** and the Chair Test by **0**.

That is not a failure of the insertions. **It is a precise diagnosis of what is left**, and it is the most useful result on this page.

The insertions were an interiority fix, a motif payoff and a mechanical-floor fix. **The Chair Test's remaining losses are not those things.** They are:

| | Chantal | 🏆 Serpent | Gap |

| | | | **−5** |

**Five of the six points between Chantal and the confirmed overall winner sit in two axes, and neither has anything to do with prose, structure, or the insertions just written.**

- **The Person — 3.** Djamela is real. The mother is furniture, Jean Luc has no object, Morisho is a function, Alicia is a device. **No one refuses their category.** In *Serpent*, Sita refuses victim, Marsha refuses busybody, and Zoongie — the woman the plot could blame — gets one good morning and her own baby.
- **The World — 2, still the lowest score anywhere in the vault.** There is no Pinto, no driving school, no letter for a mother whose son was held for cussing a policeman. The world begins and ends with the story.

**Both are additive.** No cutting, no restructuring, no risk. → [[The Three-Week Atelier]], days 17 and 18.

## 🔵 THE OTHER FINDING, AND IT SHOULD BE A RELIEF

> ### Chantal now scores **above the actual winner** on the Nine Axes — 36 to 31 — and **below it** on the Chair Test, 17 to 22.

Same inversion as before, now sharper and with a completed draft. The two instruments disagree about these two stories by **eleven points of relative position.**

**And look at where *Serpent* loses:** economy **2**, close **3**, the gap **3**. All architectural. It beat 7,805 stories anyway.

### Axis 7 is capped at 3 for Chantal — and for the winner, for the same reason

| | Why it is capped |
| --- | --- |
| **Chantal** | *I am not asking to be forgiven for that. I am asking for it to be counted correctly.* |
| 🏆 **Serpent** | *The serpent in the grove was never only a snake.* |

**Both state the controlling idea. Both are capped at 3. One of them won the prize.**

> **This matters for what you do next.** Insertion 3 was written to raise axis 7, and it genuinely improved the *dramatisation* — Daudi now chooses. **But the cap is not caused by weak implication. It is caused by two sentences saying the thing out loud**, and no amount of physical beat elsewhere lifts it while those sentences stand.
>
> **Do not cut them.** They are the best lines in the story, [[14 The Judges — Six Years of Chair Statements|six chairs in six years]] never once praised a story for its controlling idea being unstated, and the 2026 winner paid the identical price and won. **Know the price, pay it knowingly, and stop trying to fix it.**
