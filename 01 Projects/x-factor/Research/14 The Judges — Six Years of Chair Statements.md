# 14 · THE JUDGES — SIX YEARS OF CHAIR STATEMENTS

Every Chair of Judges, 2021–2026, quoted verbatim, with sources. Built to answer one question: **what do these panels actually say they reward?**

---

> [!danger] READ THIS FIRST — the campaign is calibrated against the wrong panel
> **Entries closing 1 November 2026 are the 2027 prize, not the 2026 prize.**
>
> The 2026 cycle **closed on 1 November 2025**, was shortlisted in April 2026, and was decided on 30 June 2026. It is over. Jamir Nazir won it.
>
> **Louise Doughty will not judge your entry.** Her sentence is the epigraph of [[The Judging Rubric — Nine Axes]]; [[02 Comparison Board]] draws its central finding from "this panel"; [[Home]] says *the rubric rewards architecture and this panel forgives architecture.* **That panel has finished its work.**
>
> **The 2027 chair has not been announced.** On the historic pattern — 2024's panel was named in August 2023, 2026's in 2025 — **it should be announced within weeks of entries opening on 1 September 2026.**
>
> This does not invalidate the campaign. Six years of statements below show the criteria are **remarkably stable across chairs**. But one variable is not stable, it is the largest one, and it is knowable in advance. **Watch for the announcement. It is the single highest-value input left before submission.**

### 2024 · **Jennifer Nansubuga Makumbi** (Uganda) — winner: *Aishwarya Rai*, Sanjana Thakur

> On the form: "**The short story form has neither the luxury of time nor the comfort of space. It is an impatient form; it does not dance around. The punch of a good short story leaves you breathless.**"

> On the shortlist: "This is a dream list for lovers of the short story form. You'll be **amazed and thrilled, startled and shocked, and heartbroken and humbled** in equal measure by the skill and talent, imagination and creativity."

## What six chairs actually praise

Every substantive phrase from six years, sorted. **This is the finding.**

| What was praised | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | Hit rate |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| **Emotional / moral force** — *moving, urgent, heartbroken, breathless, changes us, real heart* | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **6 / 6** |
| **Voice and prose** — *poetic, assured, tight, quiet voice, tones, mastery of craft* | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **6 / 6** |
| **Something to say** — *history, postcolonial reckoning, geography matters, something important* | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | **6 / 6** |
| **Character** — *humanity, character illumination, human nature, utterly believable* | ✅ | ✅ | — | — | ✅ | ✅ | 4 / 6 |
| **Structure, economy, plot, architecture** | ⚠️ | — | — | ⚠️ | — | — | **2 / 6** |

The two structural hits are both glancing: Wicomb's "**ingenious structure**" — a single adjective — and Makumbi's "**it does not dance around**", which is about pace, not architecture.

> [!danger] Six chairs, six years, ~8,000 entries a year, and **not one of them praised a story for its plotting, its economy, its scene-turns or its shape.**
>
> [[02 Comparison Board]] hypothesised this from two stories on 2026-07-28. [[Nazir — The Serpent in the Grove|The Nazir sheet]] confirmed it from one winner. **This is now six for six, from the judges' own mouths, across six different chairs from six different countries.**
>
> **[[The Judging Rubric — Nine Axes]] allocates 4 of 9 axes to architecture — scene turn, the gap, structural economy, resonant close.** It is measuring, at high resolution, the thing nobody has mentioned in six years.
>
> Keep the rubric. **It is a good instrument for finding what is broken and a poor one for predicting what will place** — its own warning box already says so, and this is the evidence for it.

### Doughty's three demands are not Doughty's

Her sentence — **believable characters · assured prose · something important to say** — reads like one chair's taste. It is not. Every predecessor said the same three things in their own words:

| Chair | Characters | Prose | Something to say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wicomb 2021 | *brims with humanity* | *the form fully exploited* | *love and death* |
| D'Aguiar 2022 | *character illumination* | *the varied tones of fiction* | *aware of history* |
| Tanweer 2023 | *real heart* | *mastery of craft, simplicity* | *postcolonial reckoning* |
| Makumbi 2024 | *the fracturing of the self* | *tight prose, stanza-like paragraphs* | *modern urban existence* |
| Hereniko 2025 | *many aspects of human nature* | *the quiet voice of a seer* | *geography matters* |
| Doughty 2026 | *utterly believable* | *assured* | *something important to say* |

**Six for six on the same three axes.** The rubric's epigraph is not one judge's preference — it is the prize's actual standing criteria, stated by six people who never coordinated.

### The one variable that does move: the chair's own register

The criteria are stable. **The taste on top of them is not.**

- **Makumbi 2024** is the only chair in six years to praise **humour** — *brutal irony, sarcasm, cynicism, wry humour, satire pulled off effortlessly*. She rewarded a comic story.
- **Hereniko 2025** rewarded the exact opposite register — *the quiet voice of a seer, deep and profound, an allegory.*

**A funny story submitted to Hereniko's panel and a solemn allegory submitted to Makumbi's would each have been meeting the wrong taste**, while both satisfied the standing criteria. Chairs also rotate by region — South Africa, Guyana, Pakistan, Uganda, Fiji, UK — and each brings a literature with them.

## → WHAT THIS CHANGES

**1 · Correct the target everywhere.** [[Home]], [[02 Comparison Board]] and [[The Judging Rubric — Nine Axes]] all reason about "this panel", meaning Doughty's. **The entry closing 1 November 2026 is judged by a 2027 panel that does not yet exist.** Nazir remains a valid *quality* benchmark and an invalid *panel* benchmark.

**2 · Put the judges announcement on the calendar.** Expected within weeks of 1 September 2026 — the same week entries open, and seven weeks before the self-imposed 25 October submit date. **It is the cheapest large piece of information available to this campaign and it arrives in time to act on.** The Africa regional judge matters as much as the chair: Chantal is read first by that person.

**3 · The final pass is confirmed, from six sources instead of one.** Voice, character, and position. Not structure. [[Home]]'s standing instruction — *VOICE, NOT STRUCTURE* — is now backed by six years of primary evidence rather than one inference.

**4 · The humour question is live and unanswered.** [[The X-Factor|The x-factor experiment]] argued that the drafts are solemn for 3,900 words and that no Congolese account of a bad year is solemn throughout — and both African winners on the board, **Lubwama and Makumbi's pick, are funny.** Chantal has no jokes in it. That is a real gap, and whether it matters depends entirely on a chair who has not been named yet.

**5 · Fix the *Dite* sheet.** It is not a 2025 regional winner; the 2025 five are listed above. Its craft findings stand; its status claim is now known to be false rather than merely unverified.
