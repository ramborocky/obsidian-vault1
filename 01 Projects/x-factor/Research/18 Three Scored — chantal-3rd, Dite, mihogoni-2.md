### Where chantal-3rd lost against the draft it came from

**Axis 1 · 4 → 3.** *Mine picked a Tuesday* became *Djamela's picked a Tuesday.* The possessive is clumsy where the original was a blade. Third person cost the second sentence of the story.

**Axis 2 · 4 → 3.** The FID holds most of the time, but the sympathy passages drift toward explanation — *something had been taken out of her without her noticing it go* is the narrator telling you the diagnosis. Draft 3 never does that.

**Axis 6 · 4 → 2.** 4,961 words against ~3,570, and the same move — *she tries to feel and cannot* — used four times. Band 3 is *nothing wasted.* This wastes.

**Axis 7 · 3 → 4.** The one real gain. Deleting *I am not asking to be forgiven* lifts the hard cap. The idea now rides on the arithmetic rhyme: thirty-four she cannot make smaller at the gate, thirty-four coming out even at the station door.

> **Net: −3 against Draft 4.** The sympathy rewrite scores *worse* on this instrument than the story it was trying to improve.

### Proposed revision to *Dite* — Chair axis 5, not the Nine Axes

The vault has ***Dite*** at **3 on The World.** The [[Dite — MARGINALIA (hand pass)|marginalia pass]] turned up more surplus than that band allows: Ahmad and the waistband, Preeti and Sarah-Anne crushing lice for half an hour, Guru Manikam arriving unannounced and never returning, Monsieur Hassan's clownish face, the *boutikier sinwa* with the tattered notebook, Vandana's mother. **Guru Manikam is a whole predator crossing the frame and leaving** — the Pinto move. That is band 4 at least. **Proposed: 3 → 4.** Author's call; I have not edited note 17.

### 🔴 The finding, and it repeats note 17 exactly

> **`chantal-3rd` moved the Chair Test by ZERO. Seventeen, the same as Draft 4.**

Note 17 found that three hand-written insertions moved the Nine Axes +1 and the Chair Test 0. **A 1,391-word sympathy rewrite has now done the same thing: 0.**

That is not a coincidence and it is the most useful line on this page. **The Person (3) and The World (2) do not move for interiority work of any kind** — not insertions, not free indirect discourse, not sympathy engines. They are the two axes holding five of the six points between Chantal and the confirmed winner, and every technique tried so far has aimed somewhere else.

**Sympathy was the wrong target. Or rather: sympathy was the right target and interiority was the wrong instrument for it.**

### Why mihogoni-2 scores 22 on an instrument Chantal scores 17 on

It is not better written. It buys the two hard axes structurally, for free:

| | Why the object-chorus gets it cheaply |
| --- | --- |
| **The Person · 4** | A narrator with no stake has no reason to prune. The old man who comes alongside, sits, says nothing and goes. Trésor reaching into water for a thing that was not there |
| **The World · 4** | Cormorants that avoid the fourth ship and do not spread into the empty space afterwards. The hole shaped like a hand. Bahati's smoke sideways then straight up. **The girl who counts and then stops coming** |

**A narrator that cannot act can only notice.** Noticing is exactly what axes 4 and 5 measure. That is the whole trick, and it is available to Chantal only in fragments because Djamela is inside the plot and everything she notices is *hers*.

### Where I think I have over-scored my own work

- **mihogoni-2 · Voice 5.** It meets the written test — a signature sentence (*Rust is not fast. It is arithmetic.*) inside the first 10%. But the shore-description paragraphs are flatter and more generic than that test can see. **Score it 4 and I would not argue.**
- **mihogoni-2 · Nine Axes 3 · Character interiority 3.** Marked down deliberately. Kasongo has no interior life on the page and neither does his son. A panel could read the whole thing as **frigidity** in Gardner's sense — withheld feeling passed off as restraint. That is the real risk of the technique and the instrument barely registers it.
- **The Position · 4, not 5.** I wanted to give it 5. The band forbids it: *object-narrator* is named in band 4 as a proxy device. Same ceiling as *Dite*'s epigraphs. The instrument caught me.

## What this actually says

**1 · Do not port `chantal-3rd` into the draft.** It scores −3 on one instrument and ±0 on the other, and it spends 1,391 words doing it. **One passage is worth taking**: the corridor, where the count is lost and retrieved and lands on thirty-four. In first person it costs perhaps 150 words.

**2 · The Person and The World are still untouched after three separate attempts.** Insertions, FID, sympathy. All aimed at interiority. **The next attempt must aim at other people** — a minor character with a want the plot does not need, and one who refuses their category. That is Atelier days 17 and 18, and neither is a prose problem.

**3 · A high Nine Axes score means less than it looks.** *Dite* takes 43 and its standing is unverified; the confirmed overall winner takes 31. **This instrument is good at finding what is broken and poor at predicting what places** — the note's own warning, and it applies to `mihogoni-2`'s 37 more than to anything else here.

**4 · Every number on this page is missing axis 6.** The largest single component of what a chair means is blank in all five columns because there is no cold reader. **That is the finding that outranks the rest of the page.**
