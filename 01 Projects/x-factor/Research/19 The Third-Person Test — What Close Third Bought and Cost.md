# 19 · THE THIRD-PERSON TEST
### What close third bought, what it cost, and what turned out not to depend on it

> [!danger] x-factor · analysis of AI drafts
> The two files under test are Claude's, written as experiments. **Nothing here crosses into `01 Story Brain`.** What is transferable is named at the foot of this note, and it is device shapes, never sentences.

---

## The question

[[mihogoni-3f|SHEMEJI]] moved the Chantal material into close third and came back with more world than any first-person version had carried: a ship's papers in four languages, Article 15, the railway that ends, the crossing to Uvira, a paragraph of surplus that goes nowhere.

The obvious reading is that **close third bought atmosphere and authority.** That reading is wrong, and the test says so.

**Hypothesis:** the devices are independent of person and survive a flip to first person unchanged.

---

## The design

One variable. [[mihogoni-3g]] is 3f with the grammatical person changed and **nothing else** — no scene moved, no image improved, no known fault repaired. Any difference between the two files is attributable to person and to nothing else.

**Measured the same way on both**, opening epigraph to last line, appendices excluded:

| | Prose words | Sentences that changed for reasons other than person |
| --- | --: | --: |
| **3f** — close third | 4,482 | — |
| **3g** — first person | 4,483 | **0** |

A one-word difference across a whole story. **The flip is length-neutral**, which is the evidence that nothing was quietly rewritten while the pronouns moved.

---

## Result — five of seven devices were free of person

| Device | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| **The four ship epigraphs** — one hull, four flags | **Held completely.** Extra-diegetic; they belong to the document, not the narrator, exactly as *Dite*'s recipes do |
| **The after-deck clause** — *Eingeborene* → *les indigènes* → *deck passengers to provide their own covering* → *staha* | **Held, and improved.** See below |
| **Article 15** | **Held.** Spoken aloud by a character; person cannot reach it |
| **The surplus paragraph** — the Lukuga bridge, the left-foot shoes, the tailor's photograph | **Held, marginally better.** A girl who counts everything is the right narrator for an inventory of irrelevant true things |
| ***Shemeji* as the last word** | **Held.** Placement does the work |
| **The language stratification** — four paragraphs on French / Lingala / Swahili / English | **BROKE** |
| **The father teaching Patrice at the water** | **BROKE** |

### The one that got better

The after-deck clause. In third person the reader watches the tariff land on her. In first person **she rides that deck and reports it without ever connecting it to the papers printed above her head** — the naive-narrator engine, running on the strongest material in the story. Same words. The flip is what activated it.

---

## The two failures, and they are the same failure

**1 · The language stratification.** In third person, *French is what things are written in… Lingala… is spoken over the top of you rather than to you* reads as a narrating intelligence describing a town. In first person it is a thirteen-year-old delivering four paragraphs of sociolinguistics about her own home, and there is nowhere for it to hide.

It was already flagged as the story explaining its own world. **The test upgrades that from a stylistic complaint to a structural fault**: the passage was never doing work, it was being *covered*. Everything in it is dramatised later and better — the police station runs French, Swahili and Lingala in one paragraph of action, the deck runs Swahili and English in one sentence.

**2 · The father teaching at the water.** The lesson is spoken, so the lines survive. What does not survive is who is listening. Djamela is on the porch, *nearer the house than the water* — out of earshot of a quiet man teaching a boy — and the scene is reported in detail she cannot have. The flip turns *he had a listener and it was not me* from a good line into an admission that the six paragraphs before it are impossible.

**Both failures are one failure. Close third was buying access the narrator does not have** — to a lecture she would not deliver, and to a conversation she could not hear.

---

## The finding

> **Close third does not buy atmosphere or authority. It buys reach — and it does not itemise the bill.**

Neither cost was visible from inside 3f. Both became obvious the moment the person changed, because first person prices access line by line and third person does not.

The corollary matters more than the finding: **reach is the thing a story about a girl who is systematically kept out of rooms should probably not have.** Her exclusion from the police station's Lingala, from her father's teaching, from what the ship's papers say about the deck she is sitting on — that exclusion *is* the story. A narrating position that can see into all three rooms is working against the material it is describing.

**So the third-person experiment did not fail. It returned a negative result cleanly, which is what it was for.**

---

## What is transferable, and what is not

**Shapes only. No sentences.** Nothing in either file crosses into the manuscript; if any of this is used it gets rebuilt from scratch, the way *shemeji* and the counting failure were.

**Portable, and none of it needs third person:**
- **An extra-diegetic epigraph series over one object.** One thing, several registers, in sequence, where the sequence is the argument and no line of prose states it.
- **A clause that survives every register and quietly changes category** — the strongest single device in either file, and it costs nothing but placement.
- **A bureaucratic joke a character delivers aloud**, plus somebody laughing half a second late.
- **A closing inventory of true things that serve nothing**, ending on a flat denial that they are relevant. → the Atelier's [[The Three-Week Atelier|Day 18]] instruction, *install six, at least one a person whose life the story does not follow.*
- **An untranslated word held back and spent once, last**, its meaning delivered by staging rather than gloss.

**Not portable:**
- The register itself. Djamela's coldness only reads as her temperature when she is the one saying it; reported, it becomes an assessment of her.
- Anything requiring her to know what she cannot know, or to hear what she cannot hear. **That is the whole content of this finding.**
