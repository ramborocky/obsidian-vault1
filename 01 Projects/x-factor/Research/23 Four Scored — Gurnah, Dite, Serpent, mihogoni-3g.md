### Why 44, axis by axis where it is not obvious

**1 · Opening authority · 5.** *'My mother lived on a farm in Africa,' she heard her daughter Khadija say.* The band reads *first line is the best line* — here the first line is the title, the engine, the thing the story spends seven pages arguing with, and **the last line's target.** It is also overheard rather than spoken, which is the story's whole method installed in eleven words.

**4 · Scene turn · 5, and this is the score I checked hardest.** Eight scenes, eight turns: the kitchen (rage → regret → guilt) · the arrangements (*a few days off school* → weeks) · arrival (fear of the country silence → a dull routine she found surprisingly pleasant) · the shop under the mango tree (Issa) · night one (**the mound of hard earth arrives the next morning, after the fact**) · night two (she shuts both shutters; *don't hide from me*) · Amina (**removal within the hour, and the lie to Omar**) · the kitchen again. **Not one of those turns is the expected one.**

**6 · Structural economy · 4 — the only dock, and I would not fight a reader who said 5.** The Jamal paragraph is the one cuttable thing on the page. It earns its place (Kadi's forgetting is the *second* forgetting; the stories were already lost to the elder child) but the story would survive without it. Band 5 is *cutting any scene breaks two others*, and this breaks one.

**7 · Controlling idea · 5 — retracted from 1, and the correction is the point of this page.**

Band 1 reads *absent **or stated***. Note 21 said Gurnah states his. **The text says otherwise, twice over:**

> *They were fourteen years old, and she would have embarrassed them, and Kadi most of all, **if she had gone in there and told them** that the farm she lived in was nothing like that make-believe luxury… that it was not in Africa, but in a real place with names for everything, from the smell of grass and leaves to the smallest change in the weather.*

**It is a conditional. She never says it.** In a story where the aunt says *don't say anything to Omar*, then lies; where Munah never tells anyone what happened at the window; where the daughter is never told — **the one passage that looks like a statement is itself a refusal to speak.** Gurnah put his position in the mouth of a speech that does not happen.

And the Blixen rebuttal — *she was the subject people, subject to life and to others, sent from here to there and back by those who loved her and owned her* — **is not the controlling idea.** It is the decoy. The controlling idea is never stated by anyone: **the people who love you deliver you to harm, and then remove the evidence to keep a friendship, and afterwards nobody can be bothered to know.** Band 5: *never stated, impossible to miss.*

**9 · Something to say · 5.** The reader is set up for a story about colonial representation and handed a story about a girl removed from a house within the hour so that two men's friendship survives. **Not dismissible, and not expected.**

> [!important] 44 / 45 is the highest score this board has ever returned, and that is a result about the instrument
> [[The Judging Rubric — Nine Axes|The Nine Axes]] scored the 2026 Commonwealth winner at **31** and warned it *"over-weights architecture relative to the panel it was built to model."* It has now scored the best story in a British Council anthology at **44**.
>
> **Both results are the same finding from opposite ends: this rubric measures architecture very well.** Gurnah is architecturally near-perfect and it sees that. Nazir is architecturally loose and won anyway. **The instrument is not broken and it is not a predictor. Use it to find what is broken, exactly as its own callout says.**

**1 · The Move · 5.** Point at it: **~82%.** *The next morning she went to look outside and saw that there was a small mound of hard earth that he would have stood on to look in.* The barred window was given forty per cent earlier as room description — *the small window was barred and looked away from the stream, up the slope towards a grove of banana trees.* **The move lands on material the reader was handed for another reason.** That is band 5's exact wording, and it is the cleanest example of it in the whole calibration set.

**3 · The Position · 5, retracted from 3.** The instrument's test is two-part: *state the position, then find where the story says it.* Stated above; **it is nowhere in the text.** It is enacted — *don't say anything to Omar* · *I just forgot that I'd promised to take her back today* — and never said. The earlier 3 came from searching for the **decoy** and finding it, which is precisely the failure mode band 3 is designed to catch. **The test worked; I ran it on the wrong sentence.**

**4 · The Person · 5.** Two characters refuse their category. **Aunt Amina** protects the man's reputation *and* gets the girl out within the hour — she is neither ally nor villain, and *just looked sad, as if Munah had given her news of a terrible loss.* **Issa** is a predator at a barred window who has a wife, children, a trip to Pemba, and an aunt who *sometimes called him son.*

**5 · The World · 5.** Twelve surplus items survive the cut test. The band-5 one: ***'It was video in their house anyway, DVD in the others.'*** Eleven words carrying the entire class position of an immigrant household in England, serving **nothing whatever** — and a strong candidate for the most memorable sentence on the page.

### ⚠️ 2 · The Voice · 4 — and this axis cannot score plainness

Band 5 requires the signature sentence **inside the first 10%**. Gurnah's is *she was the subject people, subject to life and to others, sent from here to there and back by those who loved her and owned her* — at **~19%**. Band 5 is unreachable by nineteen per cent.

Worse, band 4's blind-paragraph test is genuinely borderline for him. Take a middle paragraph at random — *In the day, she was expected to stay close to Aunt Amina, and wait for chores to be given to her* — and it could be anybody's. **His plainness is a deliberate refusal of signature, and the axis reads that as a deficiency.**

> ***Dite* scores 5 here and Gurnah scores 4.** An axis on which the anthology's best story loses a point to a story of unknown standing, for being less quotable in its first ten per cent, is measuring **showiness with a stopwatch on it.** → [[15 The Chair Test — Scoring the Hidden Factor]], limitation to add.

### And the rest of the instrument is corroborated

**Gurnah 24 · Lubwama 24 · Nazir 22 · everything else 21.** The Chair Test now separates a **four-point validated band** from the vault's own experiments, on three external points instead of two. That is the strongest thing anyone has been able to say about it.

**But 3g still returned 21 — for the fifth time**, this pass through a complete flip of grammatical person. [[20 mihogoni-3f Scored — The Chain and the Board|Note 20]]'s escape hatch was *"it sorts narrator strategies, not drafts."* **Person is a narrator strategy. It did not move.** Limitation 1 should be hardened: above ~20 the instrument has resolution *between stories* and none *within one*.

**G1 · 5.** A fourteen-year-old's pleasure at a thirty-year-old man's attention is put on the page with no safety net — *her body became heated under his scrutiny and his stolen glances* · *she smiled back and looked away, pleased* — and the story neither punishes nor absolves her for it. **The risk is what makes the story necessary**, because without it the window scene is melodrama.

**G3 · 5.** Both hinges are unmarked. Kitchen → farm is *Memories troubled her. She could not forget.* then straight into *The arrangements were made in her earshot.* Farm → kitchen is *Munah heard Kadi calling for her.* **No "she remembered", no white space doing the work. The reader is across before noticing.**

**G2 · 4, and it is the one I would argue about.** Munah is flawed and never redeemed — band 3 — but band 5 needs the *reader* implicated. The opening does gesture at it: the story you are consuming is somebody's entertainment, and the last line says so. **I scored it short of 5 because the reader is positioned with Munah against Kadi, not alongside Kadi.** A reader who thinks the *Out of Africa* frame implicates them too would be right to say 5.

## WHAT THIS SAYS

**1 · The method finding, and it is the one to keep.** This page was first written off a good structural summary and **got the single most important thing wrong — whether a sentence is spoken.** Note 21 recorded architecture accurately: two panels, the hinge, the mound arriving as evidence, the ending as speech. All correct. But *"Gurnah states his position"* missed a conditional, and one missed conditional moved **two axes by four points each and produced a false headline about the instrument.**

> **Architecture survives a summary. Grammar does not.** No axis that depends on whether a thing was said, by whom, in what mood, may ever be scored from second-hand description again. → append to [[The Three Gates — Unified Instrument]].

**2 · The Nine Axes are vindicated, not indicted.** 44 for Gurnah and 31 for the Commonwealth winner are the same finding from both ends: **it measures architecture accurately and does not predict panels.** Its own calibration callout already says exactly this. **Nothing needs changing in axis 7's band text** — the retracted proposal is withdrawn.

**3 · The Chair Test gets its third external point and its clearest weakness.** Gurnah 24, Lubwama 24, Nazir 22, everything else 21 — a real four-point band. **And the Voice axis cannot give a 5 to plainness**, because band 5 is a stopwatch on the first ten per cent. Gurnah's signature sentence is at 19% and *Dite* outscores him there. Add it to the limitations.

**4 · 🔴 The short-sentence target is a panel preference and should stop being treated as a craft standard.** Gurnah runs 17.0 with 26% short. **Chantal at 14.9 / 47% is already more "Commonwealth-shaped" than the best story in NW14.** Note 22 predicted this from Makumbi's statement; it is now measured. **Stop spending passes on it.**

**5 · Nothing here changes the manuscript.** 3g fails 1b. The portable findings are a scoring rule (never score grammar from a summary), a limitation (Voice band 5 vs plainness), and a retraction (the short-sentence target).
