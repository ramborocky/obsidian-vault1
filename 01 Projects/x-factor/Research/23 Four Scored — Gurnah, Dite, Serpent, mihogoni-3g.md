### The three that moved on 3g — and all three follow from the flip

**Prose control 4 → 3.** The language-stratification block — *French is what things are written in… Lingala comes from Kinshasa… it is spoken over the top of you rather than to you* — is now a thirteen-year-old delivering four paragraphs of sociolinguistics about her own street. Band 3 is *clean, occasional spike*; this is longer than a spike and the vocabulary is not hers. **3g's own appendix calls this finding 6 and it is right; this is what it costs on the board.**

**Structural economy 4 → 3.** Two passages no longer earn their place — the same block, now redundant *and* implausible, and the father's teaching scene, reported at length in detail Djamela cannot have from the porch. Band 3 is *nothing wasted.* Two wasted passages is band 2 territory and 3 is generous.

**Character interiority 4 → 5, and the file misses this.** Band 5 is **the reader knows what the narrator won't say.** 3g does it twice, on the record: *I am not going to describe the hotel* → the inventory instead. *I am not going to describe that either.* A narrator who substitutes a list for the scene and counts a kiss at twenty seconds is band 5 by definition.

> **3g's appendix scored seven devices and found five held, two broke. It did not score the axes.** Had it done so it would have found the flip also *bought* something — and interiority is the axis Chantal has never got above 4 on. **This is the first point the experiment chain has gained anywhere on axis 3.**

### ⚠️ And the Gurnah result, which is the reason to write this note

**Axis 7 · Controlling idea · 1.**

Band 1 is *"absent **or stated**."* [[21 NW14 — The Editors' Statement and Three Stories|Note 21]] records that Gurnah **states his position**, once, mid-story, in free indirect — not "Africa" but *a real place with names for everything, from the smell of grass and leaves to the smallest change in the weather.*

By the letter of [[The Judging Rubric — Nine Axes|the instrument]] that is a 1. And the instrument's own rule is explicit: ***a single 1 anywhere caps the story regardless of total.***

> **So the Nine Axes eliminate the best story in a British Council anthology of a thousand submissions, sifted by a Caine Prize winner — on the axis the story is strongest on.** Note 21 already proposed the repair: *a statement survives when it is a character's argument with someone else rather than the author's summary for the reader.* Gurnah's clause is a rebuttal of a Blixen film playing in the next room. It is not a summary for anybody.
>
> **Under the repaired band it scores 4** — a real position, delivered against a named counter-text and dropped. Total on the seven scoreable axes becomes **33 / 35**.
>
> **This is the second time this vault has caught the Nine Axes mispricing a story that actually placed.** The first was the Serpent at 31/45. → the calibration callout on [[The Judging Rubric — Nine Axes]] needs this appended.

**Why the other Gurnah numbers are safe to give.** Interiority 5, the gap 5, close 5 and something-to-say 5 all rest on structural facts note 21 records directly: the assault never named and arriving afterwards as *a small mound of hard earth*; the daughter's closing *I was only winding them up* against a mother who has just relived it, **with nobody in the story noticing the gap**; the aunt telling her to say nothing to Omar and then lying to the uncle. Those are architecture, and architecture survives summary. **Sentences do not, so axes 1 and 2 stay blank.**

### 🔴 21. For the fifth time. And this one kills the escape hatch.

[[20 mihogoni-3f Scored — The Chain and the Board|Note 20]] recorded **21 four passes running** and offered a way out:

> *"Stop using the Chair Test to choose between versions of the same story. It sorts narrator strategies. It does not sort drafts."*

**3g changes the narrator strategy.** Grammatical person is the largest single variable available in the whole chain — it is the one thing the entire seventh pass was built to isolate, and it demonstrably moved two of seven devices and three of nine axes.

**The Chair Test returned 21.**

> **So the instrument does not sort narrator strategies either.** It moved 17 → 21 once, when the narrator changed *identity* (Chantal → a concealed narrator), and has not moved since through: five epigraphs, four stratified languages, Article 15, a Faulkner pass, nineteen sentence splits, a new last word, **and now a complete flip of person.**
>
> **Limitation 1 needs hardening, not appending.** It currently says anything above ~20 should be read as *in the room* rather than as a ranking. The honest version is stronger: **above ~20 this instrument has no resolution at all.** Five identical totals across changes this large is not a coarse instrument, it is a saturated one. → [[15 The Chair Test — Scoring the Hidden Factor]]

**The Voice · 4, and the two effects cancelled exactly.** Band 5 needs *the signature sentence inside the first 10%*. 3g has one now and 3f did not — ***Nobody tells you the exact day your body stops being only yours. Mine picked a Tuesday.*** That is band 5's requirement, met on line one. **But band 5 is cumulative and band 4 is the blind-paragraph test**, which the sociolinguistics block fails outright — it could have come from anybody. The flip bought the 5 condition and broke the 4 condition in the same move. **Fix the block and this is a 5.** That is the cheapest point on this page.

## WHAT THIS SAYS

**1 · The Nine Axes would eliminate Gurnah, and the fix is already written.** Axis 7 band 1 reads *absent or stated*, and a single 1 caps the story. Note 21's repair — **statement-as-rebuttal survives; statement-as-summary does not** — should stop being a marginal observation and go into the band. Under it Gurnah is a 4 on that axis and the anthology's best story stops being capped by our instrument. **Second confirmed misprice, after the Serpent at 31.**

**2 · The Chair Test is saturated, not coarse.** Five passes at 21, the last of them through a full change of grammatical person. Its own escape clause — *it sorts narrator strategies* — is now falsified by the cleanest narrator-strategy experiment in the chain. **Stop running it on this material.** It separated Chantal's 16 from the prize band's 21–24 once, usefully, and it has told us nothing since.

**3 · The flip cost one point and changed what the score is made of.** 39 → 38: prose control and economy each down one, **interiority up to 5 — the chain's first 5 on that axis.** Both losses are the two devices 3g's own appendix already identified, so the board and the appendix agree, which is the first time two instruments in this chain have corroborated each other rather than argued.

**4 · One point is sitting on the floor.** Cut or shrink the language-stratification block and 3g takes **prose control back to 4, economy to 4, and the Chair Test's Voice to 5** — three points from one deletion, and the block's content is already delivered better twice over: the police station does French, Swahili and Lingala in a single paragraph of action, and the deck does Swahili and English in one sentence.

**5 · Nothing here changes the manuscript.** 3g fails 1b. **The transferable finding is a rubric repair, not prose:** axis 7's band text, and a harder limitation 1 on the Chair Test.
