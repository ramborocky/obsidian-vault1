**Opening authority — 4.** *Sun on galvanise is a cruel instrument.* A voice arrives fully formed in eight words. Not a 5 only because the best sentences come later.

**Prose control — 4.** Genuinely fine at sentence level and better than either placed story. Loses the fifth point to **figure density** — almost every sentence carries a simile or personification, which [[Three Faults of Soul — Sentimentality, Frigidity, Mannerism|Gardner would call mannerism]] — and to the coda, which abandons narration for incantation.

**The gap — 3, and this is the sheet's real criticism.** He plans a murder. **He never attempts it.** The plank gives way on its own, and he arrives to find the rescue already underway. **The story arranges for him to be spared the act**, so his turn costs him only an intention. A wider gap — and a harder story — has him push, or reach the well first. See [[Delay and the Anguish of Choice]].

**Structural economy — 2.** The tail above. Four separate endings: the rescue, *Plant it by the door*, Puttie's daughter at 92.4%, and the coda at 95.8%.

**Controlling idea — 3.** Genuinely implied and dramatised for 95% of the story — **and then stated outright** in the coda: *the serpent… was never only a snake.* [[Writing That Learns, Not Teaches|Kiteley's distinction]] exactly, and Giorgis loses the same point the same way.

**Resonant close — 3.** *You talk soft so it can talk soft back* is a 5-scoring close. **The story then continues for twenty more lines.** The final image genuinely resonates, but the reader has to walk through a sermon to reach it.

## 🔧 COMPETENCE — **Y** · hypothesis now 3 for 5

**The first unqualified `Y` from an outside writer**, and it appears twice.

- **Vishnu knows his land as economics, not scenery:** the price of rice in the shop, what buyers give for wet cocoa, and how the distance between the two leaves a man short. That is a farmer's arithmetic, and it is the only place the story explains his poverty rather than describing it.
- **Marsha at the critical moment:** she tears vine from a mango trunk and **peels it in her hands to feel whether it will hold a woman.** Procedure, under pressure, in one clause. It is why the rescue is credible.

Running tally: Giorgis **N** · Uwazuruike **N** · Baldwin **Y (qualified)** · Beat of My Hurt **Y** · Nazir **Y**. **The kill criterion — N eight times in ten — will not now be met.** The hypothesis survives.
