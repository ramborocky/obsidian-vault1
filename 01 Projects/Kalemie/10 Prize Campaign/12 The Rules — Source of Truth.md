## 🔴 THE ONE THAT MATTERS — AI, AND WHAT IT MEANS FOR THIS VAULT

The 22 June statement records that the Foundation **investigated allegations that AI was used to write winning stories.** How they investigated:

> They examined *"working drafts, time-stamped documents and notes"* and concluded: *"we are satisfied that AI was not used to write the winning stories."*

Two facts sit beside that, and they are not comfortable.

**Fact 1 — the rules.** Section 3 of the entry rules, verbatim:

> **a.** Entries, including those in translation, **must be made by the original author.**
> **e.** The story **must be the entrant's own work.**

**Fact 2 — what this vault records.** [[02 Comparison Board]], row 3, labels [[The Beat of My Hurt (Draft 4 — Free)|Beat of My Hurt Draft 4]] as **"(Claude's)"**. Chantal D3 is labelled **"(ours)"**.

> [!danger] I am withdrawing the advice I gave in [[10 The Three Moves — Reader, Winners, Record|Move 3]]
> This morning I told you to fix the currency line and send Beat of My Hurt to a smaller market. **That advice was given before I had read these rules and I am retracting it.**
>
> A story this vault's own records attribute to Claude is not *the entrant's own work* under rule 3e, and almost every literary magazine has an equivalent term. Submitting it would not be a grey area.
>
> **This is not a judgement about what actually happened** — I was not there, the label may be shorthand for something else, and only you know how those drafts were made. **It is a statement about what the rules require and what your own files currently say.** Those two things need to agree before anything is sent anywhere.

**What you need to decide, and only you can:** for each draft, who wrote the sentences. Not who suggested, diagnosed, scored or restructured — **who wrote them.** If the answer for any draft is "Claude", that draft cannot be entered, and no amount of revision changes its origin.

**If the answer for Chantal is you** — and the vault's own labelling suggests it is — then the evidence the Foundation looked for is evidence you already have and most entrants do not:

| Evidence | Where you have it |
| --- | --- |
| Working drafts | Four dated Chantal drafts in `01 Story Brain/` |
| Time-stamped documents | Git history, dated frontmatter on every file |
| Notes | The entire Learning Curve, the Craft Log, the Prize Campaign |

**Keep the git history clean and keep committing.** It is the single best answer to a question you may never be asked — and if you are asked, you want it to already exist rather than be assembled afterwards.

## The rules that change the plan

**Only one entry per writer per year.** Rule 3d. Chantal versus Beat of My Hurt was never a comparison — **it is a single choice, once a year**, and [[08 Three Gates — Field Comparison]] plus the AI question above settle it.

**Entries previously submitted are not eligible.** Rule 3g. One shot per story, forever. There is no "try again next year" with Chantal.

**Simultaneous submissions are allowed** — rule 3i — *provided* you notify `creatives@commonwealthfoundation.com` immediately if the story is accepted elsewhere or wins another prize.

**Must remain unpublished until 1 May** following the cycle. Personal blogs, personal websites and personal Facebook pages do not count as published. Rule 3f.

**Shortlisting elsewhere is fine.** Rule 2i — if it has been shortlisted or placed in another competition and was never *published*, it is still eligible.

**Swahili is an accepted language.** Rule 3h lists Kiswahili among the languages accepted where the writer has no English translation. Not relevant for Chantal, worth knowing for the cycle.

**Citizenship is verified before winners are selected.** Rule 2a. Judged by country of citizenship — Tanzania sits in the **Africa** region.

## The scale — worth knowing before you hope

**More than 7,800 writers entered in 2026.** Five winners, one per region. Each winning story was read by *"at least seven people, through multiple rounds"*, and the longlist is assisted by experienced readers before the named judges see anything.

Africa is one of five regions. This is not a reason to soften the work — it is a reason to keep [[10 The Three Moves — Reader, Winners, Record|Move 3]] alive in some legitimate form, because one story on one door once a year is a fragile plan regardless of quality.
