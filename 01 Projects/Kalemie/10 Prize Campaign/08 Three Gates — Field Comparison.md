## Gate 1 — the instrument was wrong, and this comparison broke it

[[The Three Gates — Unified Instrument|As written yesterday]], Gate 1 puts the 2,000–5,000 word ceiling in the same pass/fail list as spelling, tense control and cohesion. **Run against the field, that collapses immediately.**

Baldwin fails Gate 1 at roughly 12,000 words and is the best story on the board by six points. Giorgis fails it at 5,179 and placed. Neither failure says anything about the writing.

**Two different things were being called one gate:**

| | What it measures | What a failure means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1a · Hygiene** | Spelling, sentence structure, name consistency, tense, cohesion, punctuation system, paragraphing | The prose is broken. Fix before scoring anything |
| **1b · Eligibility** | Word ceiling, genre, deadline | Wrong door. Says nothing about quality |

All four stories pass 1a. Two fail 1b, and both failures are administrative. **Chantal and Uwazuruike are the only two stories here that could be posted to the Commonwealth at all** — which is the entire practical content of Gate 1b and the reason it should never have been mixed into a quality check.

Fix applied to the instrument. The Gate 1 table there now splits.

### Baldwin — 25/25, and why it is not a formality

A narrator who broke a promise to his dying mother, married two days later, and forgot it until her funeral — and the story never forgives him for it. **G2 is the whole engine:** the reader spends thirty pages agreeing with the sensible brother and discovers at the bandstand that they were the ones not listening. That is complicity, not sympathy.

**G3 at 5 is the one to study.** The Grace detonation at ~64% is followed one point later by the five-word hinge, and the reader is inside the shift before registering that a shift happened. The story does not announce that the narrator has begun to hear. It simply starts hearing, and the reader is already there.

### Chantal — 23/25, losing on the slide

Confirmed at 3 against the field, and the reason holds: a voice that declares its own retrospection cannot slide. **Uwazuruike scores the same 3 for a different reason** — its 97% reversal is a hard turn delivered in reported speech, not a slide either — and Giorgis scores 2 because the narration stays outside its characters throughout.

**So three of four stories on this board score 2–3 on the slide, and the one that scores 5 is the best story measured.** That is the strongest single argument this comparison produces for treating G3 as a real technique rather than a taste.

### Giorgis — 14/25, and the lowest score is G2

*He knew she would be enraged at the sight of him. He knew she had a right to be.* [[Giorgis — A Double-Edged Inheritance|The sheet]] already names what that sentence does: it hands down a moral ruling and relieves the reader of deciding. That is the exact inverse of ugly honesty. Characters are installed as good or bad rather than discovered, and a reader who is told the verdict cannot be made complicit in it.

**It placed anyway, on architecture.** Which is the point of having Gate 2 and Gate 3 separate.

## ⚠️ The finding that matters, and it is against us

**Chantal scores 63/70 and sits above both placed stories. Do not celebrate this.**

Two explanations, and they are not exclusive:

**1 · Gate 3 may be rewarding subject matter rather than execution.** Chantal's G1 and G2 both score 5. Its material — a thirteen-year-old narrating her own commodification and refusing to ask for pity — is inherently higher-risk than a coming-of-age betrayal. A rubric that scores *danger* will hand marks to the more dangerous subject regardless of how well it is handled.

[[The Judging Rubric — Nine Axes|The Nine Axes]] already names this trap in its own words: *having an important subject is not having something to say.* **Gate 3 as built has no defence against it**, and Chantal is exactly the kind of story that would exploit the hole.

The counter-evidence is Baldwin, whose subject is a brother and a piano and who scores 25/25 on execution alone. So the criterion *can* measure craft. It is the Chantal-versus-Uwazuruike gap — 23 to 18 — where subject weight is doing work the rubric cannot separate out.

> **Corrective test, to run before these numbers are used for anything:** re-score Gate 3 asking of every criterion *is this the execution or is this the material?* If Chantal's G1 and G2 both drop a point under that question, the gap closes to 21–18 and the instrument needs the question written into it permanently.

**2 · The instrument has no weighting, and [[02 Comparison Board|the Comparison Board]] does.** The Board's central finding is that these panels forgive architecture and do not forgive voice — and that **Chantal is the inverse shape, strong exactly where the panel forgives.** My Gate 2 total cannot see that. It adds axis 6 and axis 2 as though a point of each were worth the same to a judge, and on the Board's own evidence they are not.

> [!danger] The two instruments disagree, and the Board is more likely to be right
> Three Gates says Chantal outranks both placed stories by 12 and 16 points.
> The Comparison Board says Chantal's strength sits in the column panels forgive, and its two weakest axes sit in the column they do not.
> **These cannot both be good news.** The Board is built on measured evidence from stories that actually placed. Three Gates is built on reasoning. Where they conflict, follow the Board.

## What this changes

- **Gate 1 splits into hygiene and eligibility.** Applied to [[The Three Gates — Unified Instrument]].
- **Gate 3 needs the execution-versus-material question written into it.** Not yet applied — it needs the corrective re-score first.
- **Chantal's slide score of 3 is now field-validated as normal**, not as a fault. Three of four stories sit at 2–3. Only Baldwin clears it, and Baldwin is the ceiling, not the bar. **Nothing should be rewritten to chase this.**
- **G2 is the criterion with the widest spread on the board** — 5, 5, 3, 2. It separates these four stories better than any single axis in Gate 2 does. That makes it the most informative criterion in the whole merged instrument and the one most worth testing against stories 4–10.
- **Two of the four stories here cannot be entered for the Commonwealth at all.** Worth holding on to whenever a technique is imported from Baldwin: he is not solving the problem Chantal has to solve.
