## ⭐ THE FINDING — six out of six, and nobody planned it

**[[What You Are Censoring|Kiteley: every writer censors one major thing]]. Every story on this board withholds one central fact and never releases it.** Not delayed — *never given.*

| Story | The unreleased fact |
| --- | --- |
| **Baldwin** | Whether Sonny stays clean. Sonny forecloses it himself — *it can come again*, said twice |
| **Uwazuruike** | Whether anybody actually died. The boy reaches the mortuary and never goes in |
| **Giorgis** | That the car accident was murder. Stated nowhere; the reader converts it across 37% of the story |
| **Chantal** | The hotel. *I am not going to describe the hotel* — the refusal is on the page |
| **Found Things** | How Djamela died |
| **Beat of My Hurt** | What killed the Ngoie boy — and the narrator's mother is never named once |
| **Nazir** | What Sita knows. She sends the child away, and **never accuses him once** |
| ~~Master draft~~ | ~~Nothing. Every clue is delivered and explained~~ — and it scores 24 |

**Seven for seven among the finished stories, and the one that withholds nothing is the one that fails.** This is the strongest empirical result anywhere in this vault. It is not a technique to consider. It is the entry condition.

**The corollary that makes it usable:** the withheld fact must be *load-bearing* — the thing the reader most wants. Withholding something trivial is coyness. See [[The Art of Omission — Unity, Padding, and Proportion|Barrett on omission]] and [[Reveal Answers Slowly|Cleland]].

## Structure — McKee

### [[The Inciting Incident]] — where the story actually starts

| Story | Position | What |
| --- | --: | --- |
| **Chantal** | **~0%** | *Mine picked a Tuesday.* Sentence one |
| **Beat** | ~25% | Beat four requested for a twenty-four-year-old |
| **Found Things** | ~2% | The dala dala is not at the stop |
| **Baldwin** | ~3% | The arrest, read in a newspaper |
| **Master** | ~5% | Morisho taken |
| **Uwazuruike** | **19.7%** | The first gunshot |
| **Giorgis** | 20.6% | *Or else I will* |

**Both placed stories start latest**, and Uwazuruike scored **2/5** on opening authority for it — *a fifth of a prize story spent clearing the throat* — and placed anyway. Against [[The Beginning — Three Paragraphs at Most|Barrett's three paragraphs]] and [[The First-Page Cut]], this is the clearest evidence that **panels forgive a slow opening.** They do not forgive a flat voice.

### [[Controlling Idea]] — the position, not the subject

| Story | The idea, in a sentence |
| --- | --- |
| **Baldwin** | *My trouble made his real.* Five words at 65%, and the story turns on them |
| **Kalemie** *(all drafts)* | Inaction is complicity |
| **Found Things** | Noticing and looking are opposite activities |
| **Beat** | The craft that comforts is the craft that lies |
| **Giorgis** | Inherited violence — but **stated before it is dramatised**, which is the fault |

**Baldwin's is a sentence in the text. Giorgis's is a sentence in the narration.** That difference is the whole of [[Writing That Learns, Not Teaches|Kiteley's learning-not-teaching]] and worth more than any definition of controlling idea.

### [[Active Vs Passive Protagonists.md|Active vs passive]] + [[Working Backward from the Climax — the Victim-Story Trap|Gardner's victim-story trap]]

**This is the single most useful row on the page for your own work.**

| Story | Protagonist does what | Result |
| --- | --- | --: |
| **Chantal** | **Acts constantly** — lies about Rue Saint Michel, takes the name, kisses him, shows the photograph, hides the fob | **40** |
| **Beat** | Passive until the climax, then plays the true beat in front of the town | 25/25 at Gate 3 |
| **Baldwin** | Passive thirty pages, then one act — takes Sonny to the club and *listens* | **44** |
| **Found Things** | Passive throughout, acts once, nine weeks too late, and knows it | — |
| **Giorgis** | *"Installed rather than revealed"*; never shown at work | 33 |
| **Master draft** | **Pure observer. Nothing is chosen** | **24** |

**Your own two highest-scoring drafts are the two with a protagonist who acts.** The master draft's 24 and Gardner's warning about victim-protagonists are the same fact stated twice — once as a score, once as theory.

**And Baldwin's variation is the one to steal:** he gives the competence to the man being *watched*, not the man narrating. The narrator fails to understand for thirty pages and understands in one scene. **Ilunga currently holds the drum and the grief — two jobs Baldwin deliberately split.**

### [[Scene Design]] and [[Scene Analysis Method]] — does the scene turn?

- **Chantal** — every scene turns. The birthday turns on *what is Kim Kardashian*; the police station turns on the phone being handed over.
- **Master draft** — Scenes 1 and 2 do not turn at all. Axis 4 scored **2**.
- **Baldwin** — the algebra-class scene turns on the boy who laughs like Sonny.

## Suspense and sentences — Cleland

### [[Reveal Answers Slowly]] + [[05 The Late Reframe|the late reframe]]

| Story | Plant | Detonate | Gap |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| **Baldwin** | 20% | **64%** | **44 pts** |
| **Uwazuruike** | 52.4% | 94.5% | 42 pts |
| **Giorgis** | 46.4% | 83.4% | 37 pts |
| **Chantal** | — | 85.1% | — |
| **Beat** | ~4% | 69% | 65 pts *(but see below)* |

**Baldwin plants Grace in one clause and does not return for 44% of the story.** Beat's raw gap is the widest on the board and the weakest in effect, because the father is mentioned only once before the reveal — **the reader has nothing filed to be wrong about.** A plant is not a mention. It has to be misfiled first.

### [[Whisper Dont Shout]]

- **Chantal** — *It was not shame. It was arithmetic.* Whispered.
- **Giorgis** — *Powerful men were dangerous, their lust for dominion more potent than any love…* Shouted, and it is why prose control scored 3.

### [[Write Sentences That Work]] — and the metric that died

Uwazuruike averages **11.0** words a sentence and places. Baldwin runs several times that and wins everything. **There is no target.** See [[02 Comparison Board]] — the metric is retired.

## The dream and its faults — Gardner

### [[Three Faults of Soul — Sentimentality, Frigidity, Mannerism]]

**Every story on the board has one, and they are all different — which is how you learn to see your own.**

| Story | Fault | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Master draft** | Sentimentality + mannerism | *the bioluminescent stream of pure everything* · *désvu* · *squirmish* |
| **Uwazuruike** | Sentimentality | *the sky wept, nature's part in the cycle of sorrow* |
| **Giorgis** | Mannerism | *their affections waned with all the grace of poison* · stacked participial openers |
| **Found Things** | Mannerism *(risk)* | *if you had asked him / he would have said* — three times; a fourth would be one too many |
| **Chantal** | Mannerism | The antithesis cluster — six against a budget of one. Found by [[The De-Slop Pass — English Prose\|the de-slop test]] |
| **Beat** | — | Nothing found |

### [[Psychic Distance]]

- **Found Things** — free indirect: the distance moves *inside sentences*, so his self-justifications arrive in the story's own voice
- **Chantal** — fixed and close, declarative throughout. Its strength, and why it scores only **3/5 on the slide**
- **Baldwin** — retrospective first person that closes to nothing at the bandstand

### [[The Resonant Close]] + [[Climax and Conclusion — The Story in Six Words|Barrett]]

| Story | The close |
| --- | --- |
| **Giorgis** | The rifle at 99.7% — the title detonates, four plants paid at once |
| **Chantal** | Four ships. *It came out even, the way it does.* |
| **Beat** | *She did not ask me why I was warming a drum I was not going to play.* |
| **Found Things** | The replacement conductor on the dala dala |

## The form's edges — Barrett

### [[The Isolation Principle — One Emotion, One Incident]] — the rule the evidence breaks

| Story | Obeys? | Outcome |
| --- | :--: | --- |
| **Baldwin** | ❌ Decades, two cities, a whole family history | **Best on the board** |
| **Giorgis** | ❌ 5,179 words, three generations | Placed |
| **Uwazuruike** | ⚠️ One day, one subplot | Placed |
| **Chantal** | ✅ One year, one girl, one emotion | 40 |
| **Beat** | ✅ Two weeks, one trade | — |

**The most-violated rule on the board, and violating it costs nothing.** Barrett is writing in 1900 for magazine editors buying by the column. **Keep his first-page cut and his ending test. Overrule his unities** — this is the fourth thing to overrule him on, alongside dialect, the period sexism, and *"the object is to amuse."*

### [[Character by Salient Detail]]

- **Chantal** — *a collar with a good buckle* · the gutter that came off the year before last
- **Master draft** — *tall and beautiful just like her mother.* The vault's own sheet calls it thin, and interiority scored 3

### [[Inevitable but Unforeseen — Suspense and Surprise]]

**Found Things executes this at the level of premise, not plot** — *noticing and looking are not the same activity* is given away in paragraph one and remains unguessable. Uwazuruike does it at the level of plot: the largest gap measured, and the reader still does not see it.

## Getting it written — Kiteley

### [[Internal Structure — Shapes for an Ending]]

- **Found Things** — a circle. The dala dala missing at the open, present with a replacement conductor at the close
- **Beat** — a circle. The fire in the yard at the open, the same fire at the close, warming a drum he will not play
- **Chantal** — a count. Four ships at the open, four ships at the close, *it came out even*

### [[Point of View — The Reluctant I]]

**Chantal and Beat are both first persons who resist the confession they are making.** *I am not asking to be forgiven for that. I am asking for it to be counted correctly.* / *I am not asking anybody to make that all right. I am saying it is what the job is.*

**Two stories, written in different months, arriving at the same sentence shape.** That is either a signature or a tic — worth knowing which before both are ever seen by the same reader.
