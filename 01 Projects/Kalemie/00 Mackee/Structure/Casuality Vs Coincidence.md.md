# MCKEE — CAUSALITY VS COINCIDENCE

## The Distinction

> **CAUSALITY:** A story in which motivated actions cause effects that become causes of yet other effects, expressing the interconnectedness of reality.

> **COINCIDENCE:** A story in which unmotivated actions trigger events that do not cause further effects, expressing the disconnectedness of existence.

---

## Comparison

| | Causality | Coincidence |
|--|-----------|-------------|
| **Events** | Cause and effect chain | Random collisions |
| **Meaning** | Actions have consequences | Actions are meaningless |
| **Structure** | Archplot/Miniplot | Antiplot |
| **Examples** | Chinatown, The Godfather | After Hours, Weekend |

---

## Causality (Archplot/Miniplot)

**Characteristics:**
- Motivated actions
- Effects become causes
- Chain reaction
- Expresses the interconnectedness of life

**Examples:**
- **Chinatown:** Every event is caused by something before it
- **The Godfather:** Actions have consequences that ripple outward
- **Kramer vs. Kramer:** Each event leads to the next

---

## Coincidence (Antiplot)

**Characteristics:**
- Unmotivated actions
- Events don't cause further effects
- Fragmented story
- Expresses the disconnectedness of existence

**Examples:**
- **After Hours:** Random events that don't chain together
- **Weekend:** Coincidence rules
- **Strangers in Paradise:** Events happen without cause

---

## The Rule for Coincidence

> **Never use coincidence to turn an ending. This is deus ex machina, the writer's greatest sin.**

### Two Rules for Using Coincidence
1. **Bring coincidence in early** to allow time to build meaning out of it
2. **Never use coincidence to turn an ending**

**Example: Jaws**
- Coincidence: The shark eats a swimmer (random chance)
- But the shark stays and gathers meaning
- It becomes an icon of evil

## For Kalemie

**Primary Structure:** Causality

**Daudi's Actions Have Consequences:**
- He does nothing → Djamela leaves
- He says nothing → She enters the BMW
- He waits → The police fail her

**Where Coincidence Fits:**
- The motherboard appears randomly (coincidence)
- But it gathers meaning (Daudi's collection becomes a theology)
