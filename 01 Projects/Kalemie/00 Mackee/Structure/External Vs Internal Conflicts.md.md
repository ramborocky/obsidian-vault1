# MCKEE — EXTERNAL VS INTERNAL CONFLICT

## The Distinction

> **EXTERNAL CONFLICT:** Struggles with personal relationships, social institutions, or forces in the physical world.

> **INTERNAL CONFLICT:** Struggles within the character's own thoughts and feelings, conscious or unconscious.
