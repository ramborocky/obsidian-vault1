# MCKEE — THE STORY TRIANGLE

## The Three Corners

### ARCHPLOT (Classical Design)
- Active protagonist
- External conflict
- Closed ending
- Causality
- Consistent reality
- Linear time
- Single protagonist

### MINIPLOT (Minimalism)
- Passive protagonist
- Internal conflict
- Open ending
- Inner causality
- Consistent reality
- Linear time
- Multiple protagonists

### ANTIPLOT (Anti-structure)
- Passive protagonist
- Internal conflict
- Open ending
- Coincidence
- Inconsistent reality
- Nonlinear time
- Multiple protagonists

---

## The Seven Formal Differences

| | Archplot | Miniplot | Antiplot |
|--|----------|----------|----------|
| **Ending** | Closed | Open | Open |
| **Conflict** | External | Internal | Internal |
| **Protagonist** | Single, Active | Multiple, Passive | Passive |
| **Time** | Linear | Linear | Nonlinear |
| **Causality** | Causality | Causality | Coincidence |
| **Reality** | Consistent | Consistent | Inconsistent |
| **Change** | Absolute | Moderate | Ironic |

---

## The "Politics" of Story Design

- **Hollywood Film** → Archplot (optimistic about change)
- **Art Film** → Miniplot/Antiplot (pessimistic about change)

**McKee's Warning:** The audience shrinks as you move away from Archplot. The larger the budget, the more you need Archplot.

---

## Which Structure for Your Story?

### Ask Yourself:
1. What is my protagonist's desire? External or internal?
2. What is the nature of the conflict? Inner? Personal? Extra-personal?
3. What ending does my story demand? Closed? Open? Ironic?
4. What is my budget? Small? Large?
