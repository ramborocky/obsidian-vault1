# MCKEE — CONSISTENT VS INCONSISTENT REALITY

## The Distinction

> **CONSISTENT REALITY:** Fictional settings that establish modes of interaction between characters and their world that are kept consistently throughout the telling to create meaning.

> **INCONSISTENT REALITY:** Settings that mix modes of interaction so that the story's episodes jump inconsistently from one "reality" to another to create a sense of absurdity.

---

## Comparison

| | Consistent Reality | Inconsistent Reality |
|--|--------------------|----------------------|
| **Rules** | Established and kept | Rules are broken |
| **Characters** | Behave predictably | Behave unpredictably |
| **World** | Makes sense | Makes no sense |
| **Structure** | Archplot/Miniplot | Antiplot |
| **Examples** | The Godfather, Casablanca | Weekend, Last Year at Marienbad |

---

## Consistent Reality (Archplot/Miniplot)

**Characteristics:**
- Rules are established
- Rules are kept
- Even bizarre rules must be consistent

**Example: Who Framed Roger Rabbit**
- Rule: Cartoon characters can flatten and slide under doors
- Kept consistently throughout the film
- When Roger is caught later, a non-human agent is needed

---

## Inconsistent Reality (Antiplot)

**Characteristics:**
- Rules are made to be broken
- Reality shifts without explanation
- Creates a sense of absurdity

**Example: Weekend (Godard)**
- Parisian couple travels to murder an aunt
- Emily Bronte suddenly appears (from the 19th century)
- They burn her to death
- No explanation. It just happens.

---

## For Kalemie

**Recommended:** Consistent Reality

**Why:**
- Kalemie is a real place
- The characters behave in believable ways
- The system (police, class) is recognizable

**The Rules of Kalemie:**
1. The system is corrupt
2. Poverty is inescapable
3. Those with money have power
4. Daudi watches, but doesn't act

---

## Reality vs. Truth

> Consistency doesn't mean "realistic." It means "true to itself."

**Fantasy is More Rigid than Realism**
- Fantasy allows one great leap (magic, aliens)
- Then demands tight-knit probabilities
- No coincidence in fantasy

**Realism can be Less Rigid**
- Gritty realism often allows leaps in logic
- The Usual Suspects is full of improbabilities
