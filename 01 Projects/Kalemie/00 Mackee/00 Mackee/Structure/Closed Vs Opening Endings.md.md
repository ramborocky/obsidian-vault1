# MCKEE — CLOSED VS OPEN ENDINGS

## The Distinction

> **CLOSED ENDING:** A Story Climax of absolute, irreversible change that answers all questions raised by the telling and satisfies all audience emotion.

> **OPEN ENDING:** A Story Climax that leaves a question or two unanswered and some emotion unfulfilled.

---

## Comparison Table

| | Closed Ending | Open Ending |
|--|---------------|-------------|
| **Questions** | All answered | Some unanswered |
| **Emotion** | Fully satisfied | Some residue left |
| **Closure** | Complete | Partial |
| **Audience** | Leaves satisfied | Leaves thinking |
| **Structure** | Archplot | Miniplot/Antiplot |

---

## Examples

### Closed Endings (Archplot)
- **Chinatown:** Evelyn is killed, Cross escapes with Katherine. Justice fails. Questions answered.
- **The Godfather:** Michael becomes the new Don. His transformation is complete.
- **Kramer vs. Kramer:** The custody battle ends. The family is permanently changed.

### Open Endings (Miniplot)
- **Paris, Texas:** Father and son are reconciled, but the future of the family is unresolved.
- **The Accidental Tourist:** The protagonist changes, but the love story remains open.
- **Leaving Las Vegas:** The protagonist's death is certain, but the meaning of his relationship remains ambiguous.

## Which Ending for Your Story?

### Ask Yourself:
1. What does the story demand?
2. What would feel true to the characters?
3. What would satisfy the reader?

### For Kalemie
- Daudi watches Djamela leave
- The police fail her
- He does nothing
- **Recommended:** Open Ending
- **Why:** The tragedy is that the system remains unchanged. Daudi changes, but the world doesn't.

---

## Techniques for Open Endings

1. **Leave one key question unanswered**
   - "What happened to Djamela after she left?"
   - "Will Daudi ever act?"

2. **Leave some emotion unfulfilled**
   - The reader carries the sadness out of the story
   - The emotion lingers

3. **End on an image, not an explanation**
   - Don't tell the reader what to feel
   - Show them the final image and let them feel it
