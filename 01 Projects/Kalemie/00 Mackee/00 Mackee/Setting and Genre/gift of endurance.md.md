# MCKEE — THE GIFT OF ENDURANCE

## The Principle

> Mastery of genre is essential for yet one more reason: Screenwriting is not for sprinters, but for long-distance runners.

---

## The Long Journey

> From first inspiration to last polished draft, a quality screenplay consumes six months, nine months, a year, or more.

**The Question:**
> What will keep your desire burning over those many months?

---

## Finding Your Subject

> Generally, great writers are not eclectic. Each tightly focuses his oeuvre on one idea, a single subject that ignites his passion, a subject he pursues with beautiful variation through a lifetime of work.

### Examples

| Writer | Subject |
|--------|---------|
| Hemingway | How to face death |
| Dickens | The lonely child searching for the lost father |
| Molière | The idiocy and depravity of society |

---

## For Kalemie

### What Is Your Subject?

**Possible Subjects:**
- The cost of inaction
- The meaning we create from debris
- The failure of systems to protect the vulnerable
- The power of witnessing

### What Is Your Genre?

> Write in the genre you love. For although the passion for an idea or experience may wither, the love of the movies is forever.

**For You:**
- Literary Fiction
- African Literature
- Social Drama

**Why This Genre:**
- It's what you love to read
- It's what you love to write
- It's what will sustain you through the long journey

---

## Genre as Reinspiration

> Genre should be a constant source of reinspiration. Every time you reread your script, it should excite you, for this is your kind of story, the kind of film you'd stand in line in the rain to see.

### For Kalemie
- Does this story excite you?
- Would you read this story if someone else wrote it?
- Does the genre sustain you?

---

## The Warning

> Do not write something because intellectual friends think it's socially important. Do not write something you think will inspire critical praise.

**Write what you love.**
**Write what sustains you.**
**Write in the genre you'd stand in line to see.**
