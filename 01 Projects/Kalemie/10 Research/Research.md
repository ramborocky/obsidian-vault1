**The 28s are the finding worth keeping even if every sentence in those files is binned.** The Arabic alphabet has 28 letters; the moon crosses 28 *manazil*; in ʿilm al-ḥurūf they are the same 28, and the last mansion is *Baṭn al-Ḥūt*, the belly of the fish. A nominal cycle is 28 days. Djamela counts to thirty-four. See [[09 The Practice — Calendar, Ritual and Ilmu Huruf]], which had this half-written already.
