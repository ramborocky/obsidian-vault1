## Congolese Slang (Lingala)

> **Regional note**: most of this list came in tagged as *Kinshasa* street/youth slang. Kalemie is on the eastern shore of Lake Tanganyika — a different register from Kinshasa's western urban slang, and per [[research for Kalemie]], Kalemie's actual language mix is Swahili (everyday), French (power/officialdom, Jean Luc's register), Lingala (street/music). Entries below marked **(Kin)** are plausibly too Kinshasa-specific for this story as written — keep them logged for a future story in the cycle actually set in Kinshasa (see [[Collection Tracker]]) rather than dropping them, but verify before putting them in a Kalemie character's mouth.
