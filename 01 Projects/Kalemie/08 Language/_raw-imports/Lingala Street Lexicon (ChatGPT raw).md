## MONEY

### Mbongo
Meaning:
Money.

Usage:
Nazangi mbongo.
(I have no money.)

Swahili:
Pesa

English:
Money

---

### Lokotro

Meaning:
Cash.

Swahili:
Cashi

English:
Cash

---

### Dimi

Meaning:
Money.

Origin:
Popularized in Kinshasa youth slang.

---

### Soso

Meaning:
Small amount of money.

Example:
Pesa ngai soso.

---

### Mvula

Meaning:
Big money.

Notes:
Used mostly among youth.
