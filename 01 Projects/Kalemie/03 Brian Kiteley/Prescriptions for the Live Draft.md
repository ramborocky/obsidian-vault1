# PRESCRIPTIONS FOR THE LIVE DRAFT

> The working document of this base. Every open fault in Kalemie, mapped to a numbered Kiteley exercise with its word count. Nothing here is theory.

## How To Use This

Pick **one row**. Do the exercise at the stated length. Put the result into the draft or throw it away. Come back for another row tomorrow.

Do not browse the book. Do not do exercises that are not on this table. See the caution in [[The 3 AM Epiphany by Brian Kiteley|the index]] — for a project with this much apparatus already, Kiteley is a scalpel and not a buffet.

## The Seven-Day Version

If [[Dashboard|the Dashboard's]] scene-a-day schedule is not moving — and it has not moved since Day 1 on 23 July — replace it with this for one week. Every item is a fault already named in [[Kalemie — Standing Diagnosis]], and the total new prose is under 3,000 words, most of which is throwaway.

| Day | Exercise | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **137. Beginnings** — six openings, no lake line | Kills the double start |
| 2 | **108. Censorship** — what the story will not look at | Unblocks Scene 6 |
| 3 | **190. Outrunning the Critic** — 100 sentences on Djamela | Fills the interiority gap |
| 4 | **193. Surprise** — Daudi does one unexpected thing | Breaks the passivity |
| 5 | **188. A Circle** — the ending as a shape | Writes the missing ending |
| 6 | **200. Q and A** — twenty questions, then delete them | Converts callouts into prose |
| 7 | **132. Economy** — cut the whole draft by 20% | Buys the word budget back |

**Kiteley's rule on day 7 is worth quoting exactly:** *any time you can cut a piece of prose by 20 percent, you should cut it by 20 percent.* At 3,941 words that is 788 words recovered — nearly twice what the ending needs. Cross-read [[The Balance Test — Length as a Function of Idea]].

## Questions for AI

1. Which single row in Tier 1 unblocks the most other rows? Do that one first and say why.
2. After each exercise, tell me honestly whether the result belongs in the draft or in [[Craft Log|the trunk]]. Do not be generous.
3. Apply Kiteley's success test to whatever I produce: days later, does it feel like a whole story kicking at the walls of the fragment?
4. Am I using this table to avoid writing the ending? Answer plainly if so.
5. When a row is done, strike it from this file and note the date. This table should get shorter.
