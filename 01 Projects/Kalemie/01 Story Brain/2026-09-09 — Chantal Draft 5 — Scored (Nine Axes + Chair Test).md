### ⚠️ The two-point disagreement, reported as the rubric requires

Note 17 scores **The World at 2** and calls it the axis Chantal fails worst. I score it **4**, and I checked whether the text had changed before saying so. **It has not** — Draft 4 already contains the escort woman, the replaced bench slats, Bahati, the antelope skull, the gutter, the four stomachs, the watermelon pyramid and the cracked chair. Only *Christian Bella* and the pebble are new in Draft 5.

**So this is a scoring disagreement on identical material, not a text difference.** My case: band 2 is *"one or two decorative details"*, and the story plainly carries ten or more items of true surplus, including **a person with a life the story does not follow** — the woman in the black dress at the station insisting it was escort services, not prostitution, who gets one paragraph and is never seen again. That is band 4's explicit requirement, met.

It does not reach 5: no surplus item is *the most memorable thing in the story* while serving nothing.

**If note 17 is right and I am wrong, the Chair total is 18, not 20.** Worth your own eye — you built the instrument.
