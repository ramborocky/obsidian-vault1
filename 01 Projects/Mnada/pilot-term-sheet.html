Livestock inventory finance secured on a Livestock Deposit Receipt

Non-binding · Subject to credit approval, due diligence and documentation

This term sheet creates no obligation on any party. It is an indicative summary for discussion only, subject to the Lender's credit approval, satisfactory due diligence and executed documentation, and must be reviewed by Tanzanian counsel before it is relied on. Figures are illustrative and based on the MNADA index as at the date shown. Nothing in this document is a credit offer, a commitment to lend, or investment advice.

01Why this transaction exists

Everything else in the MNADA memorandum is a claim. This is the test.

If a Tanzanian bank advances real money against 200 head of cattle it has never seen, secured only on an electronic document of title, and is repaid on day 45 out of a settlement the exchange controlled end to end — then livestock is a bankable asset class in Tanzania and MNADA owns the rails. If it fails, no amount of platform, map or projection matters.

One lender. One lot. Forty-five days. Deliberately small enough that failure is survivable, and large enough that success is evidence.

05Security

  1. First-ranking pledge over the Livestock Deposit Receipt covering the lot. The LDR is an electronic document of title held in the MNADA registry and flagged to the Lender's order. MNADA is contractually unable to transfer, split or release it without the Lender's release instruction.
  2. Tripartite Collateral Management Agreement between Lender, Borrower and MNADA, under which MNADA acknowledges it holds the LDR to the Lender's order and owes the Lender direct reporting duties.
  3. Custody Agreement with the Custodian, acknowledging the Lender's interest, granting inspection rights, and waiving any lien ranking ahead of the Lender other than accrued feed and care charges as scheduled in §06.
  4. Assignment of the mortality insurance policy, Lender noted as loss payee.
  5. Irrevocable settlement instruction directing 100% of gross sale proceeds into the escrow account held at the Lender, applied per the waterfall in §06. The Borrower cannot intercept proceeds.
  6. Personal guarantee of the Borrower's principal, limited to TZS [—].

Repayment does not depend on the Borrower choosing to repay. It happens inside the settlement rail, before the Borrower sees any money.

Custody ranks ahead of the Lender because unpaid custodians do not release animals, and a lien fight destroys more value than the ranking costs. MNADA ranks behind the Lender in all cases — the platform is not paid until the bank is whole.

07Conditions precedent

No drawdown until every one of these is satisfied.

  • CP1 — Registry MoU between MNADA and the Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries executed and in force.
  • CP2 — Custodian holding-ground licence current; site inspected by the Lender and by MNADA.
  • CP3 — All 200 head tagged, graded and photographed; tag register reconciled 100% against physical count by MNADA in the Lender's presence.
  • CP4 — Mortality insurance in force, Lender noted as loss payee, premium paid.
  • CP5 — Collateral Management Agreement, Custody Agreement and settlement instruction executed.
  • CP6 — Escrow account opened at the Lender in MNADA's name, designated as a trust account.
  • CP7 — Veterinary certification: lot free of notifiable disease; no movement restriction over the district.

09Events of default

  • Reconciliation shortfall exceeding 1% of head count, unexplained for 48 hours.
  • Mortality exceeding the insured limit, or insurer repudiation.
  • Lapse, suspension or revocation of the Custodian's licence.
  • Movement of any animal off the holding ground without Lender consent.
  • Failure to cure a margin call within 5 business days.
  • Non-payment of any amount at maturity.
  • Any attempt to transfer, pledge or encumber the LDR outside the registry.

10Why the Lender is protected

The number that matters is not the advance rate. It is the price at which the Lender stops being whole. Working back through the waterfall:

G × (1 − 0.015) − 19,800,000 ≥ 120,327,671 G ≥ TZS 142,261,595 → TZS 711,000 per head
Per headHeadroom
Lender break-even realisationTZS 711,000—
Entry index at drawdownTZS 742,000+4.4%
Expected exit after 45 days finishingTZS 900,000+26.6%

The lot can realise 21% below its expected exit price, or 4.4% below the price it was valued at on day one, and the Lender is still repaid in full — before touching the guarantee, the insurance or the margin-call mechanism. Mortality is insured, not borne. Price risk is capped by the coverage triggers. The residual exposure is operational: does the reconciliation hold, and does the settlement rail execute. That is what the pilot is testing.

11Success criteria

The pilot passes only if all five are met. Anything less is a data point, not a proof.

Criterion 5 is the one that changes the Lender's business. The other four prove one loan was repaid. Criterion 5 proves the bank can value any lot in custody without inspecting it — which is the difference between a pilot and an asset class.

12If it passes — and if it fails

On success, subject to fresh credit approval, the parties to discuss

  • A revolving livestock inventory facility of TZS [—] across MNADA-licensed holding grounds.
  • Settlement-bank exclusivity for the Lender for 60 months.
  • Right of first refusal on all LDR-secured lending originated on the platform.
  • Joint publication of the result to the Ministry and the Bank of Tanzania, as the evidence base for a livestock collateral framework.

On failure

MNADA meets the Lender's out-of-pocket costs up to TZS [—], and publishes the failure analysis to the Lender in full, including reconciliation and settlement logs. A quiet failure teaches nobody anything and would poison the second attempt.

Before this document is sent to any bank it requires review by Tanzanian counsel, particularly on whether an electronic Livestock Deposit Receipt is enforceable as a document of title under current Tanzanian law without amendment to the Warehouse Receipts framework.
